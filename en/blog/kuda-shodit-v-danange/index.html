Where to Go in Da Nang: Events and what’s on

September 5, 2026

Da Nang is Vietnam's third-largest city and one of the fastest-growing: wide beaches, a new waterfront, and many expats and digital nomads who choose it for its warm winters and low prices. Along with the people, the event scene is growing too: Da Nang hosts regular shows, yoga studios, film clubs, and conversation meetups, and in the evenings cafes host board games and Mafia.

The main feature of the local event scene is that it's almost invisible from the street: events are announced in Telegram channels and chats, making them hard for newcomers to find. MyPins gathers Da Nang events on one map—with date, time, address, price, and organizer—so you don't have to plan your evening from scratch.

What Events Are Held in Da Nang

According to our map, the most frequently posted formats in Da Nang are:

  • Regular shows—cultural performances in central theaters: Vietnamese costumes, music, and national flavor. They run almost every day.
  • Yoga, qigong, and fitness—morning practices in studios and on the beach, classes for adults and teens.
  • Board games and Mafia—regular meetups in cafes and restaurants, from classic Mafia to One Night and board games for beginners.
  • Quizzes and movie parties—team trivia and group film screenings with discussions.
  • Master classes—from tote bag painting and candle making to floristry and solid perfume.
  • Networking and meetups—business breakfasts, book clubs, women's circles, and psychological sessions.

The current what is on in the coming days is always visible on the map: events in Da Nang with filters by category, price, and date.

Where to Find Events in Da Nang

If you're new to the city, here are three working ways not to miss something interesting:

  • MyPins map: Da Nang events are grouped by date and category, each with an address and price. It's convenient to see what's happening near your area.
  • Organizers' Telegram channels: most events in Da Nang are announced there, including urgent reschedules and cancellations. Before heading out, it's worth checking the channel—'still spots today' is also posted there.
  • Word of mouth in cafes and coworking spaces: some meetups live only in the chats of the My An and An Thuong districts, where expats settle.

How Much Do Events Cost

Prices in Da Nang are noticeably lower than in Bali: most paid events cost 100–300 thousand dong (roughly $4–12). Many meetups are free or work on a donation basis—the map has a 'Free' filter for this. If the price isn't listed, it's confirmed with the organizer when signing up: some events require registration so the organizer knows the number of guests.

Where Events Take Place

Da Nang's event life is concentrated in two areas. First, the My An district and An Thuong streets near My Khe beach—this is the heart of expat Da Nang: here you'll find most yoga studios, cafes with board games and quizzes, and networking breakfasts. Second, the city center, Hai Chau—there are theaters hosting regular shows and large concert venues. The exact address of each event is listed on its card on the map.

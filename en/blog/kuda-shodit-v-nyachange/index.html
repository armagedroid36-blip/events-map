What to Do in Nha Trang: Events and what’s on

September 5, 2026

Nha Trang is a beach resort in Vietnam where Russian-speaking travelers and expats live and stay for long periods. The city is small, but its event life is rich: almost every evening something happens — speaking clubs, film screenings, quizzes, yoga, concerts, and parties. The problem is different: the listings are scattered across dozens of Telegram channels, stories, and cafe announcements, and it's easy to miss an interesting event even if it's happening in the next building.

MyPins is an event map that gathers Nha Trang's event listings in one place. The map shows what's happening in the city today and in the coming weeks: date, time, venue, price, and organizer. No registration needed — just open and look.

What events take place in Nha Trang

According to our map, the most frequently searched and posted formats in Nha Trang are:

  • Speaking clubs — there are several in the city, the most famous is SmallTalk: one-on-one communication, a new conversation partner every 15 minutes, and after the meeting participants have dinner together. A great way to practice English and meet people.
  • Yoga and health — morning and evening practices by the sea, breathing sessions, qigong, stretching.
  • Games and quizzes — team trivia in cafes and bars, board games.
  • Film club — group screenings with discussions.
  • Workshops — from cooking to creative activities.
  • Concerts and parties — live performances, DJ sets on weekends.
  • Food and drinks — gastro meetups and tastings.

Current dates and venues change every week, so it's better not to rely on old posts but to open the live listings: events in Nha Trang on the map.

Where to look for events in Nha Trang

There are three working ways, and we recommend combining the first two:

  • MyPins map: filters by category ("Speaking Club," "Yoga and Health," "Games and Quizzes," and others), by price — you can filter only free or "donation" events, by date. Each event is shown on the city map with address and price.
  • Telegram channels and organizer chats: they publish urgent changes — rescheduling, cancellation, "still spots available today." If you're going to an event, check the organizer's channel before heading out.
  • Announcements in cafes and coworking spaces: some small events exist only on paper flyers, but they also appear on the map.

Events in Nha Trang are easy to search on the map: they are grouped by date, and you can see what's happening today and what's next week.

How much do events cost

Many events in Nha Trang are free or work on a donation basis. On the map, there's a "Free" filter for this. Paid events show a price — for example, speaking clubs usually charge a small entry fee. If no price is listed, the organizer asks you to confirm when booking: almost all events require registration via direct messages — this helps the organizer plan the meeting.

Where events take place

Most events are in cafes and spaces in the city center and on the first line near the beach. The exact address and coordinates are listed in each event's card on the map — you can immediately see if it's far from you.

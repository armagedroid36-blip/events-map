Events in Bali: where tourists and expats should go

September 5, 2026

Bali has long ceased to be just a beach resort. The island is one of the main hubs for digital nomads and expats in Southeast Asia, and the event scene here runs every day: sunrise yoga, weekend markets, concerts, parties, retreats, and networking meetups.

The challenge is that Bali is not a city but an island: events are scattered across areas 10–40 minutes apart by car, and you can't just 'check the listings'. MyPins shows events directly on a map — you can see what's happening near you and what's worth traveling for.

What events are most searched for in Bali

  • Yoga and wellness: practices, breathwork sessions, meditations, women's circles.
  • Markets and food events: designer stalls, gastro festivals, street food.
  • Parties and concerts: from DJ sets in Canggu to live performances.
  • Masterclasses and workshops: crafts, creativity, cooking.
  • Meetups and networking: expat meetups, interest clubs.
  • Festivals: from music to culture — Bali hosts many of them.

What happens where: island areas

Each area of Bali has its own character, and this is reflected in the events:

  • Canggu — the party and most 'nomadic' area: the most parties, concerts, and networking, many events for expats.
  • Ubud — the cultural heart of the island: yoga, retreats, meditations, handmade markets, and festivals.
  • Seminyak and Kuta — beach classics: bars, concerts, oceanfront parties.
  • Less touristy areas host events less often — but they are also visible on the map.

If you've just arrived and don't know where to stay based on the event scene, the map will help: see which area has what interests you.

When to go: event seasonality

In the dry season (roughly April–October), Bali has more outdoor events: beach markets, poolside parties, festivals. In the rainy season, life doesn't stop — it just moves indoors: film clubs, workshops, lectures, and yoga studios run year-round. Always check exact dates for specific events on the map — the listings are constantly updated, and past events are removed.

About MyPins

What is MyPins

MyPins is an events map for travellers and expats in Southeast Asia: yoga and retreats, concerts and parties, markets, festivals, speaking clubs and meetups. The map now covers three destinations: events in Bali, in Da Nang and in Nha Trang. Organizers publish the events themselves, and every pin shows the date, venue and price — so you can see at a glance what is happening nearby and how much it costs.

How events get on the map

Organizers add events through the form on the site. Before publication every event goes through moderation — the editorial team checks the date, venue and description so the listings stay free of spam and duplicates. Here is how the map is built:

  • an organizer adds an event with a date, a place on the map and a price;
  • the event is moderated before it is published;
  • the listings update daily and past events are removed from the map.

Want to list your own event? Step-by-step instructions are on the page for organizers.

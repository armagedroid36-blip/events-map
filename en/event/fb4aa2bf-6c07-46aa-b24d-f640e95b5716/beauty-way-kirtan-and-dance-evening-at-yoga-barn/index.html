On October 10 at Studio Nyoman in The Yoga Barn complex in Ubud, Beauty Way will take place — an evening of kirtan, cacao ceremony, dance, and instrumental meditation. The lineup features Akari Tamura, Acharya, and Ryan Narayan performing as NRYN. Gathering time: 18:30–22:00 WITA. Doors will open at 18:30, and the evening will begin with a DJ set by NRYN. At 19:00, the cacao ceremony and kirtan by Akari & friends will start, lasting until 20:00. Then Acharya will play until 21:30. The final half hour will be a calm sound session with the Beauty Way team. Early Bird tickets are available at 300,000 IDR and Presale at 400,000 IDR. Full price is 490,000 IDR; as of September 27, sales for this tier have not yet opened. Buy tickets (https://megatix.co.id/events/beauty-way-oct). Children under 10 are not admitted. Participants aged 10–15 require an accompanying adult: a parent, guardian, or sibling over 18. Tickets are personalized and non-transferable. Arrive 15 minutes before the start.

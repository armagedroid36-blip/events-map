On October 31 at 22:00, Dracula on Ice: Halloween Night will be performed at Block42 in the Nuanu Creative City area. The ice show combines professional figure skating, circus acts, the gothic story of Dracula, and theatrical projections. This evening version is recommended for audiences aged 16 and up. A ticket to the show costs 666,000 IDR; VIP with a front-row seat and lounge access is 1,666,000 IDR. Both tiers include entry to Nuanu. From 23:30, The Afterlife Party with DJs and special effects will take place at Block42; it can be added to a show ticket for 266,000 IDR. Ice access and skate rental may require a separate fee and are subject to availability. Tickets: https://megatix.co.id/events/shows-on-ice-halloween-edition-2026

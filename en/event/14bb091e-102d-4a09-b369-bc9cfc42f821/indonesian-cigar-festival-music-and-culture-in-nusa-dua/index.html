Indonesian Cigar Festival: Music and Culture in Nusa Dua

Event has ended — October 1, 2026

Jl. Kw. Nusa Dua Resort, Benoa, Kec. Kuta Sel., Kabupaten Badung, Bali 80361, Indonesia

The Indonesian Cigar Festival will take place from October 1 to 3 at the atrium of Bali Collection in Nusa Dua. The festival starts on October 1 at 16:00 WITA. The music lineup includes Gugun Blues Shelter, Dewa Budjana, Ecko Show, Ganzerlana, and Vimast. The festival brings together concert performances, culture, and cigar themes. The venue is the Atrium Bali Collection, inside the Bali Collection shopping complex.

The second Art & Bali exhibition returns to Bali from September 11 to 13. It is an international art fair that brings together commercial art, curated projects, and open dialogue about contemporary art. 🎨 This year, guests can look forward to a marketplace featuring works from 20+ contemporary galleries, as well as the main exhibition "What the Body Remembers," curated by Bandana Tewari and Brina Pasca. The project features 26 haute couture masters and works with traditional materials from Indonesia and India. The exhibition explores the connection between the body, memory, craft, and cultural heritage. 📅 September 11–13, 2026📍 Nuanu Creative City🎟️ Tickets are already on sale at artandbali.com instagram (https://www.instagram.com/nuanuevents/)

💃 BACHATA: Intensive for beginners If you've been wanting to start dancing for a while, or feel like your basics lack confidence, come join us) We break down the fundamentals of bachata: gently, clearly, and sometimes spontaneously. What to expect at the workshop: Basics from scratch: How to stand, how to step, and not get tangled up in your feet. Connection: Leading and following technique (so the dance feels great for both partners). Confidence: Letting go of tension and learning to hear the music.

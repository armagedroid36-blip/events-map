DREAMSTATE Presents MANOLACO Live in Cyprus! Athens’ powerhouse DJ & producer MANOLACO is taking over the decks for an unforgettable night! With track releases on top global labels (MOAN, Mindshake, Nervous) backed by Keinemusik and Jamie Jones, expect nothing less than infectious Afro & Tribal grooves, pulsating basslines, and high-octane energy all night long!

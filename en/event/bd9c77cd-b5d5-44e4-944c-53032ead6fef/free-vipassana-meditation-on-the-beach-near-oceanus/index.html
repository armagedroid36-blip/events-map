Free Vipassana meditation on the beach near Oceanus When: 19.09 at 08:00 Where: beach near Oceanus, Nha Trang In the morning we meet on the beach, do a short warm-up and move on to practice, to calmly tune in for the day, work with attention, slow down a bit and be in contact with ourselves and others.

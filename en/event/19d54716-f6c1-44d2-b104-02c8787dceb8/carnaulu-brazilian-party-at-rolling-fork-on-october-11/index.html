On October 11, CARNAULU will take place at Rolling Fork in Pechatu — a party with Brazilian rhythms. Tickets indicate a start time of 20:00; the dance program from 21:00 to midnight includes house sets by Calore DJs with live trumpet and flute, Dimas' percussion, carnival dancers, and face painting. Dinner with background music can be attended separately from 19:00. Entry to the party is 21+. A limited free guest list is currently available. A regular ticket costs 257,500 IDR and includes one cocktail. To secure a spot on the guest list or buy a ticket, open https://megatix.co.id/events/carna-ulu

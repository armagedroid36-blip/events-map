The traditional wine village of Arsos celebrates Cyprus’ long vine-growing and winemaking heritage with the “Kazaniasmata” Festival. Held in the village square, the festival takes place during the autumn grape harvest season, when Cyprus’ wine-producing villages celebrate their centuries-old connection with vineyards and winemaking. It is an opportunity to visit Arsos and experience the atmosphere of one of the traditional wine villages of the Limassol District. Date: 25 October 2026 Time: From 18:00 Venue: Arsos Village Square, Arsos, Limassol District

On October 15, 22, and 29, from 14:00 to 16:00, Chroma natural dyeing workshops take place at Good Times General Store in the Desa Potato Head area in Seminyak. Participants create dyes from plants and food scraps, including marigolds and turmeric, and dye a bandana, as well as a T-shirt or bag. Tickets cost 1,000,000 IDR per session. Fabric blanks and dyeing materials are included. To participate, select your preferred date and purchase a ticket: https://megatix.co.id/events/chroma-natural-dye-workshop

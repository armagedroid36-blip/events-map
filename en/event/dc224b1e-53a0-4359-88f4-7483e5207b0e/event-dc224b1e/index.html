The Snow Queen

November 8, 2026, 11:00 AM

Satiriko Theatre, Nicosia, Cyprus

The Children's Stage of the Satirical Theatre presents "The Snow Queen," inspired by Hans Christian Andersen's classic fairy tale, adapted and directed by Eleni Anastasiou. A musical performance for the whole family! Aris and Aphrodite are two inseparable friends who have made a promise: if one of them is ever lost, the other will search to find him. But one day Aris begins to change. He withdraws, gets angry, and everything he loved until yesterday suddenly seems foolish to him. And as he closes himself off more and more, the world around him begins to grow cold. And then...

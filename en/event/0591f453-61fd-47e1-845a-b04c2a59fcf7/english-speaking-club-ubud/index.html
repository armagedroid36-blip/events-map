English Speaking Club, Ubud Not courses. Not a lesson. Not a lecture. This is a place where English becomes a natural way of communication. We don't have the usual "teacher — students" format. Instead of a teacher, there's a facilitator who guides the conversation, suggests activities, and helps everyone join in the discussion. Here, all participants are on equal footing. Stronger speakers help those less experienced, and beginners quickly get drawn into lively conversation without fear of making mistakes. That's why many people start speaking much more confidently after just the first few sessions. Every meeting is different. 🎲 Modern board games 🃏 Conversation cards and interactive tasks 🎬 Movies and series with discussions of real-life English 🎵 Songs, modern colloquial vocabulary, and expressions ⚔️ Debates and discussions on interesting topics 📖 Stories, improvisation, describing objects, and dozens of other activities Every game and every task is designed with one goal — to get you speaking as much as possible. 🌍 A truly international environment Foreigners, including native English speakers, regularly attend the meetings, so you practice English not only with Russian-speaking participants but in a natural international company — just as it happens in everyday life in Bali. Why this format works ✅ Maximum speaking practice ✅ No rote memorization or boring exercises ✅ Small groups — everyone actively participates ✅ English is used from the very first minute of the meeting ✅ The chance to communicate with people of different levels and get used to natural speech faster ✅ New connections, a friendly atmosphere, and a community of people interested in growing together It doesn't matter whether you only know a few phrases or already communicate confidently — the club's format is built so that everyone feels comfortable and gradually reaches a new level. 📍 Ubud 👥 Small groups 🌍 International community 🎲 New activities every week Less theory.

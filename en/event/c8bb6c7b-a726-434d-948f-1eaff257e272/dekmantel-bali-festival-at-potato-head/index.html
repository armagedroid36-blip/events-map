Dekmantel Bali Festival at Potato Head

September 26, 2026, 2:00 PM

Jl. Petitenget No.51B, Kerobokan Kelod, Kec. Kuta Utara, Kabupaten Badung, Bali 80361, Индонезия, Семиньяк, Bali

On September 26, the Bali edition of Dekmantel will take place at the Beach Club of the Desa Potato Head complex. The Amsterdam festival brand will bring an electronic music program featuring live performances and DJ sets by artists from the USA, Japan, Europe, and Indonesia. The program also includes a deep listening session, live Q&A, and Desa Dialogue. Starts at 14:00, ends at 04:00.

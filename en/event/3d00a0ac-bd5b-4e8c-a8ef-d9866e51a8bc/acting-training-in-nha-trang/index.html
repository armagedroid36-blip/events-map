🎭 Acting Workshop in Nha Trang Friends, I'm launching a new acting workshop in Nha Trang — a space where you can not only get acquainted with the art of acting, but also open yourself up to this world, feel lightness, and find new keys to inspiration. Acting is not just about the stage. It's an opportunity to let go of control, step out of your usual role, and allow yourself to be real. At the workshop we will work with: ✨ Psychophysics — body, attention, energy, and awareness. ✨ Emotional freedom — learning to feel and freely express your emotions. ✨ Unwinding — releasing inner tensions and becoming freer in expressing yourself. ✨ Improvisation — developing imagination, spontaneity, and creativity. ✨ Fear of the audience — learning to calmly be in the spotlight and interact with others. 🌿 Here you can laugh, make mistakes, try new things, be different — and gradually find your own freedom. It's also a chance to meet new people, find friends, and be in a creative atmosphere where everyone can open up in their own way. No previous acting experience needed. You can come just out of curiosity — and see what opens up inside you. 🎭 ⏰ Duration: 2 hours 📍 Nha Trang — I'll share the exact location after registration 💰 Participation format: donation 📅 Date: 11.10 📩 To sign up, message me privately: @W1kot Looking forward to meeting you at the venue ❤️ #date: 11.10

🎨 Saturday drawing sessions — a lovely tradition. We gather as "girls" in a cozy café to relax, draw, and recharge. This Saturday we'll work with the "Wheel of Emotions." We'll draw the spectrum of our feelings over the past week — from joy and inspiration to tiredness, irritation, or sadness. Through color and form, we'll see what was really happening inside during these days, which emotions were vivid, and which we didn't notice or pushed aside. This is a gentle practice that helps restore connection with yourself and understand what truly matters right now. And, as always: coffee, chatting, clearing your head, and good vibes in great company. 🍀 I'm Anna Potapova, psychologist, art therapist. @poaniki 🗓 Sat, September 19, 11:00 🖌 Materials: markers, watercolor, pencils, charcoal, A4/A5 paper — I'll bring them.

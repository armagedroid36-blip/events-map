Friends, we invite you to the art exhibition "Truth — Kindness — Tolerance"! 📅 Sunday, September 20, 16:00 The exhibition will feature reproductions of works by artists from China and Canada, created in classical and realistic style — from meditative scenes to philosophical canvases. Themes of the exhibition: spiritual improvement, the power of the human spirit, the revival of traditional culture, as well as the confrontation between good and evil. What awaits you: ✨ a guided tour with a story about each work ✨ tea and treats ✨ a handmade lotus flower as a gift The exhibition will be of interest to everyone who appreciates art, beauty, and the culture of the East.

Pesta Boneka in Ubud: performances on October 18

Event has ended — October 18, 2026

October 18, 2026

Jl. Cok Rai Pudak No.48, Peliatan, Kecamatan Ubud, Kabupaten Gianyar, Bali 80571, Indonesia

80000 IDR

On October 18, the final day of the Pesta Boneka #10 puppet theatre festival will take place at Titik Dua Ubud. Thailand's Talentshow Theatre will present the non-verbal Ta Lent Show at the COCA Art Space venue. Finland's KO-KOO-MO will perform Block Theatre in the amphitheatre. Both stages are located within the Titik Dua space in Peliatan. The one-day pass for October 18 is sold out. A separate ticket for each of the two performances costs 80,000 IDR; a three-day pass for the festival on October 16–18 is 463,500 IDR. Children under six enter free. Tickets for the performances and the three-day pass: https://megatix.co.id/events/holding-hands-pesta-boneka-on-wheels-bali

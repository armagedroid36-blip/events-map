1st Amathounta Municipality Gastronomy & Culture Festival The Amathounta Municipality is organising its 1st Gastronomy and Culture Festival on Sunday, 11 October 2026, at 18:00, at Peace Square, Amathounta Town Hall in Agios Athanasios. Under the theme “From Olympus to the Municipality of Amathounta”, the festival will celebrate gastronomy, culture and tradition, inviting visitors to enjoy a special evening filled with authentic flavours and cultural experiences. The Festival is held under the auspices of the Minister of Transport, Communications and Works, Ms Evy Tsolaki. 📅 Sunday, 11 Oct

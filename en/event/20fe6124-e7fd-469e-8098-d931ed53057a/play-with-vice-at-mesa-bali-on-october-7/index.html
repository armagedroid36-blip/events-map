On October 7, from 20:00 to 05:00 the next morning, PLAY with Vice — an evening of hip-hop and R&B — will take place at MESA Bali in Berawa. Live music plays on the rooftop from 20:00 to 22:00, followed by performances in the lounge by Rwin, Pablo, DyoMase, and MC Knowledge. Entry is 21+. The free guest list is valid for entry strictly before midnight and is limited to the first 150 guests; re-entry is not permitted with it. The General Admission ticket at 250,000 IDR is valid all evening and includes one drink. Registration and tickets for October 7: https://megatix.co.id/events/play-wednesday-mesa

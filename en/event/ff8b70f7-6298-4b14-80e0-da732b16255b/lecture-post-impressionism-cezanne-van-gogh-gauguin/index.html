Lecture "Post-Impressionism. Cézanne, Van Gogh, Gauguin" Post-Impressionism emerged from Impressionism but took completely different paths. And most interestingly: this movement laid the foundations for the further development of 20th-century art. We will explore: - How did Cézanne influence Cubism? - Why is Van Gogh considered a precursor of Expressionism? - How did Gauguin inspire the Fauves?

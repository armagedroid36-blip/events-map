Whispers of Legacy: The Art of John Corbidge

Event has ended — October 15, 2026

October 9, 2026, 7:00 PM

morfi gallery, Limassol, Cyprus

Opening reception on Friday 9 October at 7pm, addressed by Melissa Corbidge. The exhibition then stays open until Thursday 15 October. As part of Limassol Art Walks 2026, morfi gallery presents a retrospective exhibition showcasing a rare selection of paintings by the late artist John Corbidge. Curated by Anna Agapiou, the exhibition presents significant works drawn directly from the private collection of the artist's daughter, Melissa Corbidge, offering art lovers, collectors and a new generation of art enthusiasts a rare, intimate and deeply personal glimpse into Corbidge's artistic legacy

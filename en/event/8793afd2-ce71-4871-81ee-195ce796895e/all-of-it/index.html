Ktirion 53, Nicosia, Cyprus

KTHRION 53 presents, in a nationwide premiere, "All of It," a stream-of-consciousness monologue that follows the journey of an anonymous woman from birth to final parting. A torrent of thoughts and memories maps out all the stages of life: childhood, coming of age, sexuality, marriage, motherhood, daily life, loss, and death. A narrative with humor, honesty, and tenderness about life that passes and slips from our hands like a grain of sand. Premiere: September 20, 20:00 Performances: September 23, 27, 28 & 30 5,

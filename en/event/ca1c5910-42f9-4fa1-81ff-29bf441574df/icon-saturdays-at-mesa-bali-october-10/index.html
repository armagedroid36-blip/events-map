On October 10, from 20:00 to 05:00 the following morning, ICON Saturdays will take place at MESA Bali in Berawa — a club night with hip-hop, R&B, Latin music, and funk. Live music plays on the rooftop from 20:00 to 22:00, then DJs perform in the club and lounge. Entry from age 21. The free guest list is valid for entry strictly before midnight and is limited to the first 150 guests; no re-entry is allowed with it. A General Admission ticket for 250,000 IDR is valid all evening and includes one drink. Registration and tickets for October 10: https://megatix.co.id/events/mesa-icon

Throwback Thursday at MESA Bali on October 8

Event has ended — October 8, 2026

On October 8, from 20:00 to 05:00 the next morning, Throwback Thursday will take place at MESA Bali. Live music plays on the rooftop from 20:00 to 22:00, followed by sets from Anjaz, Ivebo, Goatee, and Pablo; MC Camel hosts the program. The evening is dedicated to R&B, hip-hop, and urban music. Entry is 21+. The free guest list is valid for entry strictly before midnight for the first 150 guests; re-entry with it is not possible. A General Admission ticket for 250,000 IDR is valid all evening and includes one drink. Guest list registration and tickets for October 8: https://megatix.co.id/events/throwback-thursday-the-garden-of-mesa

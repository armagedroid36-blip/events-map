🌿 YOGA FOR PREGNANT WOMEN Pregnancy is a time when your body changes every day. And movement helps not just to "maintain yourself," but to feel your body better, understand it, and learn to manage it. In class we will: • move and work with the body without overloading it; • release tension from the back, lower back, and pelvis; • maintain strength and mobility of the body; • practice breathing and relaxation; • learn to feel the body and manage your state; • prepare for childbirth through movement, breathing, and relaxation. Yoga for pregnant women is not about the perfect shape. It's about a body that becomes your support. Suitable for any level of fitness — even if you have never done yoga before. 📅 September 15 🕥 10:30–11:30 📍 Prime Space 💰 250,000 VND 📩 Registration: @katyahudyakova_yoga Come move, breathe, and enjoy your body — even when it changes every day. 🤍 #date: 15.09

Monkey Forest Ubud Run 2026 race

October 18, 2026, 5:00 AM

Monkey Forest Cental Parking, Ubud, Kecamatan Ubud, Kabupaten Gianyar, Bali 80571, Indonesia

Monkey Forest Ubud Run 2026 will take place on Sunday, October 18. The start is at 05:00 from the central Monkey Forest parking lot in Ubud. Participants can choose between 5 and 10 kilometer distances. This is an early morning run in one of Ubud's most recognizable areas, suitable both for those living on the island and for tourists ready to plan it into their trip in advance. Early bird tickets are already sold out, regular registration is available. Participation terms and tickets: https://megatix.co.id/white-label/monkey-forest-ubud-run-2026

"WHY DO I TRY TO EARN LOVE?" Fear of rejection, self-worth, and the habit of striving for others' attention. Women's gathering in a coffee brunch format🩷 ❓Why are we so strongly drawn to people whose attention must be won? ❓Why do we try to be prettier, more interesting, more agreeable in order to be liked? ❓And why are we sometimes so afraid of losing a person that we gradually begin to lose ourselves? ➡️And most importantly, how do we stop seeking confirmation of our worth in others' attention and learn to choose ourselves?

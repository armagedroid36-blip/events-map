Friends, we invite you to the art exhibition "Truth — Kindness — Patience"! 📅 Sunday, September 6, 4:00 PM The exhibition will feature reproductions of works by artists from China and Canada, executed in a classical and realistic style — from meditative scenes to philosophical canvases. Exhibition themes: spiritual refinement, the strength of the human spirit, the revival of traditional culture, as well as the confrontation between good and evil. You can expect: ✨ a guided tour with a story about each work ✨ tea and treats ✨ a handmade lotus flower as a gift The exhibition will be of interest to everyone who appreciates the art, beauty, and culture of the East. Free admission. Contact: @llliiir #date: 09/06/2026 Posted by: Sonya

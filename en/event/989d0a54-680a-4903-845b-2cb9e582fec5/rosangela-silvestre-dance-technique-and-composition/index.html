On October 7, choreographer Rosangela Silvestre will hold the first module of a dance workshop, Silvestre Technique: Practice and Composition, at The Yoga Barn. The session will take place from 11:00 to 12:30 WITA in the Mango studio on the upper floor. The practice focuses on dance technique, the symbolism of movement, and composition creation. Participants will explore how individual body characteristics and modes of expression become the foundation of dance. The cost of one module is 400,000 IDR. A package of two modules costs 750,000 IDR; the second, Symbology of Orixá, will take place on the same day from 13:30 to 15:00. Registration and payment can be completed online or at The Yoga Barn reception. Children under 10 are not admitted. Participants aged 10–15 must be accompanied by a parent, guardian, or sibling over 18. Please arrive 15 minutes early: latecomers may be denied entry without a refund. The ticket is personalized and non-transferable. Refunds are available if requested no later than 24 hours before the start; service fees are non-refundable.

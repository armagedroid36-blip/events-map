SYSTEMIC CONSTELLATIONS // SEPTEMBER 6 Sometimes a person understands their situation very well intellectually. They understand why relationships unfold the way they do. They understand where the fear comes from. They understand it's time to change jobs, step out of an old scenario, or finally make a decision. But understanding is not enough!! Because part of what drives our lives lies deeper than habitual logic — in relationships with parents, family ties, old adaptations, feelings, and inner decisions that once helped us survive but today only limit us. On September 6 at 12:00 we meet again at the open systemic constellations group in Nha Trang. You can come to the meeting with various requests: — relationships and partnership — mom, dad, and the parental family — money and fulfillment — difficult choices — recurring scenarios — inner conflict — the feeling that "I understand everything, but nothing changes" — or simply with a question that has been bothering you for a long time You don't have to do your own constellation right away. You can come as a guest, be a representative, watch how the field works, get acquainted with the constellation method and the facilitator. (if you want to guarantee working on your own situation, it's better to book a client spot in advance — the number of full sessions in one group is limited) The meeting is led by Oleg Shtark @ShtarkOleg — psychologist, systemic constellations facilitator, student of Hellinger, over 18 years of practice. 📍 Nha Trang, Yen Garden Bistro 🌞 September 6, Sunday ✍ 12:00 🐆 Participation for guests and representatives — donation To sign up, write @arhangelinaa the word CONSTELLATIONS. Come with your question. We will look not at how it "should be," but at what is actually happening. #date: 06.09

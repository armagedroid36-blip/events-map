LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD in Pafos

November 7, 2026, 3:00 PM

Markideio Municipal Theatre, Cyprus

With a new adaptation of the beloved fairy tale that generations grew up with, Skini 018 of the Cyprus Theatre Organisation opens the season. Little Red Riding Hood arrives in a modern adaptation by Andri Theodotou and with the poetic directorial vision of Fotis Nikolaou, to touch the sensitive chords of young and old spectators alike. Life's big and small questions, plenty of humour and a generous dose of imagination, promise a performance that will take us on a journey into a magical environment where anything can happen.

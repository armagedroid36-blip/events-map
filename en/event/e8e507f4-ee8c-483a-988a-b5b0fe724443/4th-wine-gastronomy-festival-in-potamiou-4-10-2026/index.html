Event has ended — October 4, 2026

Potamiou Village, Potamiou, Cyprus

Free

Potamious Festival is a festival of local traditions, culture, and community. Visitors can enjoy traditional foods and products such as palouzes, soutzoukos, trachanas, tremithopita, local grapes, wine, and zivania. The festival also offers workshops where participants can learn how to prepare traditional delicacies. Activities include a cycling wine route, a cultural exploration of Potamious through local stories and houses, and traditional music and dancing, creating an enjoyable experience for people of all ages.

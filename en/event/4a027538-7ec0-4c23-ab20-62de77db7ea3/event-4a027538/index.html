To Gala

October 21, 2026, 8:30 PM

Rialto Theatre, Limassol, Cyprus

Vasilis Katsikonouris’ acclaimed play is brought to the stage by Lexi Theatre, directed by Marina Vronti and produced by Giorgos Tsiakkas. A strong story that explores the limits of love and sacrifice, touching the most sensitive chords of human existence. With the captivating performances of Christina Pavlidou, Andreas Papamichalopoulos, Vassilis Charalambous and Stavriana Kadi. In Greek. Duration: 105’ Tickets: €25 / 22 / 18

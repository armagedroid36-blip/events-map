🌊 Son Tan Mountain Waterfalls • "Above the Mountains" This is one of the few routes near Nha Trang where we walk along the riverbed for most of the way. We'll be hopping from rock to rock, crossing small rapids, swimming in natural pools, and resting by wild waterfalls. And in a few spots, you can safely jump into the water from the rocks if you want to. We'll return along a trail to see the route from a different side. 🟡 Route level — yellow. Suitable for anyone ready to walk about 8–10 km. No special training needed, but feeling confident on rocks will be a plus. In terms of effort, it's roughly like Angel Mountain twice in a row. ⏱ About the route: — about 6–8 hours — 8–10 km — elevation gain of about 200 meters — 40–50 minutes from Nha Trang 🗓 October 3, Saturday 🕕 07:00 — meet up 🕡 07:15 — depart 📍 Locations: 🍽 Open meeting point 🏍 Open parking and start point 🍜 After the route, you can optionally have dinner together 🎒 What to bring: — sneakers with good grip — quick-dry sportswear — 3–4 liters of water — a light snack — a waterproof bag for your phone ⚠️ Important: — we walk along the riverbed for most of the route — clothes and shoes will almost certainly get wet — do not drink water from the river 💚 Participation is free. Registration is required, spots are limited. 📩 Register via direct message: @maxgooooo #date: 03.10

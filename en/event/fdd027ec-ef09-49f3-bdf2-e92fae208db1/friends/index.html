Friends! ✌️ This Sunday at Da Nang English Speaking Club — the best place for meeting people and practicing English — we will talk about traditions. Especially since Vietnam is currently celebrating the Mid-Autumn Festival — Tết Trung Thu. What better reason? We will talk about the customs and rituals that shape our lives, families, and culture. Some traditions unite people and create a sense of belonging, while others may seem outdated or unnecessary over time. Do you prefer to follow traditions or create your own? Which traditions are worth preserving, and which are better to let go of?

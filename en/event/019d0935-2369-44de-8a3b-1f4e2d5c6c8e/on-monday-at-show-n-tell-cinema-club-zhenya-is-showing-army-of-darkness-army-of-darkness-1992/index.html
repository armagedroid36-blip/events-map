🎬 On Monday at Show’n’Tell Cinema Club, Zhenya is screening "Army of Darkness" (1992)! Director Sam Raimi sends Ash Williams to medieval England, where he finds himself at the center of a battle against an army of the dead. Armed with a chainsaw, a shotgun, and enormous self-confidence, Ash must survive the undead onslaught and find a way back home. The film is packed with insane action, distinctive humor, absurd situations, and Raimi's signature visual style — it's a chaotic mix of horror, fantasy, and comedy. 🗣 The film will be shown in its original language with English subtitles, after which we'll discuss the film together and share our impressions. 📽 Admission: donation (recommended amount 50k) ⏰ Time: September 28, 19.00 📍 Venue: The Workshop 📫 Address: 35 Che Lan Vien, Da Nang, Vietnam Contact info @infraredfade #date: 28.09

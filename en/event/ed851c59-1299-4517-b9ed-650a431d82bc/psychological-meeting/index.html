Psychological meeting "Attachment Types and Our Relationship Scenarios" Someone, feeling distance from their partner, starts to get anxious, writes, asks: "Do you really love me?" Someone else, on the contrary, pulls away first — stops replying, shuts down, and convin themselves: "I don't need anyone at all." And someone might simultaneously crave closeness and fear it, now drawing near, now sharply pushing the person away. Behind these reactions often lies an attachment type — how we are used to building close relationships, experiencing distance, asking for support, and coping with the fear of loss. At the meeting, we will explore: — what attachment types exist and how they differ — why one person can trigger intense anxiety in us, while another makes us want to run away — how our habitual relationship scenarios are formed — why we sometimes choose partners who activate our most painful spots — how anxious, avoidant, and disorganized attachment manifest — and most importantly — whether it is possible to change our habitual way of building closeness This will not be a "what type am I" test, but an opportunity to better understand yourself and your relationships. If any of this sounds familiar: "I get too attached" "I constantly fear being abandoned" "As soon as someone gets too close — I want to disappear" "Why am I so drawn to emotionally unavailable people?" — this meeting is for you. 🗓️ September 6 (Sun) at 7:00 PM 📍Wind Garden ✅ Cost: 250k VND 👥 Limited number of spots 📩 Registration: @Dana_psychology #date: 6.09

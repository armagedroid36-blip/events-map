On October 8, from 14:00 to 16:00 at Good Times General Store — Bay 5 at Desa Potato Head in Seminyak, the Chroma workshop will take place. Participants create colors from kitchen scraps, marigold flowers, and turmeric, then dye a bandana and a T-shirt or bag. The session is dedicated to natural dyes and material reuse. A General Admission ticket costs 1,000,000 IDR; blanks made from cotton scraps or recycled linen and dyeing materials are included. Tickets for October 8: https://megatix.co.id/events/chroma-natural-dye-workshop

Dusty Munky, Limassol, Cyprus

On Friday, 20 November, Rick Offen and Ander Race take over Dusty Munky. Founder of Ritmo Futuro, Rick Offen brings a sound shaped between Portugal and the UK, blending rhythm, emotion and storytelling into deep, sophisticated musical journeys. Joining him is Ander Race, one of Barcelona’s consistent names, known for his soulful sets and releases on Anims, W (Iconyc) and Urge To Dance, with upcoming music on Magnifik and a debut album on Ritmo Futuro.

🦇 PLAYING "ONE NIGHT" 🦇 One Night Werewolf — a fast-paced game of bluff and intuition. At the start of the game, everyone receives a secret role. Night falls, and the werewolves, the seer, the troublemaker, and other characters take turns using their abilities, and in the morning the collective investigation begins. ⏳ You'll only have a few minutes to figure out: 🩸 who is lying; 🩸 whose role was swapped; 🩸 who actually saw the werewolf; 🩸 and who serves the darkness themselves. After the discussion, everyone simultaneously votes for a suspect and shoots. The villagers must find the werewolf, while the werewolves must confuse the others and survive the vote. 🔥 Here you can't even trust yourself: while you were asleep, your role may have changed...

Club VOLSHEBNAYA SPICHKA — learning to make wishes come true 10.10 at 10:10 — first open meeting. I share knowledge about a technology that helps me expand my possibilities. What if a wish is not something to be pursued, but something that already exists within us? All that's left is to clear the way for it. At the meeting we'll find out why I not only "have the right to want" — "I am obliged to want." We'll explore our desires and create an Intention to achieve our "want." We'll look at what exactly prevents them from becoming reality.

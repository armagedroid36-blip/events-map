🎬 On Monday at Show’n’Tell Cinema Club, Jake is showing “Possession” (1981)! Director Andrzej Żuławski tells the story of a married couple whose relationship begins to unravel in increasingly terrifying and mysterious ways. As the relationship descends into paranoia, jealousy, and violence, it becomes harder to tell where emotional breakdown ends and something far more sinister begins. A disturbing, surreal, and emotionally extreme film about love, obsession, betrayal, and the dark side of human relationships. 🗣 The film will be screened in its original language with English subtitles, followed by a group discussion where we’ll share our impressions. 📽 Admission: donation (recommended amount 50k) ⏰ Time: October 5, 19:00 📍 Venue: The Workshop 📫 Address: 35 Che Lan Vien, Da Nang, Vietnam Contact info @infraredfade #date: 5.10

The Effect

Event has ended — October 17, 2026

October 17, 2026, 8:00 PM

Satiriko Theatre, Nicosia, Cyprus

The Satiriko Theatre presents for the first time in Cyprus the play "The Effect" by award-winning British playwright Lucy Prebble, directed by Evita Ioannou Connie and Tristan participate in a clinical trial for a new antidepressant drug. Within the framework of the experiment, they meet and fall in love. Soon, however, the question arises: is what they feel true love or a result of the medication? As the effect of the drug grows, the boundaries between reality and the possible illusions caused by its side effects begin to blur

Bali Emergency Care Conference 2026 - a professional conference for emergency and urgent care specialists. The program takes place at The Trans Resort Bali in Seminyak and is designed for doctors, nurses, paramedics and other medical specialists. The program features interactive CPD sessions, e-learning, practical workshops, a welcome event and a closing event. For BaliForum, this is a niche but significant educational event: it expands the events section beyond parties, wellness and festivals. Registration is paid: for nurses, paramedics and intern doctors, the early rate is A$849, the regular rate is A$949; for consultants, specialists and general practitioners - A$1,149 and A$1,249 respectively.

An Evening of Operetta - Singing in the Storm

November 22, 2026, 7:00 PM

Rialto Theatre, Limassol, Cyprus

A host of Cypriot classical singers delve into the enchanting world of Greek operetta through much-loved excerpts from works by Theophrastos Sakellaridis. From The Daughter of the Storm, Pic Nic, I Want to See the Pope and The Godson to other popular hits of the era, the evening brings to life melodies brimming with romance, humour, theatrical flair and nostalgia. A musical-theatre evening that seeks, despite the storms of everyday life, a brief escape into the joy and power of song. Even when the storm is raging outside, there is always a picnic happening somewhere. Soloists: Margarita Eli

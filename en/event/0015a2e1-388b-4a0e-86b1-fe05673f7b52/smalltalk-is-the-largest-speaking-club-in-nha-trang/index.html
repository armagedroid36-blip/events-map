🎤 SmallTalk — the biggest speaking club in Nha Trang Want to speak more English but don't like presenting in front of the whole group? At SmallTalk, it's simple: you chat one-on-one, and every 15 minutes you meet a new conversation partner. Topics can be anything, and beginners and advanced speakers practice separately, so everyone feels comfortable. ✨ Our record — 55 participants at a single meeting.

The Heroic Muse – Breast Cancer Awareness Edition

Event has ended — October 25, 2026

October 25, 2026, 7:00 PM

Ktirion 53, Nicosia, Cyprus

This October, Sketching Sundays presents The Heroic Muse — a special live figure drawing session dedicated to beauty, strength, femininity, and the female form after mastectomy. Through art, we celebrate the idea that a woman’s beauty and identity are not defined by her body or by what she has been through. Expect expressive poses, curated lighting, and a powerful pink-and-black setting created especially for this Breast Cancer Awareness edition. All funds raised will be donated to the Cyprus Anti-Cancer Society.

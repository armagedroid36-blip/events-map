Kyperounta Christmas Village

December 6, 2026, 10:00 AM

Kyperounta Village Square, Limassol, Cyprus

Kyperounta is located in the Troodos mountain range at the foot of Mount Madari. It belongs to the Pitsilia region and falls under the Limassol District. Kyperounta has an altitude of approximately 1,300 meters above sea level. It is the largest mountain village in Cyprus and is famous for its apples, wine, and traditional cold cuts. This village, with its dense foliage, retains its traditional character. A particular feature of the village is its steep uphill roads, the vineyards, the small picturesque courtyards of the houses, the high-ceilinged rural storerooms, and the houses with tiled roofs.

Painting excursions with the Troodos Art Club in Dymes

November 25, 2026, 11:00 AM

Dymes Village Square, Limassol, Cyprus

The experiential painting tour workshop by the Troodos Art Club invites young and old to discover the beauty of the mountain villages through art. Under the guidance of visual artist Konstantina Niina Achilleos, participants will wander through the picturesque alleys, observe nature, traditional architecture, and the small details that compose the character of each community, and capture them in a series of painting creations.

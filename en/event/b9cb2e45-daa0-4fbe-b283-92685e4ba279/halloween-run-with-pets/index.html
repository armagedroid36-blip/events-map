Elpis Run Club is organising “Halloween Run with Pets” at Limassol Molos, inviting children and adults to take part in a festive activity alongside their beloved pets. The event combines exercise, entertainment and a love for animals, bringing together Halloween spirit and a charitable cause. All proceeds from participation fees will be donated to Project Whisker Animal Welfare Society, supporting its work in protecting and caring for animals.

On October 2, Free Your Voice with Australian musician Holloway will take place at Studio Lotus in The Yoga Barn complex in Ubud. This is a group vocal gathering from 16:30 to 18:00 WITA. Participation fee is 450,000 IDR. Participants will try breathing, rhythm, and voice exercises, guided improvisation, and group singing. No musical experience is required: the program does not involve solo performances or judging singing quality. The finale will feature live instrumental music. Participation is allowed from age 10. Children aged 10–15 need a parent, guardian, or a sibling over 18. The ticket is personalized and cannot be transferred to another person. Arrive 15 minutes before the start: if you are late, entry may be denied without a refund. You can request a ticket refund no later than 24 hours before the start via the Yoga Barn reception. Booking fees are non-refundable. Buy a ticket for Free Your Voice (https://megatix.co.id/events/free-your-voice-OCTOBER).

Friends! ✌️ This Sunday at Da Nang English Speaking Club — the best place to meet people and practice English — we'll be talking about travel: where we've already been, where we dream of going, and what makes us ready to pack our bags again. What's more your style — a spontaneous trip with a backpack or a vacation planned down to the smallest detail? Popular attractions or places only locals know about? And what travel story still makes you smile? Come share your adventures, find ideas for your next trip, and meet people from all over the world. You don't have to cross half the planet for a great conversation — sometimes the most memorable story happens right nearby! 🌍✨ We communicate in English, exchange impressions, and spend Sunday evening in good company.

The Municipality of Kythrea is organizing the anti-occupation artistic event With a Suitcase and a Key

Event has ended — October 30, 2026

October 30, 2026, 7:00 PM

Αίθουσα Μελίνα Μερκούρη, Nicosia, Cyprus

The Municipality of Kythrea is organizing on Friday, October 30, 2026, at 19:00, at the "Melina Mercouri" Hall in Nicosia, an anti-occupation artistic event titled "With a Suitcase and a Key," dedicated to the memory of the occupied homeland and to keeping alive the hope of return. The event will be honored by the presence of the Presidential Commissioner, Mr. Marios Chartsiotis, on behalf of the President of the Republic, who will deliver a greeting. Greetings will also be delivered by the Mayor of Kythrea, Mr. Marios Zampakidis, and the Mayor of the twinned Municipality of Aigaleo, Mr. Lampros

Group meeting: Osteopath session for children and adults. Dear parents and adults, I invite you to discover osteopathy. Sometimes we seek help because of back or neck pain, discomfort after childbirth, the consequences of injuries, or constant tension. And when it comes to children, we may be concerned about posture, motor development, and overall well-being. What can you consult an osteopath about? For children: posture and motor development peculiarities; tension and discomfort in the body; recovery after childbirth. For adults: tension in the back, neck, and lower back; headaches; a feeling of stiffness and muscle tension; recovery after childbirth and injuries; stress and difficulty relaxing. Osteopathy is not only about working with individual parts of the body, but also about a attentive attitude toward the person as a whole. My name is Sergey. I will be glad to answer your questions.

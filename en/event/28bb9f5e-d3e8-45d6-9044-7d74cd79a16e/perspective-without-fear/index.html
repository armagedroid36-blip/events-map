Perspective without fear 🖊 How do you fit an entire city onto a sheet of paper? Shama Bakery, 3rd floor, view of the sea and the city From the third floor you can see everything at once: the promenade, the buildings, the sea, the clouds. Usually at that moment your hand freezes, because it's unclear where to start and how to fit it all into the format of a sheet In an hour and a half we'll figure out how to choose what matters most, build depth, and make a sketch that has air in it Markers, liners, and paper are ready!

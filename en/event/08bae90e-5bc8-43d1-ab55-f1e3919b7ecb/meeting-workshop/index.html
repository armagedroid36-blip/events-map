Workshop meetup "HOW IS OUR PSYCHE STRUCTURED?" Why do we understand everything but keep repeating the same patterns? And what inside us governs feelings, decisions, and relationships with others? On September 24, the first meetup in a series of psychology lectures will take place. We will gradually explore how the psyche is structured, how personality is formed, how we build relationships with other people, and why understanding a problem alone is sometimes not enough to change something. The first meetup will be dedicated directly to the psyche — its structure, functions, and internal processes that influence our perception of ourselves and the world around us. How the meetup will unfold: 💬 introductions and a short sharing 📖 theoretical part 🧩 a large practical block where you can explore the topic through your own feelings, reactions, and contact with other participants Facilitator — Aleksandr Mekhedov, practicing psychologist, student at the International Gestalt School. 📍 Nha Trang, Prime Space 📅 September 24, Thursday 🕥 10:30–13:00 🌿 Participation — donation To sign up, message @photobraun Limited number of spots We will not only talk about the psyche but also observe how it works right during our meetup. #date: 24.09

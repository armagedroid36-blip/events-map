💥Dragon Labyrinth, sheep farm and Cam Ranh beaches💥 📍September 20, departure at 9:00 from Oceanus (we'll also pick up in the center), arrival at 19:00📍 1️⃣First we go to the dragon labyrinth. It's a temple complex with a huge dragon, and you can walk inside it, they say it cleanses you of sins). The path winds a lot, narrows in places, you can go through with a flashlight, and for those who want thrills - without one. Entrance is free. We'll spend an hour to an hour and a half there, depending on how it goes. 2️⃣Then we'll drive for about 20-30 minutes and find ourselves on a gorgeous lawn among the mountains, where there are lots of cute sheep. It's just very beautiful and touching, lots of photo spots, swings, etc. Entrance costs 50 dong, paid on site. We'll also spend about 1-1.5 hours there. 3️⃣And after that we'll go swimming at the endless beaches of Cam Ranh 🌊🌊🌊 entrance is free and you can have lunch right on the shore at local cafes with seafood ☝️Participation in the joint trip is free, transfer fee is 490 VND.

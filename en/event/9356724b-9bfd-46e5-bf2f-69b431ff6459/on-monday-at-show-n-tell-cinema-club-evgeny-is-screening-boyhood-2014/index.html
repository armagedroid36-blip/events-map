🎬 On Monday at Show’n’Tell Cinema Club, Evgeny screens “Boyhood” (2014)! Director Richard Linklater tells the story of Mason — from childhood through adolescence and into his first years of adult life. The film’s uniqueness lies in the fact that it was shot with the same actors over 12 years, so we literally watch the characters and the actors themselves grow up and change. Instead of unexpected twists and big drama, “Boyhood” finds the interesting in ordinary moments of life: relationships with family, first love, friendship, moves, and the gradual understanding of who you want to become.

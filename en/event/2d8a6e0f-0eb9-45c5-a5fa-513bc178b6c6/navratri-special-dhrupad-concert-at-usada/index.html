On October 16 at Usada Bali in Ubud, a Navratri Special concert will take place featuring Dhani Gundecha and Dnyaneshwar Deshmukh. Start time is 19:00 WITA, the performance will be held on the second floor of the center. Dhani Gundecha will perform a vocal Dhrupad program, and Dnyaneshwar Deshmukh will perform the Pakhawaj part. The evening is dedicated to Devi Bhakti compositions, ancient ragas, and spiritual poetry in the Navratri tradition. Early bird rate is 185,400 IDR, standard is 206,000 IDR. As of September 27, 2026, online sales are not yet open. Tickets: Navratri Special Concert (https://megatix.com.au/events/Navratri-Special-Concert).

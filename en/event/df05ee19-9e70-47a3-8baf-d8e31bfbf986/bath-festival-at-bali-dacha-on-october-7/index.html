Bath Festival at Bali Dacha on October 7

Event has ended — October 7, 2026

October 7, 2026, 4:00 PM

Bedulu, Blahbatuh, Bedulu, Blahbatuh, Kabupaten Gianyar, Bali

600000 IDR

On October 7, from 16:00 to 22:00, a one-day bath festival will take place at Bali Dacha in Ubud. Three Russian banyas and an art hammam will be operating; the program also includes a tea ceremony, group aroma steam sessions, a fire show, sound meditation, and ecstatic dance. The tea ceremony and banyas are open from 16:00 to 22:00, group aroma steam sessions run from 17:00 to 20:00. The fire show will begin at 19:00, the nail-standing practice at 19:30, and sound meditation and ecstatic dance at 20:30. Participation costs 600,000 IDR. The same program is announced for October 11.

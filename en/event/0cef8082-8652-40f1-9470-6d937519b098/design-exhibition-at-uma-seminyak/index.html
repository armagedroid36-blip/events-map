MBDW 2026 — Chapter Bali is back! The CONTINUUM — Daur Rupa exhibition offers a fresh perspective on the places, materials, and everyday practices that shape our perception of Bali. The project features research and works by Lingkar Studi Arsitektur, Rania Sidanta, Tisa Granicia, Menggah Agung, Wayan Subudi, Pulau Studio, Yoga Natha, and TIBA TIBA, as well as collaborative projects by Capybara UV × Melontang Project × Nuturang. The exhibition brings together different approaches to studying the environment, rethinking the familiar, and seeking new ideas. From cultural heritage and the properties of materials to the concept of the "third place" — explore observations, research, and design solutions that raise important questions: what do we preserve? What do we change? And what do we continue to develop? 📍 Uma Seminyak, Jl.

Anniversary concert CORFU - CYPRUS: From West to East

October 28, 2026, 7:00 PM

Eleftheria Square, Nicosia, Cyprus

The Band of the Philharmonic Society of Corfu (PALAIA 1840) is coming to Nicosia for a special concert on October 28, 2026. Nicosia is preparing to host one of the most important cultural events of the year. On Wednesday, October 28, 2026, the historic Band of the "Philharmonic Society of Corfu" (PALAIA 1840) travels to Cyprus for the grand anniversary concert "CORFU – CYPRUS | From West to East. Two Islands, One Nation, One Music full of Greece." The concert will take place at 19:00 in the evening, at Eleftheria Square in Nicosia, with free admission

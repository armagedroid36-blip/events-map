2nd Gastronomy Festival Days of Commandaria and Tradition

October 18, 2026, 11:00 AM

Kalo Chorio Lemesou Village Square, Cyprus

The 2nd Gastronomy Festival "Commandaria and Tradition Days" of Kalo Chorio Lemesou, which was scheduled to take place on September 19 and 20, 2026, and due to mourning in the community, was postponed, has announced a new date. The event has been rescheduled and will take place on October 17 and 18, 2026, with the festival's program and activities being transferred to the new dates. So, note the new dates and get ready for a two-day event dedicated to Commandaria, gastronomy, and Cypriot tradition.

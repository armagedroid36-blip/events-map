🌊 Song Tan Mountain Waterfalls • "Above the Mountains" This is one of the few routes near Nha Trang where we walk directly along the bed of a mountain river for most of the way. We'll be jumping from rock to rock, crossing small rapids, swimming in natural pools, and resting by wild waterfalls. In several spots, if you wish, you can safely jump into the water from the rocks. We'll return via a trail to see the route from the other side. 🟢 Route level — green. Suitable for beginners and anyone ready to cover about 8–10 km. No special training is needed, but feeling confident on rocks will be a plus. ⏱ About the route: — about 6–8 hours — 8–10 km — elevation gain of about 200 meters — 40–50 minutes drive from Nha Trang 🗓 Saturday, September 6 🕕 07:45 — meeting 🕡 08:00 — departure 📍 Locations: 🍽 Open meeting point 🏍 Open parking and starting point 🏞 Open natural pools and waterfalls 🍜 After the route, we can have dinner together if you wish 🎒 What to bring: — sneakers with good grip — quick-drying sportswear — 2–3 liters of water — light snack — dry bag for phone and documents ⚠️ Important: — most of the route goes along the riverbed — clothes and shoes will almost certainly get wet — mobile signal may disappear near waterfalls — we don't drink water from the river 💚 Participation is free. Registration is required, spots are limited. 📩 Sign up via direct message: @maxgooooo #date: 06.09

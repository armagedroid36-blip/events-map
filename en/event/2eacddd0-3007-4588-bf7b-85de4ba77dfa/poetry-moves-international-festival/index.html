Poetry MOVES International Festival / International Festival of Poetry and Syn+Movement

October 21, 2026, 6:00 PM

Artos House, Cyprus

The International Poetry and Syn+Movement Festival "Poetry MOVES International Festival / International Poetry and Syn+Movement Festival" includes exhibitions, music, videos, and performances. For the 4th consecutive year, the Poetry MOVES International Festival returns, having now been established as an international, annual institution. Poetry MOVES is not a poetry festival in the traditional sense. It is an innovative, multi-thematic festival that places poetry at the core of all arts and functions as a platform for dialogue between poetry, music, contemporary dance, song, photography, and other

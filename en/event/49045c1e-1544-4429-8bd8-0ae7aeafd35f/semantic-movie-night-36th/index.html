🎥 MEANINGFUL MOVIE NIGHT #36 with a film discussion 🎬 "The Cosmos Is Falling Asleep" 2025, starring Mark Eydelshteyn ("Anora") Sometimes, to get closer to your dream, you first need to deal with what you left behind. A story about choosing between a big future and the people who need us here and now. About a home you want to escape from, and the roots that keep holding you back. And about how not to lose your dream when life suddenly changes all your plans. 🗣🎬 After the screening, we'll discuss the film together – we'll talk about symbols, images, and hidden meanings, look at what psychological processes drive the characters' actions, and try to understand why this particular story resonates with each person differently. ❗️P.s. if you just want to watch the film in Russian dub on the big screen, with great picture and sound, you can skip the discussion – it's optional 📅 Friday 16.10 🕖 Start: 18:30 📍 Venue: Tổ càphê 💰 Price: 150k ✉️ Registration – @breezeblock_psy #date: 16.10

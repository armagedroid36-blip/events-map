Meeting "Gestalt Underground" A secret society of ordinary people A slightly strange name that perfectly captures the atmosphere of what we will be doing with you this Thursday 🙂 I invite you to spend a few hours in our small gestalt community. You will find out the topic of the meeting once you're there — let it remain a small intrigue for now, but as always the program will have three parts: — Sharing: we'll share what we came with and listen to each other. — Theory: we'll talk about the topic of the meeting in simple and clear language. — Practice: through exercises we'll see how all of this manifests in our lives. With each meeting this format becomes more thought-out and cohesive. It seems to me that right now I've just found the way I want to hold our meetings going forward. I invite you to take part — both your experience and your feedback are important to me.

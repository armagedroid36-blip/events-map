COCORICO: French Party at MESA Bali on October 5

Event has ended — October 5, 2026

October 5, 2026, 8:00 PM

On October 5, from 20:00 to 05:00 the next morning, COCORICO will take place at MESA Bali in Berawa — a French party with Afrobeats, hip-hop, and Shatta. From 20:00 to 22:00, live music plays on the rooftop, then Anjaz, Talisa, DyoMase, O.N.E, Badessone, and Pablo play in the club and lounge; the program is hosted by MC Baggio and MC Meekz. Entry is 21+. The free guest list is valid for entry strictly before midnight and is limited to the first 150 guests; re-entry with it is not allowed. The General Admission ticket for 250,000 IDR is valid all evening and includes one drink. Registration and tickets for October 5: https://megatix.co.id/events/cocorico24

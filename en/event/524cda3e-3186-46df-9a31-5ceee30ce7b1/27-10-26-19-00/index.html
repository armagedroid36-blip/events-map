Strovolos Cultural Centre, Nicosia, Cyprus

PRESS RELEASE The Municipality of Strovolos and Mov Skiouros Publications invite you to the presentation of Evangelia Charalambous' novel "Mikrokosmika" on Tuesday, October 27 at 19:00 at the Strovolos Cultural Center. "Mikrokosmika" (Mov Skiouros Publications, Athens, 2026) is a composite, social novel (336 pages) that unfolds the stories of the people of a city. It consists of 52 stories of everyday people, some shorter and some longer, which compose the mosaic of the city. In third-person narration, many microcosms form a mosaic of microcosms

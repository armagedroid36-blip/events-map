Bali Fashion Network: Fashion Supplier Fair in Denpasar

Event has ended — October 17, 2026

On October 17, from 11:30 to 18:00, Bali Fashion Network will take place at H Convention Hall at Harris Hotel & Residence Sunset Road in Denpasar. More than 65 companies will present fabrics, garment manufacturing, printing, packaging, and accessories. At the fair, you can meet suppliers and partners for the fashion business in person. The Final Release ticket costs 257,500 IDR; an on-site entrance ticket is 515,000 IDR. Buy a ticket for October 17: Megatix (https://megatix.co.id/events/bali-fashion-network-2027).

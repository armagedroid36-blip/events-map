What The Body Remembers: exhibition at Nuanu until November 29

Event has ended — October 8, 2026

October 8, 2026, 12:00 PM

Jalan Galiran Br Nyanyi, Beraban, Kec. Kediri, Kabupaten Tabanan, Bali 82121, Indonesia

Free

The exhibition What The Body Remembers is open at Labyrinth Art Gallery in Nuanu Creative City until November 29. It can be visited daily from 12:00; admission to the gallery is free. The exhibition features 26 artists, designers, and studios from Indonesia and India. Through contemporary art, textiles, craft, and fashion, they explore bodily memory, the transmission of skills, the migration of ideas, and cultural exchange. The exhibition is curated by Bandana Tewari and Brina Paska.

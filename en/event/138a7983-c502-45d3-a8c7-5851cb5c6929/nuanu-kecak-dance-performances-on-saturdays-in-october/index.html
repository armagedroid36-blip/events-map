Event has ended — October 17, 2026

On October 10, 17, 24, and 31 at 7:30 PM at Labyrinth Backyard next to Labyrinth Dome in the Nuanu Creative City area, a Balinese Kecak dance show will take place. The performance combines traditional dance with the modern stage environment of Nuanu. The ticket includes entry to Nuanu. For Indonesian citizens and KITAS holders, an adult ticket costs 220,000 IDR, a child ticket — 170,000 IDR. For international guests, an adult ticket costs 270,000 IDR, a child ticket — 220,000 IDR. To choose a date and buy a ticket: https://www.nuanu.com/events/nuanu-kecak-dance

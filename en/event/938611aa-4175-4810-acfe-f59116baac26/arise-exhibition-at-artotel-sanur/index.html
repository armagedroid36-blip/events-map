ARISE Exhibition at ARTOTEL Sanur

Event has ended — October 19, 2026

October 19, 2026, 4:30 PM

Jl. Kusuma Sari No.1, Sanur, Denpasar Selatan, Kota Denpasar, Bali 80227, Indonesia

We invite you to the solo exhibition of artist Wayan Aris Sarmanta (@aristsarts) titled ARISE. ARISE is a story of awakening, growth, and becoming. Not just a movement forward and liberation from the past, but a rediscovery of oneself, the attainment of a new awareness, and the embrace of change and new opportunities. 📅 Exhibition dates: August 10 — October 19, 2026📍 Venue: ARTspace, ARTOTEL Sanur Bali The exhibition opening took place on August 10 at 16:30. It was an evening of stories, colors, conversation, and art that invited guests to pause, look closely at the details, and see familiar things in a new way. And this is only the beginning — there is much more interesting to come. Supported by @studionutur and @senidibali.Organizer: @artotelsanur. instagram (https://www.instagram.com/studionutur/)

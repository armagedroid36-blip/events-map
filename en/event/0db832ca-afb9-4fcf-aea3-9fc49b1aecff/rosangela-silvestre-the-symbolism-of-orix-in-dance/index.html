On October 7, Rosangela Silvestre will hold the second module of her dance workshop — Symbology of Orixá — at The Yoga Barn. The session will take place from 13:30 to 15:00 WITA in the Mango studio on the upper floor. Participants will explore the symbolism of the Orixá tradition through movement, sound, and the expressive forms of traditional dances. The workshop focuses on artistic experience and the exploration of dance language. A single module costs 400,000 IDR. A two-module package is 750,000 IDR; the first, Silvestre Technique, will take place on the same day from 11:00 to 12:30. Registration and payment are available online and at The Yoga Barn reception. Children under 10 are not admitted. Participants aged 10–15 must be accompanied by a parent, guardian, or sibling over 18. Please arrive 15 minutes early: latecomers may be denied entry without a refund. Tickets are personalized and non-transferable. Refunds are available upon request no later than 24 hours before the start; service fees are non-refundable.

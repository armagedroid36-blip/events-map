On 18 October from 20:00 to 21:00 at Klymax in Desa Potato Head in Seminyak, Bali 1928: Live Score Cinema will take place. On screen — archival footage of Balinese ceremonies, gamelan and everyday life of the late 1920s. The screening is accompanied by live music and a talk by cultural archivist Marlowe Bandem about the restored materials of Arsip Bali 1928. A General Admission ticket costs 100,000 IDR. Tickets for 18 October: https://megatix.co.id/events/bali-1928-live-score-cinema

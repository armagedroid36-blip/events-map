Cyberness Wild West Market @ Kolla

Event has ended — October 3, 2026

October 3, 2026, 1:00 PM

Kolla Culture Factory, Limassol, Cyprus

Cyberness returns to Limassol with a new Wild West-themed edition, bringing together local makers, designers, food vendors and performers for a two-day creative market at Kolla. Visitors can explore stalls featuring independent brands and handmade products, enjoy food and drinks, DJ sets and live performances, take part in creative workshops and discover activities for children. The event is also pet-friendly. Dates: 3–4 October 2026 Venue: Kolla, Limassol Admission: Free

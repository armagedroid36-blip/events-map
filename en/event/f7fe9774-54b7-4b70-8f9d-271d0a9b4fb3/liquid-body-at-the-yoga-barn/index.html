Liquid Body at The Yoga Barn

September 19, 2026, 11:30 AM

F7M8+PVG, Jl. Sukma Kesuma, Peliatan, Kecamatan Ubud, Kabupaten Gianyar, Bali 80571, Indonesia

250000 IDR

On September 19, from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM at The Yoga Barn, Liquid Body will take place — a water movement and sound practice in a warm pool. Participants will explore free movement, reflection, and interaction in the water; the session will conclude with a floating meditation with gentle support from facilitators. No swimming or dance experience is required. The cost is 400,000 IDR, and for Indonesian citizens and KITAS holders — 250,000 IDR. Registration and payment are available at The Yoga Barn reception. It is recommended to arrive 15 minutes before the start.

Friends! ✌️ This Sunday at Da Nang English Speaking Club — the best place for making connections and practicing English — we’ll be talking about the balance between work and personal life — about how we allocate our time, energy, and priorities. Is it really possible to "get everything done," or is balance more about choice? We’ll discuss burnout, productivity, rest, and the pressure of constant busyness. How do different cultures approach the work-life balance? And what does it look like for you personally?

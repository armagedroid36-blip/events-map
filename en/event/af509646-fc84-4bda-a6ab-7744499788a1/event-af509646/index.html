Ah! That wife of mine on Klirou

October 24, 2026, 8:00 PM

Klirou Village Square, Cyprus

The Theater Group of ESTIA Multipurpose Center presents the classic Greek comedy by the writing duo Tsiforos – Vasileiadis "Ah! This Wife of Mine". It is a comedy of misunderstandings, particularly beloved by the general public due to its 1967 film adaptation directed by Giorgos Skalenakis and starring A. Vougiouklaki, D. Papamichail, S. Notara and other iconic actors.

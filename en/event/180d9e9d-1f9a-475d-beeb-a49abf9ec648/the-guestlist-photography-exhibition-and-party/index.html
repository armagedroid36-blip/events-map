THE GUESTLIST - Photography exhibition and Party

Event removed from the lineup — October 9, 2026

October 9, 2026, 3:00 PM

S//IP Slip Nightclub, Cyprus

**Shortened Description:** Photography, music, and club culture intertwine in *The Guestlist*, an exhibition by New York photographer and DJ Jamie Jar. Through his archive of nightlife moments, Jar reflects on the power of underground scenes—where music, people, and spaces collide. The night blends visual storytelling with live DJ sets, featuring KALYJAMA (Athens) and PICADILLYS (Cyprus). KALYJAMA blends psychedelic grooves and deep vinyl sets, while PICADILLYS crafts cosmic journeys across global sounds. Free before 22:00, then €12. See you on the dancefloor.

BMW Motorrad Days Bali 2026

September 25, 2026, 3:00 PM

Pulau Peninsula Nusa Dua, Nusa Dua, Benoa, Kec. Kuta Sel., Kabupaten Badung, Bali 80363, Indonesia

700000 IDR

BMW Motorrad Days will be held in Bali for the first time. The two-day festival at Peninsula Island will bring together the international motorcycle community around riding, adventures, an exhibition program, and entertainment; participation is open not only to BMW owners. On September 25, the program runs from 3:00 PM to 10:00 PM, and on September 26, from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM Bali local time. Tickets start from 700,000 IDR. For riding activities, a valid license and appropriate protective gear are required. Tickets: https://www.loket.com/event/bmw-motorrad-days-bali-2026-wye

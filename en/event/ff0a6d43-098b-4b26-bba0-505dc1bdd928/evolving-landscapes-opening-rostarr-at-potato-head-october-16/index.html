Evolving Landscapes Opening: Rostarr at Potato Head October 16

Event has ended — October 16, 2026

October 16, 2026, 3:00 PM

Jl. Petitenget No.51B, Kerobokan Kelod, Kec. Kuta Utara, Kabupaten Badung, Bali 80361, Indonesia

250000 IDR

On October 16 at 15:00 at Desa Potato Head in Seminyak, the unveiling of Evolving Landscapes will begin — a new large-scale facade mural by artist Rostarr. The program includes a conversation between Rostarr and Daniel Mitchell about working on the installation, and at 17:00 the opening ceremony with Balinese gamelan and performances by Bumi Bajra, Putu Septa, and Nata Swara. After the ceremony, Archie, Ryosuke Fukunaga, Yuka Mizuhara, Eric Duncan, Alex From Tokyo, and Suze Ijó will perform at the venue. In the evening, the facade will become a screen for projections, and the music program will continue at Klymax. The mural itself will remain at Desa Potato Head for about a year. A general ticket costs 250,000 IDR and includes one cocktail. Buy a ticket for the opening: https://megatix.co.id/events/evolving-landscapes-rostarr

🔥 Evening by the campfire on the seashore • "Above the Mountains" On Sunday we're gathering at Cozy Beach to spend an evening by the sea: campfire, tea, music, new acquaintances and conversations about everything. No stage, no hosts, no rigid program. You can come alone, meet people, sit by the fire, play some music, or just spend the evening in good company. And a special topic for those who have recently arrived in Nha Trang or are just figuring out how life abroad works. Among the participants will be people who have lived outside their home country for more than 15 years and have lived in various places. You can ask about Vietnam and other countries: where it's more comfortable to live, how to handle visa issues, find housing and the right people, adapt, find your community, and generally figure out where the grass is greener 😁🤌🏻 If you've just arrived and don't know anything yet — come. If you've lived here for a long time — come too, maybe you can already help someone with advice. 🔥 What's happening: • campfire on the seashore • tea and conversation • new acquaintances • music and a small jam session • talks about Nha Trang, travel and life abroad 🎸 If you play an instrument — bring it 🫖 You can bring tea, fruit or something for the shared table 🙌 You can come alone and invite friends 🗓 October 4, Sunday 🕕 We start at 18:00, then see how it goes 📍 Cozy Beach, beach in the south of Nha Trang: Meeting point 💚 Participation is free. 📩 Registration via direct message is required: @maxgooooo #date: 04.10

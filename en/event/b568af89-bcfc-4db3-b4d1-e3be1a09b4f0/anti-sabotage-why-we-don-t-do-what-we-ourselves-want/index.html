⚡ Anti-sabotage: why we don't do what we actually want You know what you need to do, but end up on your phone again? You start — and quickly run out of steam? An important task hangs in your head for weeks? It's not always laziness or lack of willpower. Behind the resistance there may be fatigue, unclearness, too big a step, fear of making a mistake, or someone else's "should" At the workshop we'll work through one of your real cases: ✔️ find what exactly is stopping you from starting ✔️ separate lack of energy from resistance to the task ✔️ check whether this "want" is yours or an imposed "should" ✔️ find a working way to get moving ✔️ lock in a concrete step for 24 hours with a buddy 🌚 No esoterics or motivational speeches.

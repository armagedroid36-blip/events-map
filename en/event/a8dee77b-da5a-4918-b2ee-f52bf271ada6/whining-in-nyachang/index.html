WHINING IN NYACHANG Tired of working or looking for housing? Fed up with the heat, rains, motorbikes, visas, money, and the eternal "what's next?"? Friends from Russia think it's paradise here just because there are palm trees and the sea? Let's whine together 🫂 What is Whining? It's a meetup for those who just want to say: "I have no strength, I feel bad" And not hear in response: "I wish I had your problems" Here you can complain about work, relationships, emigration, money, people, weather, yourself, or everything at once. You can talk. You can listen. You can stay silent. The rules are simple: - no unsolicited advice - no trying to fix each other - no saying "but" or "others have it worse" - we listen and support, if there's a request for it Whining is not therapy and not a support group We don't solve problems. We temporarily allow ourselves not to solve them 📍 Cedruss Coffee (North) 🕐 06.09, at 20:00 Come. Sometimes whining matters too 🫂 🎫 Free participation 📲 Sign up: @A_logoped #date: 06.09

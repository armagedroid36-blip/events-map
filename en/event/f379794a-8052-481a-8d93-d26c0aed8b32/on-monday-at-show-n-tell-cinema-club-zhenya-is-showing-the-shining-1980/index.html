🎬 On Monday at Show’n’Tell Cinema Club, Zhenya is screening “The Shining” (1980)! Director Stanley Kubrick tells the story of writer Jack Torrance, who takes a job as winter caretaker at the isolated Overlook Hotel and arrives there with his wife and young son. But the quiet life away from everyone gradually turns into a nightmare: the hotel’s past makes itself felt, and Jack’s behavior becomes increasingly terrifying. A legendary horror classic whose images have long become part of mass culture. Hypnotic corridors, unsettling music, and Jack Nicholson’s unforgettable performance — a film that continues to frighten and spark debate decades later. Whether you’re watching for the first time or have long wanted to revisit it — come! 🏨❄️ 🗣 The film will be shown in its original language with English subtitles, after which we will discuss the film together and share our impressions. 📽 Participation: donation (recommended amount 50k) ⏰ Time: October 26, 19:00 📍 Venue: The Workshop 📫 Address: 35 Che Lan Vien, Da Nang, Vietnam Contact information @infraredfade #date: 26.10

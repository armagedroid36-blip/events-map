🎨 Saturday drawing sessions — a lovely tradition. We gather as "the girls" in a cozy café to relax, draw, and recharge. This Saturday — a free-form session with MAC cards. No preset theme: we'll work with the deck as a projective technique — it helps you hear what's already inside, see current feelings and images. Then each person draws whatever resonates with her. The cards here aren't a prediction, but a mirror and a starting point for creativity. And as always: coffee, conversations, clearing your head, and good vibes in great company. 🍀 I'm Anna Potapova, psychologist, art therapist. @poaniki 🗓 Sat, October 3, 11:00 🖌 Materials: markers, watercolor, pencils, charcoal, A4/A5 paper — I'll bring them.

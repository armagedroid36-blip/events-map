Free yoga class for moms ❤️ 📍North Nha Trang, Chandra yoga studio Sunday, September 13 — 10:15 Together we restore the body and well-being after pregnancy and childbirth — no matter how much time has passed. You don't need to find someone to leave your child with ❤️ About me: I am a certified prenatal and postnatal restorative yoga teacher KSYS, India (2022) and a FYSM style yoga teacher (2026). I create a space where a mom can practice without having to find someone to leave her little one with. After all, yoga is not just about the body.

Free yoga class for moms ❤️ 📍North Nha Trang, Chandra yoga studio Sunday, September 13 — 10:15 Together we restore the body and well-being after pregnancy and childbirth — no matter how much time has passed. In our classes we: — strengthen muscles and develop flexibility; — work with tension and discomfort in the back and neck; — strengthen the abdominal and pelvic floor muscles; — bring back strength, mobility, and a sense of self to the body. 👶 Children 0+ are welcome Little ones can lie, crawl, or walk nearby while mom practices on the mat. You don't need to find someone to leave your child with ❤️ About me: I am a certified prenatal and postnatal restorative yoga teacher KSYS, India (2022) and a FYSM style yoga teacher (2026). I am a mom of a three-year-old son myself and practiced yoga during pregnancy and after childbirth. I create a space where a mom can practice without having to find someone to leave her little one with. After all, yoga is not just about the body. It's also about acceptance, patience, and connecting with life as it is. 🤍 Message me to sign up for a trial class: @irabarabo #date: 13.09

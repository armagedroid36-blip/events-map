Ubud Writers & Readers Festival 2026 literary festival in Ubud

October 21, 2026, 11:59 PM

No.88X, Jl. Raya Sanggingan No.88X, Kedewatan, Kecamatan Ubud, Kabupaten Gianyar, Bali 80571, Индонезия, Убуд, Bali

The Ubud Writers & Readers Festival 2026 will take place in Ubud from October 21-25. This is the 23rd edition of one of Bali's leading literary and cultural festivals: organizers announce over 100 meetings, panels, readings, and conversations with authors. The theme of the 2026 edition is Samarasā: Awareness. Empathy. Action. The program includes keynote discussions, readings, meet-the-author sessions, a school program, and accompanying formats. An early 4-day festival pass with access to the main program is available on the official website; early bird sales are announced until August 18, 2026. The schedule of individual sessions is published on the festival's website. Source: https://www.ubudwritersfestival.com/

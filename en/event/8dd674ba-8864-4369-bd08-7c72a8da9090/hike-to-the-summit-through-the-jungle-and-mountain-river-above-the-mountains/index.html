🏔 Hike to the summit through the jungle and a mountain river • "Vyshe Gor" On Saturday we're going to one of the highest peaks near Nha Trang - Blue Eye, 927 meters. And we won't be going up the usual trail, but through the jungle and along a mountain riverbed. First we make our way through the forest to the river, then a significant part of the ascent goes right along the riverbed: big rocks, water, crossings from bank to bank, and sections where you'll have to use your hands to help yourself. Along the way there will be natural pools where you can get in the water and cool off. The final point is the Blue Eye summit at about 927 meters with a view of Nha Trang, the sea, and the mountains around. This is one of the most difficult routes of "Vyshe Gor" Last time there were 15 of us, and 8 made it to the summit. So here it's better to properly assess your strength in advance - ahead there are about 7 hours of movement and practically a whole day in the mountains. 🟠 Level - above average A route for those who can handle prolonged exertion, ascents, and moving over rocks. If this is your first hike in the mountains, it's better to start with one of our green routes. ⏱ About the route: ascent about 4 hours descent about 3 hours a significant part of the way along the riverbed rocks, water, and jungle we set aside practically the whole day 🗓 September 19, Saturday 🕢 07:45 - meet up 🕗 08:00 - set off on the route 📍 Meeting place: Skazochnyy les / The Fairy Forest Location here 🏍 We leave the bikes next to the start of the route 🎒 What to bring: •sneakers with good grip •sports clothing that dries quickly •water at least 2 liters, better 3-6 •a proper snack •SPF •a small backpack •gloves may come in handy A long-sleeve top will be a plus - in places there's a lot of dense vegetation. Shoes and clothing may get wet. 💚 Participation is free. Registration is mandatory, the number of spots is limited. 📩 If you're ready to complete the route fully and try to reach the summit - write to: @maxgooooo #date: 19.09

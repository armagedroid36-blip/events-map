🎀 LET THERE BE SWAP 🪄✨ As tradition goes, we meet on Sunday at 16:00 — our active, fast, fun exchange format 🤍 SWAP is a warm gathering where we exchange things that no longer bring us joy but could become someone else's favorite. It's about feminine vibes, lightness, new connections and the joy of the process 💛 About that very feeling when you find "your" item and leave a little happier) We bring only what we wouldn't be ashamed to gift to a friend.

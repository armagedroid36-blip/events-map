Friends! ✌️ Tomorrow (Sunday) at Da Nang English Speaking Club — the best place for meeting people and practicing English — we'll be talking about education and learning new things. When was the last time you tried something for the first time? Learned a language, picked up a new skill, or thought: "I want to, but I just can't seem to start"? This evening we'll discuss how to learn with pleasure, where to find motivation, and why you don't have to wait for Monday to start a new hobby. We'll debate whether a university is necessary for success and share what helped us personally. And also — we'll meet, chat in English, and spend a Sunday evening in good company.

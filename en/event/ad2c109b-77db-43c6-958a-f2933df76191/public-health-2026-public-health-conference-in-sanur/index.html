Public Health 2026: Public Health Conference in Sanur

Event has ended — October 21, 2026

October 21, 2026

Jl. Bypass Ngurah Rai No.165, Sanur, Denpasar Selatan, Bali

230 USD

The international Public Health and Well-Being conference will take place on 20–21 October 2026 at the Grand Palace Hotel Sanur. You can attend in person in Bali or online. The presentation topics are health system resilience, maternal and child health, nutrition, infectious diseases, digital medicine, and the impact of climate on health. The programme features Dr Hannah Degge, Dr Zuhrah Taufiqa, and Professor Retneswari Masilamani. Participation is paid. For international attendees without a presentation, an in-person ticket costs 410 USD, and online participation costs 230 USD. Registration and participation options (https://publichealthconferences.com/registration-fee-and-packages/).

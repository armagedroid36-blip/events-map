On September 29, Afro Fusion will take place at The Mesa Bali in Canggu — an Afro-Caribbean night with Afrobeats, Amapiano, Dancehall, and Riddim. Doors open at 20:00 and close at 05:00 the following morning Bali time. Age restriction: 21+. From 20:00 to 22:00, there will be live music on the Roofdeck. From 22:00, the program continues with DJs in the nightclub and lounge. The lineup features Anjaz, Gunk, Goatee, Vebo, Jondry, JB Smooth, and Rhyno; hosts are MC Camel, MC Baggio, and Jafronesia. With pre-registration on the guest list, entry is free strictly until midnight. Capacity is limited; a ticket grants single entry only with no re-entry. A standard ticket costs 250,000 IDR and includes one drink; after midnight, paid entry applies. Paid tickets are non-refundable, and date transfers are not available. Registration and tickets (https://megatix.co.id/events/afro-fusion-at-mesa-bali): select the September 29 session, 20:00–05:00.

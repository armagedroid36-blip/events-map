Cypriot, musical fairy tale With narrator, soprano, oboe, piano "The Weaver Who Wove Songs" is a new Cypriot musical fairy tale by Korina Vasileiou, inspired by the images, traditions and folk wisdom of Cypriot fairy tales and legends. The performance tells the story of a mysterious Weaver, who does not weave clothes and carpets, but songs. Each colorful thread that passes through her loom gives birth to a different song, which travels with the wind throughout Cyprus. One day, however, her loom falls silent. The songs have been lost.

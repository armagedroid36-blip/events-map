🎨 Saturday drawing sessions — a lovely tradition. We gather as "girls" in a cozy café to relax, draw, and recharge. The more we try to control everything, the more tension builds up inside. Anxiety grows, and lightness fades away. This Saturday we'll let go of control. We'll draw with our non-dominant hand: right-handed people — with the left, left-handed — with the right. Our usual hand is our control, while the other is freedom and spontaneity. No skills needed: the less experience, the more interesting the result. Plus: coffee, conversations, clearing your head, and good vibes in great company. 🍀 I'm Anna Potapova, psychologist, art therapist. @poaniki 🗓 Sat, October 17, 11:00 🖌 Materials: markers, watercolor, pencils, charcoal, A4/A5 paper — I'll bring them.

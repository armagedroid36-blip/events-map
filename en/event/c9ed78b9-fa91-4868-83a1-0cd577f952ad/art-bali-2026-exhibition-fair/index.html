Art & Bali 2026 Exhibition-Fair

Event has ended — September 13, 2026

September 13, 2026, 11:59 PM

93CX+6C, Beraban, Kediri, Tabanan Regency, Bali 80351, Indonesia

Art & Bali 2026 will take place on September 11–13 at Nuanu Creative City. The international exhibition-fair will bring together 21 artists, designers, and studios, as well as more than 20 galleries and art spaces. In addition to the exhibition portion, the organizers announce discussions, master classes, performances, film screenings, and a family program. This is a standalone cultural event, for which you can plan a separate trip to Nuanu. The detailed schedule of individual sessions for 2026 is still being formed, so the listing shows the confirmed dates without invented times. Program and tickets: https://www.artandbali.com/

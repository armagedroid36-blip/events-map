Contemporary dance masterclass! For those interested in the body, dance, contemporary art, contact with the body, and a creative community! Dance class, 2 hours of laboratory, where we will work extensively with support, the spine, technique, and somatics. 1. where support is located in our body, 2. how we can adapt the body to find support 3. what we can think about to find support 4. shifting weight with a sense of support Who can come? Those who have never danced And those who have been dancing for more than 10 years too Why? Because we are looking for your movement, in your state, in your body. Choreographer: Rodion Uvarov - choreographer, director of the author's choreography ensemble "ARU", graduate of SOUKI, SGIK, Olga Tsvetkova's New Academy of Contemporary Dance. 🗓️The class will take place on October 16 ⏰12:00-14:00 📍JK Dance & coffee 31 Phạm Cự Lượng, An Hải, Đà Nẵng 🎟️Price: 1500 RUB / 450,000 VND #Date: 16.10

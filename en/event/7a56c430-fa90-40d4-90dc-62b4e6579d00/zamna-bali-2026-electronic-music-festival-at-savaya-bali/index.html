ZAMNA Bali 2026 Electronic Music Festival at Savaya Bali

October 17, 2026, 3:00 PM

Jl. Belimbing Sari, Banjar Tambiyak, Pecatu, Uluwatu, Kabupaten Badung, Bali 80364, Indonesia, Печату (Улувату), Bali

ZAMNA returns to Savaya Bali for a two-day October weekend. The format is designed for adult guests 21+ and combines electronic music, the daytime cliff club atmosphere, and a large-scale festival production. Day one kicks off on October 17 at 3:00 PM, day two on October 18 at 1:00 PM. Savaya notes that different DJs will perform throughout the day, with the lineup building toward the headliners. Tickets Tickets are non-refundable, non-upgradeable, and non-transferable; entry is strictly 21+.

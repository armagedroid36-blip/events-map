Bali Emergency Care Conference 2026 is a professional conference for emergency and urgent care specialists. The program takes place at The Trans Resort Bali in Seminyak and is designed for doctors, nurses, paramedics, and other medical professionals. The program features interactive CPD sessions, e-learning, practical workshops, a welcome event, and a closing event. For BaliForum, this is a niche but significant educational event: it expands the events section beyond parties, wellness, and festivals. Registration is paid: for nurses, paramedics, and trainee doctors, the early rate is A$849, and the regular rate is A$949; for consultants, specialists, and general practitioners, it is A$1,149 and A$1,249, respectively. The early rate is available to the first 100 registrants. Source: https://emsconferences.com.au/events/soak-up-the-sun-in-bali/

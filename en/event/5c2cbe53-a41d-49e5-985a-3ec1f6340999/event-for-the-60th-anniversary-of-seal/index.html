Event has ended — October 23, 2026

Lanitio Theatre, Limassol, Cyprus

The Limassol Reserve Officers Association (SEAL) —the first Reserve Association founded in Cyprus— completes six decades of service and invites the public to its official celebrations for its 60th anniversary. The event will take place in the presence of the President of the House of Representatives, Ms. Annita Demetriou.

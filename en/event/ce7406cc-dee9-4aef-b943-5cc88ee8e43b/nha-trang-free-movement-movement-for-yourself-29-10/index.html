Nha Trang | Free Movement - movement for yourself 29.10 🙌 Spend a lot of time at your laptop? Missing movement, emotional release, and live socializing outside of work and everyday chores? I invite you to Free Movement - a free movement practice where you don't need to know how to dance, memorize choreography, or meet any standards. Here you can switch from habitual thoughts to body sensations, explore movement, loosen up, and gradually find your own way to dance. No competition, no judgments, and no fear of doing something wrong. What will you get from the session? ✨ Physical activity instead of another day at the computer. ✨ A chance to exhale, switch off, and be in contact with yourself. ✨ Freedom to move without having to do everything right. ✨ Live socializing and new acquaintances in Nha Trang. The session is suitable for beginners, even if you've never danced and consider yourself completely uncoordinated.

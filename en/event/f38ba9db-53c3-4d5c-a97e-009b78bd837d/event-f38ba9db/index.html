Olive & Aromas Festival in Evrychou

Event has ended — October 10, 2026

October 10, 2026, 10:00 AM

Evrychou Village Square, Nicosia, Cyprus

A special celebration dedicated to the olive, aromatic plants, and the richness of the Cypriot countryside is being organized by Evrychou. On Saturday, October 10, the village square will host the Olive & Aromatics Festival, offering visitors a full day of flavors, aromas, music, and tradition. From 10:00 in the morning until 22:00 at night, the public will have the opportunity to discover and taste products connected to the olive and aromatic plants, while discovering people and customs that keep the authentic character of the Cypriot countryside alive.

Little Red Riding Hood in Limassol

November 21, 2026, 3:00 PM

Pattihio Municipal Theatre, Limassol, Cyprus

With a new adaptation of the beloved fairy tale that generations and generations grew up with, Skini 018 of the Cyprus Theatre Organisation opens the season. Little Red Riding Hood arrives, in a modern adaptation by Andri Theodorou and with the poetic directorial eye of Fotis Nikolaou, to touch the sensitive chords of young and old spectators. Life's small and big questions, plenty of humour but also a generous dose of imagination, promise a performance that will take us on a journey into a magical environment, where anything can happen.

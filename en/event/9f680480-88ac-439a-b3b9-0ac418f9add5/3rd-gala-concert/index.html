Κινηματοθέατρο Παλλάς, Nicosia, Cyprus

The Solon Michaelides Cultural Foundation presents the 3rd Gala Concert with award-winning young artists who have distinguished themselves in the Panhellenic Music Competitions "Solon Michaelides." Works by great composers such as W.A. Mozart, J. Haydn, I. Tchaikovsky, E. Séjourné, and Solon Michaelides will be performed. On the occasion of the 270th anniversary of Mozart's birth, we will hear a Clarinet Concerto with Eirini Charalampaki.

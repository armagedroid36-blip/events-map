Saturday at the Museum: The Last Queen of Cyprus

Event has ended — October 17, 2026

October 17, 2026, 11:00 AM

CVAR, The Centre of Visual Arts and Research, Nicosia, Cyprus

The educational program "The Last Queen of Cyprus" invites children to discover Aikaterini Kornaro and the history of Cyprus through a different perspective. Our starting point is the paintings of the Museum, through which children observe not only the queen and the central figures, but also all those around them who often go unnoticed. Who are the people appearing in the paintings? What are they doing? How are they dressed? What do their movements, expressions, and the spaces they inhabit reveal to us? Through observation games

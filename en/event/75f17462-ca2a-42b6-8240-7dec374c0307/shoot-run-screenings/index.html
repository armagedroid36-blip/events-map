Event has ended — October 14, 2026

October 14, 2026, 5:30 PM

Rialto Theatre, Limassol, Cyprus

A special screening will showcase the completed short films of Shoot’n’Run 2026, which invited students living in Cyprus with a passion for cinema to team up and create their own short films in just three days: a celebration of their creativity and ingenuity! The programme is implemented by the School of Fine & Applied Arts, Cyprus University of Technology, and the ISFFC.

Pyrgos boutique winery, Κρήτης 9, Limassol, Cyprus

"Learn a craft and leave it, and if you go hungry, take it up" says this proverb that carries the wisdom of folk tradition. It urges us to learn a craft, to develop skills, to acquire a lifelong asset even if we do not need it immediately. If at some point things do not turn out as we had planned, a craft might give us the solution and get us out of a difficult situation. The inhabitants of the villages along the "Heartland of Legends" route were mainly farmers or shepherds, but they also knew a craft. They had to be creative and multi-talented in order to supplement their income

"RESTART Gala Concert"

Event has ended — October 27, 2026

October 27, 2026, 8:00 PM

Rialto Theatre, Limassol, Cyprus

The RESTART Gala Concert is the grand finale of an international music competition bringing together talented performers from different countries. The Gala features only the best vocal and dance performances of the RESTART competition. Entrance fee: €10. Part of the proceeds will be donated to Mikroi Iroes, a foundation supporting children undergoing treatment for cancer and leukemia, as well as their families.

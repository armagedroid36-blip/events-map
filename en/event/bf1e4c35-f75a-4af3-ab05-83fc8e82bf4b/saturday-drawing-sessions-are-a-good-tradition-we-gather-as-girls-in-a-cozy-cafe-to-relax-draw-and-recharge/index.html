🎨 Saturday drawing sessions are a lovely tradition. We gather as "girls" in a cozy café to relax, draw, and recharge. This Saturday — free format. You come with a request: whatever is currently on your mind, bringing you joy, not letting go, or asking to be seen. And I select a technique to match — gently and individually. It could be spontaneous drawing, working with imagery, metaphorical cards, or something else that resonates with you specifically. No program in advance — just a live response to your inner request. And as always: coffee, chatting, clearing your head, and good vibes in great company. 🍀 I'm Anna Potapova, psychologist, art therapist. @poaniki 🗓 Sat, September 12, 11:00 🖌 Materials: markers, watercolor, pencils, charcoal, A4/A5 paper — I'll bring them.

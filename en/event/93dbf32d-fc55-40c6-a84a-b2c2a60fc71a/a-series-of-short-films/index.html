A series of short films.

Event has ended — September 15, 2026

September 15, 2026, 8:00 PM

Jl. Hayam Wuruk No.171, Sumerta Kelod, Denpasar Selatan, Kota Denpasar, Bali 80239, Indonesia

As part of Minikino Film Week 12, an open feedback session for short film creators will be held. 🎬 Four film crews will present rough cuts of their new short films created as part of the Lensa Iklim Bali Short Film Fellowship 2026. Each film, up to 8 minutes long, will be screened, after which the audience will be able to share their impressions and take part in an open discussion with the authors. The project is organized jointly with the Bali Net Zero Emission Coalition and is dedicated to the human, cultural, and social aspects of climate initiatives in Bali. 📅 Tuesday, September 15🕗 20:00📍 Rumah Tanjung Bungkak🎬 Short films · open discussion · feedback session Also on this day at 17:30, the Meet the Industry Guests event will take place here — an introduction to film industry representatives and an opportunity for professional networking. instagram (https://www.instagram.com/minikinofilmweek/)

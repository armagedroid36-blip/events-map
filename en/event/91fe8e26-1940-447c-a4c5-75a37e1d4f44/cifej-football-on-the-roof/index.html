CIFEJ Screenings: "Football On The Roof" (China)

October 14, 2026, 7:00 PM

Agioi Omologites Cultural Workshop, Cyprus

Film Screening: "FOOTBALL ON THE ROOF" The Cultural Workshop of Agioi Omologites and CIFEJ invite you to the screening of the Chinese film "Football on the Roof" directed by Fei Yu. The event is part of the "Cinema Wednesdays 2026" program and is a precursor to the 2nd CIFEJ International Symposium, which will take place in Nicosia on November 16 and 17, 2026, with the participation of experts in children's and youth cinema from Cyprus and abroad.

2nd Nicosia Kids Festival A Hug for the Children of CYFIELD

October 18, 2026, 10:00 AM

Cyprus

After the huge success of the first event, which welcomed more than 6,000 visitors, the Cyfield Nicosia Kids Festival "A Hug for the Children" returns for a second year! With love and dedication to children and society, Cyfield invites families to the 2nd Nicosia Kids Festival, on Sunday, October 18, 2026, from 10:00 to 19:00, at the Old GSP in Nicosia. It is a special celebration that combines joy, entertainment and creativity with kindness, giving and solidarity, creating an unforgettable experience for the whole

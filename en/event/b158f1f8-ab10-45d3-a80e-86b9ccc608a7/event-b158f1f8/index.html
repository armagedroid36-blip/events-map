Palouze and Zivania Festival of Pedoulas

October 25, 2026, 11:00 AM

Pedoulas Village Square, Cyprus

A beautiful celebration dedicated to two beloved elements of Cypriot tradition, palouze and zivania, is being organized on Sunday, October 25, 2026, at the Folklore Museum of Pedoulas. From 11:00 to 17:00, Pedoulas will fill with music, song, dance, and traditional Cypriot flavors, on a day that honors the tradition and the products of the place. Visitors will have the opportunity to enjoy free palouze and zivania, while stalls with traditional products will be operating at the venue. At the same time, there are free available spaces for stalls for those interested in participating

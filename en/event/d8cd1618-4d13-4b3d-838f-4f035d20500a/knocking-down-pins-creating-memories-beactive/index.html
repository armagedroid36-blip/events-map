The Association of Parents and Friends of Children with Special Needs, in cooperation with Alien Bowling at My Mall Limassol, is organising a bowling event aimed at promoting recreation, social interaction and the active participation of people with disabilities in an enjoyable sporting activity. Through bowling, participants will have the opportunity to develop and strengthen skills such as balance, muscle strength, hand-eye coordination and concentration.

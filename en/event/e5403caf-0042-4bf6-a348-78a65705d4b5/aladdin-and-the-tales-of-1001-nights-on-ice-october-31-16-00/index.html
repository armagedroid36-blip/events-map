On October 31 at 16:00, the ice show "Aladdin and the Tales of 1001 Nights" will take place at BLOCK42 in the Nuanu area. Figure skaters and circus artists will present the stories of Scheherazade, Aladdin, Ali Baba, and Sinbad with acrobatics, fire juggling, magic tricks, costumes, and video projections. The doors open one hour before the performance. A separate session of the same program is also scheduled for that day at 19:00; when purchasing, select the desired date, time, and seats. Tickets start from 200,000 IDR and depend on the sector and visitor category. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult; children aged 0–4 need a Kids ticket without a separate seat, and from age 5 — a regular ticket with their own seat.

On September 30, PLAY with Vice takes over The Garden of Mesa in Canggu — a night of hip-hop, R&B, and global hits. The program starts at 20:00 and runs until 05:00 the next morning, Bali time. Entry is 21+. From 20:00 to 22:00, there will be live music on the Roofdeck; from 22:00, DJs will continue the evening in the lounge. Alongside Vice, the lineup features Dyomase, O.N.E, Pablo, and MC Knowledje. Women enjoy free entry all night. Men can get free entry by registering in advance on the guest list strictly before midnight, for the first 150 guests. The guest ticket is valid for one entry only, with no re-entry. A standard ticket costs 250,000 IDR and includes one drink; after midnight, paid entry applies for men. Paid tickets are non-refundable, and no date changes are allowed. Registration and tickets (https://megatix.co.id/events/play-wednesday-mesa): select the September 30 session, 20:00–05:00.

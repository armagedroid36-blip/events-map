Clean Up & Connect: Cleanup in Changgu on October 7

Event has ended — October 7, 2026

October 7, 2026, 7:00 AM

Jalan Canggu Padang Linjong No.7, Canggu, Kec. Kuta Utara, Kabupaten Badung, Bali 80351, Indonesia

135000 IDR

On October 7, from 07:00 to 10:00, Clean Up & Connect will take place in the village of Canggu: participants will clean the area near the rice fields together. The organizers will provide gloves and bags. The exact meeting point near the cleanup site will be sent via WhatsApp after purchasing the mandatory ticket. After the cleanup, participants will head to Lima Bay Bali for drinks, snacks, music, and socializing; the pool is available throughout the day. A General Ticket costs 135,000 IDR and includes cleanup equipment, one drink, snacks, and access to the Lima Bay pool. Bring a reusable water bottle, comfortable clothing, and for the pool — a swimsuit and towel. Ticket and meeting point details: https://megatix.co.id/events/clean-bali-clean-up-connect

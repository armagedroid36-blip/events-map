Ektos Ylis Reloaded in Lefkosia

November 28, 2026, 9:00 PM

Nicosia Municipal Theatre, Cyprus

What would happen if a politician set aside carefully chosen words and spoke with honesty? If, shortly before leaving power, he decided to confront his choices and his responsibility toward the people he represents? This is the starting point of "Ektos Ylis Reloaded," coming to Cyprus with Gerasimos Skiadaresis in the lead role.

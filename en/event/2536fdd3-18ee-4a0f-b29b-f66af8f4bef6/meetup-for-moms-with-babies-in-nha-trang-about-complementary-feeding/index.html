Meetup for moms with babies in Nha Trang "About Complementary Feeding." 8.10 from 10 to 12 00 Who is hosting? Alina Zelenina — breastfeeding and complementary feeding consultant since 2020. Specialist in working with childhood eating disorders. Member of the RULacta association. What will we talk about? • When to introduce complementary feeding (Signs of readiness for complementary feeding) • How to support/develop your baby's food interest; • How to introduce complementary feeding in a new country, climate, and grocery basket; • Organizing the feeding space; • Feeding schedule and list of products up to one year (what, when, and how much); • Commercial baby food in Nha Trang and gadgets (what and where to order); • Safe serving (how and when to transition to pieces), allergens in complementary feeding, first aid rules, list of forbidden products. The meetup will take place in the cozy safe space MAG https://maps.app.goo.gl/mQYVppy9KNFsA6DN8?g_st=ic, located in the My Gia neighborhood.

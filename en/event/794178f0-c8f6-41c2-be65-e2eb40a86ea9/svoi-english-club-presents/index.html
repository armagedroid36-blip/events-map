SVOI ENGLISH CLUB presents This Saturday we're getting together for a movie screening in the original with English subtitles. And, of course, with a valuable discussion afterwards - with native English speakers! We're watching the film "Lion" - about returning home when it seems all leads are lost, and all that remains of the past are just fragmented memories. About the intertwining of fates. About life - so beautiful, and so heartbreakingly, unbearably cruel. Based on a true story 6 Oscar nominations and an amazing (in every sense) atmosphere and story. Suitable for beginners (from level A1). The vocabulary in the film will be simple, but the feelings... Will be complex and multifaceted. And that's what we'll learn to talk about! You'll get the necessary vocabulary and useful phrases, experience communicating with English speakers from different countries, and an incredibly warm and supportive atmosphere.

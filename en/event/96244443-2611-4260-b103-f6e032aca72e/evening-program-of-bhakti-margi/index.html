🕉 Bhakti Marga Evening Program In the Bhakti Marga tradition, chanting the Divine Names is called food for the soul. Just as the body needs regular food, a person needs time to pause, calm the mind and fill the heart. At 17:00 OM Chanting begins, a group practice with the sound of OM, aimed at inner harmonization and cleansing of the space. At 18:00 we sing ancient hymns, mantras and bhajans together about protection, clarity, inner strength, removal of obstacles and love. This is not a concert. You don't need to know Sanskrit or be able to sing. Before each hymn we briefly explain its meaning. You can sing along with everyone or simply listen. Come to exhale, restore yourself and give a little food to your soul. 📅 Saturday, 29.08 ⏰ 17:00 - OM Chanting 18:00 - Evening hymns and mantras 📍 Orchideya Studio 💰 Donation 📝 Registration: @GautamSV #date: 12.09.2026 Post by: Georgy Gautam | Vocals and Mantras

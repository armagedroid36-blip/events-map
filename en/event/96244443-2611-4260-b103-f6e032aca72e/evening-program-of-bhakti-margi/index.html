🕉 Bhakti Marga Evening Program In the Bhakti Marga tradition, chanting the Divine Names is called food for the soul. Just as the body needs regular food, a person needs time to pause, calm the mind and fill the heart. At 17:00 OM Chanting begins, a group practice with the sound of OM, aimed at inner harmonization and cleansing of the space. At 18:00 we sing ancient hymns, mantras and bhajans together about protection, clarity, inner strength, removal of obstacles and love. This is not a concert. You don't need to know Sanskrit or be able to sing. Before each hymn we briefly explain its meaning. You can sing along with everyone or simply listen.

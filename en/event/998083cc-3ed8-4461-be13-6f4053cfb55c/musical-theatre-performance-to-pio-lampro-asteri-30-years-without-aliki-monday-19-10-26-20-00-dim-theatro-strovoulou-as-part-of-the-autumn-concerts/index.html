Strovolos Municipal Theatre, Nicosia, Cyprus

PRESS RELEASE "The Brightest Star: 30 Years Without Aliki" A journey into the light that Aliki Vougiouklaki left burning, through her songs, her words, her images, her smile! The Municipality of Strovolos, as part of its autumn concerts, presents the musical theatre performance "The Brightest Star: 30 Years Without Aliki" and opens the box of memories once again. In this performance, Greek cinema will meet theatre and music for a luminous journey into the magical world of a woman who was not merely a leading lady, but a symbol of joy, light, and dream!

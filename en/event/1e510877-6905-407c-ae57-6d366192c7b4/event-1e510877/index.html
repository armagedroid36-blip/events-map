Stelios Dionysiou and Elena Anagiotou

October 27, 2026, 10:00 PM

Kiklos Music Stage, Limassol, Cyprus

A special musical gathering is hosted by Kyklos, with Stelios Dionysiou and Elena Anagiotou taking the stage together for the first time, on an evening dedicated to good laiko music. The two artists join their voices in a program featuring beloved songs and authentic laiko moments, in a musical experience that promises to take the audience on a journey through timeless melodies.

Kakopetria Village Square, Nicosia, Cyprus

Kakopetria is located at an altitude of 670 m southwest of Lefkosia, the capital of Cyprus. It is a relatively large community with 1300 permanent residents, built at the foothills of Troodos amidst dense vegetation between the banks of the Karkotis and Garyllis rivers. The two rivers meet in the center of the village creating the Klarios river. The village is known for its excellent quality apples while it also produces other fruits and vegetables such as pears, plums, apricots, peaches, cherries, potatoes, vines and tomatoes. Kakopetria is famous for its wall-painted churches

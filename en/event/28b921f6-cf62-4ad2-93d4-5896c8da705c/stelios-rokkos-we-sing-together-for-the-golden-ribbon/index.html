Stelios Rokkos | Singing Together for the Golden Ribbon On September 30, we sing together for the Golden Ribbon. We sing for the children who teach us what strength means. On Wednesday, September 30, 2026, the Latouros Group is organizing a special charity concert at the Pattichio Municipal Amphitheater of Larnaca with Stelios Rokkos, dedicated to the children of the Pediatric Oncology Unit and their families. The concert takes place under the auspices of the President of the Republic of Cyprus, Mr. Nikos Christodoulides, highlighting the importance and social character of the initiative.

🔴🔵How to choose a hobby that brings pleasure and development? When you have a free evening or wonder how to spend a day off with interest and benefit, the tactical board game "TAC" can be an excellent choice. 💠 What is "TAC"? TAC is an intellectual team game in which success depends on strategy, tactics, and interaction between partners. 💠 The main objective of the game: Guide your 4 game balls around the circle and get them into your "home" faster than your opponents. 💠 Goal of the match: The team that gets all 8 balls into the home first becomes the winner. We are glad to see both experienced players and beginners.

English Speaking Club, Ubud Not courses. Not a lesson. Not a lecture. This is a place where English becomes a natural way to communicate. We don't have the usual "teacher — students" format. Instead of a teacher — a facilitator who guides communication, suggests activities and helps everyone join the conversation. Here all participants are on equal footing. Stronger ones help less experienced ones, and those who are just starting quickly get drawn into lively communication without fear of making mistakes. That's why many start speaking much more confidently after just the first meetings. Each meeting is different. 🎲 Modern board games 🃏 Conversation cards and interactive tasks 🎬 Films and series with discussions of real-life English 🎵 Songs, modern colloquial vocabulary and expressions ⚔️ Debates and discussions of interesting topics 📖 Stories, improvisation, describing objects and dozens of other activities Every game and every task is created with one goal — to make you speak as much as possible. 🌍 A truly international environment Foreigners, including native English speakers, regularly attend the meetings, so you practice English not only with Russian-speaking participants, but in a natural international company — just as it happens in everyday life in Bali. Why this format works ✅ Maximum speaking practice ✅ No cramming and boring exercises ✅ Small groups — everyone actively participates ✅ English is used from the first minute of the meeting ✅ The opportunity to communicate with people of different levels and get used to live speech faster ✅ New acquaintances, a friendly atmosphere and a community of people who are interested in growing together It doesn't matter whether you speak just a few phrases or already communicate confidently — the club's format is built so that everyone feels comfortable and gradually reaches a new level. 📍 Ubud 👥 Small groups 🌍 International community 🎲 New activities every week Less theory. More real communication. That's exactly how language becomes

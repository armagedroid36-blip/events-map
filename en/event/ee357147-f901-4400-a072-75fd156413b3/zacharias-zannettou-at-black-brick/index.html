Event has ended — September 25, 2026

Black Brick, Cyprus

Zacharias Zannettou returns dynamically with his new solo show "No Returns Accepted," his second complete personal work in the field of stand-up comedy. With brand-new material, new themes, and his distinctive stage presence, Zacharias focuses on the small and large absurdities of everyday life, human relationships, the Cypriot reality and – as always – himself. Without filters, with self-mockery and sharp humor, he challenges us to laugh at what happens (to us), whether we want to or not. After his successful first solo show

On 17 October, ORIGIN Bali will take place at The Yoga Barn amphitheatre in Ubud — a morning gathering with yoga, meditation and music. Time: 07:00–09:00 WITA. The gathering includes 108 minutes of joint practice. Wayan Marcus will lead the asana practice, Jana Johnson — the meditation, and Shervin Boloorian — the Beauty Prayer sound programme. The gathering will be the Bali part of the international ORIGIN initiative. Proceeds will go to the Bali Re:Green environmental project, which works on land restoration and educating local communities. A regular ticket costs 100,000 IDR. For holders of a Balinese KTP, participation is by donation. You can register and pay at the Yoga Barn reception. Please arrive 15 minutes before the start: latecomers may be refused entry without a refund.

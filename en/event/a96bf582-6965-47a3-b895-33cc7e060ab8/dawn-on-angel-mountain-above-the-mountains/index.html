⛰️ Sunrise on Angel Mountain • "Above the Mountains" On September 30, we're going on our anniversary sunrise hike on Angel Mountain. We meet before dawn, climb to the top, and admire the sun with a view of the sea, mountains, and Nha Trang. The route passes through 3 peaks. The first one is suitable even for those going to the mountains for the first time. Further on it gets more interesting: steeper sections appear, where in some places you need to use your hands to help yourself, but without difficult technical obstacles. 🟢 Route level — green A good option for a first introduction to hiking and "Above the Mountains." ⏱ About the route: about 3 hours we go at a comfortable average pace the route passes through 3 peaks 🗓 September 30, Wednesday 🕓 04:20 — meet at the foot of the mountain 📍 Meeting place: Location near the mountain ℹ️ Important: after the first peak, if desired, you can return along the same trail we leave motorbikes in a paid parking lot After the descent, optionally: 🌊 swim in the sea 🍳 go for breakfast together 🎒 What to bring: •sneakers with good grip •1.5 liters of water •a light snack •clothes as for jogging 💚 Participation is free.

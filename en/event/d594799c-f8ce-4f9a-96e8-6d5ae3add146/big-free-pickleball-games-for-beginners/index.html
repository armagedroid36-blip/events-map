🔥 BIG FREE PICKLEBALL GAMES FOR BEGINNERS Supported by TAXI MAXIM💛 Never played pickleball but always wanted to try? Come on October 17 for free Black Team games! 👥 WHO ARE THESE GAMES FOR? You can come with no experience at all. Our coordinators will explain the rules and help you through your first games. Paddles and balls provided free of charge! 🟢 Already know how to play? For beginner players at DUPR 2.0–2.4 level, Social Games will be held. You will constantly switch partners and opponents, meet new people, and just enjoy the game. Games are held without DUPR tracking, tournament standings, or competition for places. Thanks to general partner Taxi Maxim, participation is completely free.

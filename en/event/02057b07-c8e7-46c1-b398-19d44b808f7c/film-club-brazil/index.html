Sam lives in an absurd world of the future and honestly tries to find his place: works as a low-level clerk, ingratiates himself with his superiors, obeys his mom... Until he meets a girl he saw in his dreams. Can the lovers break the totalitarian system and escape from under the surveillance of "Big Brother"? . Mix Daneliya's "Kin-dza-dza!", Orwell's "1984," and Kafka's novels — and you get a Terry Gilliam film. This tragicomedy became one of the main dystopias in cinema history. Absurd, satirical, ridiculous, profound, frightening, hopeful... More than 40 years have passed since filming, and the passions still haven't subsided. Shall we discuss? .

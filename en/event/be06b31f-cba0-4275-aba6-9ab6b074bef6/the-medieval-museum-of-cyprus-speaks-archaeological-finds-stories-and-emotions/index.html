μεσαιωνικό μουσείο κύπρου, Limassol, Cyprus

Educational and experiential activity for families with primary school children at the Cyprus Medieval Museum / Limassol Castle as part of the celebrations for World Mental Health Day. Limassol Castle opens its doors for the second consecutive year, for a unique family adventure of learning, exploration and play! We invite you to discover the history and exhibits of the Cyprus Medieval Museum through an interactive activity full of riddles and emotions. Families, as a team, will be called upon to use their observation skills, imagination, memory and logic

A Night in Paradise (Winter Tour) in Paphos

January 24, 2027, 8:30 PM

Markideio Municipal Theatre, Paphos, Cyprus

The performance you loved returns dynamically and travels again to all the cities of Cyprus! A Night in Paradise: Loris Loizidis and his company return. Cosmos Wireless presents the divine theatrical comedy A Night in Paradise on a winter tour. The modern theatrical comedy, written and directed by Loris Loizidis, which was presented by Pirasmos Productions in the summer of 2026, returns dynamically to all cities! Premiere November 27, Latsia Municipal Theater, Nicosia. Text and direction: Loris Loizidis Starring: Loris Loizidis, Sofoklis Kask

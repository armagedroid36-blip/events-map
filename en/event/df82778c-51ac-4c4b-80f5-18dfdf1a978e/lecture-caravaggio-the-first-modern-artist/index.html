Lecture "Caravaggio. The First Modern Artist" A Baroque painter, innovator and provocateur whose violent temper played a cruel trick on him. He fell in love, created, killed and went into hiding. He also invented that very contrast of light and darkness that is actively used in cinema. At the lecture we will look at Caravaggio's major works and answer the question of how this rebel became the first modern artist.

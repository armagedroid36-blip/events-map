Teen Scene: Jet skis for teenagers in Sanur on October 11

Event has ended — October 11, 2026

October 11, 2026, 10:00 AM

Pantai Mertasari, Sanur, Sanur Kauh, Denpasar Sel., Kota Denpasar, Bali

257500 IDR

On October 11, from 10:00 to 13:00, Teen Scene Bali is hosting a three-hour meetup in Sanur for teenagers aged 13–17 with jet skiing and inflatable tubing. Participants take turns doing both activities; everyone is promised at least one full jet ski ride. Additional rides depend on the number of participants and water conditions. The meeting point is Genius Cafe Sanur on Kusuma Sari Street. Participation costs 257,500 IDR with the current ticket. Booking is paid in advance; your spot is confirmed after purchase. Tickets: https://megatix.co.id/events/teen-scene-bali-jet-ski-day

Crochet (crochet)

November 7, 2026, 10:00 AM

Mammari Village Square, Nicosia, Cyprus

"Learn a craft and leave it, and if you starve, grab it" says this proverb that carries the wisdom of folk tradition. It urges us to learn a craft, to develop skills, to acquire a lifeline even if we don't need it immediately. If at some point things don't turn out as we had planned, a craft might give us the solution and get us out of a difficult position. The inhabitants of the villages along the "Heartland of Legends" route were mainly farmers or shepherds, but they also knew a craft. They had to be creative and multi-talented in order to supplement their income

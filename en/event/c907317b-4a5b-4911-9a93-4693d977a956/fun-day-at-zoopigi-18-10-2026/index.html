Fun Day at Zoopigi – 18.10.2026

Event has ended — October 18, 2026

October 18, 2026, 11:00 AM

Municipal Park Zoopigi, Koumantareas 5, Zoopigi, Limassol, Cyprus

Free

The Zoopigi Association of Expatriates and Friends is organising the Zoopigi Fun Day, a wonderful day for children and adults alike, featuring activities, food, shopping and a charitable cause. The event will take place on Sunday, 18 October, from 11:00 to 18:00, at the “Xanthos Nicolaou” Community Park. The day’s programme will include children’s activities, food and drinks, as well as stalls offering a variety of products and shopping options, creating a welcoming and enjoyable atmosphere for families, residents, expatriates and friends of the community. A donation point will also be available at the venue, giving visitors the opportunity to make an additional contribution towards the cause. The net proceeds from the Fun Day will be donated towards the restoration of the historic “Zoodochou Pigis” Church, an important landmark for Zoopigi and an integral part of the community’s history and heritage. Combining activities, food, shopping and a charitable purpose, the Zoopigi Fun Day promises to be a special day for everyone while supporting an important cause for the local community.

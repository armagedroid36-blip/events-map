IDO WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP OF LATIN STYLE IDO WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP OF DISCOFOX IDO WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP OF CARIBBEAN DANCES IDO EUROPEAN CHAMPIONSHIP OF LATIN SHOW IDO EUROPEAN CHAMPIONSHIP OF CARIBBEAN SHOW DIAMOND GRAND PRIX The competition will bring together dancers, dance teams, coaches, choreographers, judges and official representatives from different countries. Participants will compete across different age groups and dance disciplines, presenting a high level of technical, artistic and choreographic performance. The programme will include Solo, Duo, Group and Formation categories and will cover Latin, Caribbean, Discofox and Show Dance disciplines. The event will combine high-level competitive sport with professional stage performances and an international dance festival atmosphere. A special part of the programme will be the DIAMOND GRAND PRIX, providing an additional prestigious competitive platform for dancers and presenting the highest level of dance sport in an engaging show format. The event will be professionally organised with an international judging panel, competition management, sound and lighting production, stage design, athlete and official areas, spectator facilities, photography and video production, and dedicated information and media coverage. The championship is designed to welcome participants of different ages, including children, juniors, youth and adults. It will provide athletes with the opportunity to represent their countries and dance schools, gain international competitive experience and interact with dancers and professionals from other countries. The event will also create an international platform for professional exchange between dancers, coaches, choreographers and judges, contributing to the development and promotion of dance sport and dance culture in Cyprus.

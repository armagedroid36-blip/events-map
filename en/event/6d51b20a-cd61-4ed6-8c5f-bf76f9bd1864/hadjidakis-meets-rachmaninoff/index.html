Hadjidakis Meets Rachmaninoff

Event removed from the lineup — November 1, 2026

November 1, 2026, 4:30 PM

Limassol, Cyprus

Rondo Productions Ltd presents Hadjidakis Meets Rachmaninoff, a concert bringing together the music of Manos Hadjidakis and Sergei Rachmaninoff through works for two pianos and chamber music. Pianists Manolis Neophytou, Christos Fountos and Anna Avraamidou are joined by cellist Robertas Grod and violinist Wolfgang Schröder. The programme features Hadjidakis’ The Smile of the Gioconda and The Lady from Six Folk Paintings, arranged for two pianos by Manolis Neophytou. The concert also includes Rachmaninoff’s Trio élégiaque No. 1 in G minor, Suite No. 1 and Suite No. 2. Date: 1 November 2026 Tim

✨Playing Battlestar Galactica✨ ❤️‍🔥Friday 18:00❤️‍🔥 Battlestar Galactica is a semi-cooperative game based on the TV series. Players work together to save the remnants of humanity and guide the fleet to Kobol, but Cylons may be hiding among them. And the most interesting part — you don't always know who is on whose side. The main highlight of the game is paranoia and distrust: 🚀 humanity tries to survive and reach the goal; 🤖 Cylons secretly sabotage the fleet; 🕵️ you can suspect almost any player; 🃏 crises force you to make tough decisions; ⚖️ voting, accusations, and politics can be no less important than battles; 🔥 even if you're sure you've found a traitor, you might be wrong. 📆 September 18 (Friday) at 18:00 📍 Location: NEVERLAND game club (center) 👫 number of spots: 5 💰 No "entry fees."

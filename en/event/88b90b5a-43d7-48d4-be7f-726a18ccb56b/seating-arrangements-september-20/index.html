CONSTELLATIONS // SEPTEMBER 20 Sometimes a single outside perspective truly changes more than years of reflection. We can spend a long time understanding our situation with our head, analyzing, searching for causes, making decisions — and still return again to similar relationships, fears, inner dead ends, and repeating patterns. Constellations help us see what usually remains hidden. On September 20 at 12:00 we meet in Nha Trang for a group practice of systemic constellations. You can come with a request about: — relationships and partnership — mom, dad, and the family of origin — money and self-realization — a difficult choice — repeating patterns — inner conflict — the state of "I understand everything, but nothing changes" — or simply with a question that has been troubling you for a long time You don't have to do your own constellation right away. You can come as a guest, be a representative, get acquainted with the method, and see how the group field works. If you want to guaranteed work through your situation, it's better to book a client spot in advance — the number of full sessions at the meeting is limited. Facilitator — Oleg Shtark psychologist, facilitator of systemic constellations, student of Hellinger, more than 18 years of practice. For me, a constellation is not a magic button. It is a precise tool that helps to see what was previously hard to notice. And the most lasting changes begin when what has been seen becomes part of further inner work. 📍 Nha Trang, Yen Garden Bistro 📅 September 20, Sunday 🕛 12:00 🌿 Participation for guests and representatives — donation To sign up, message @arhangelinaa the word CONSTELLATIONS. Come with your question. We will look not at how things "should be," but at what is actually happening. #date: 20.09

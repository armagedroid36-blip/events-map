Earth... Transformed! Exhibition & Open Studio

Event has ended — October 17, 2026

October 17, 2026, 10:00 AM

New and Reborn by B, Nicosia, Cyprus

Ceramic exhibition and open studio by Maria Byron Panayidou at New and Reborn by B in Mammari. 'Earth... Transformed!' follows the transformation of raw clay into detailed sculptural forms, shown alongside a curated selection of hand-refurbished contemporary furniture. Visitors get a behind-the-scenes look at the active studio process in a welcoming open-air atmosphere with music, drinks and light refreshments through the day. Original ceramic sculptures, custom commemorative pieces and refurbished furniture are available for purchase. Free admission. Facebook event: https://fb.me/e/4qXInInq

On October 10, from 16:30 to 18:00, the Saturday EMPOWER program will take place at the Happiness Foundation — Donation Drop location in Ungasan. Participants join the children in active and team games, play sports, and help create an energetic evening. The organizers will prepare the program; comfortable clothing is recommended for participation. The visitor ticket costs 50,000 IDR. There is an additional donation ticket for 100,000 IDR; local residents can choose a free ticket. Proceeds support the Happiness Foundation's EMPOWER program.

On October 9, from 18:00 to 20:00, Potato Head Beach Club in Seminyak will host an evening of the Archipelago Ensemble series. The program will feature performances by Greybox and Balinese musician Ketut Lanus. The series connects the musical traditions of the Indonesian islands with contemporary sound — from live instruments to electronics.

On October 10, 17, 24, and 31 at 7:30 PM, a Balinese Kecak dance show will take place at Labyrinth Backyard next to Labyrinth Dome in the Nuanu Creative City area. The performance combines traditional dance with Nuanu's modern stage environment. The ticket includes entry to Nuanu. For Indonesian citizens and KITAS holders, an adult ticket costs 220,000 IDR and a child ticket costs 170,000 IDR. For international guests, an adult ticket costs 270,000 IDR and a child ticket costs 220,000 IDR. To choose a date and buy a ticket: https://www.nuanu.com/events/nuanu-kecak-dance

Charity Music Event in Support of Elena Antoniadou

Event has ended — September 23, 2026

September 23, 2026, 5:00 PM

Marios Tokas Municipal Open-Air Theatre in Polemidia, Cyprus

A special evening of music, love and solidarity will take place in support of Elena Antoniadou at the Marios Tokas Municipal Open-Air Theatre in Polemidia. The programme features live performances by Prodromos Tsolias, Nikolas Charalambous, Lefkios Kounounis and Smaragda Diamantidou, accompanied by Charalambos Larkos on piano and Antonis Pafios on wind instruments. The Choir of the Diamantidou Cultural Association will also participate. All proceeds from the event will be donated to support Elena Antoniadou.

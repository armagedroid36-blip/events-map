All of It

Event has ended — September 26, 2026

September 26, 2026, 5:00 PM

Ktirion 53, Cyprus

KTIRION 53 presents the pan-Cypriot premiere of "All of It," a stream-of-consciousness monologue that follows the journey of an unnamed woman from birth to final departure. A torrent of thoughts and memories maps out all the stages of life: childhood, coming of age, sexuality, marriage, motherhood, everyday life, loss, and death. A narrative with humor, honesty, and tenderness about life that passes and slips through our fingers like a grain of sand. Premiere: 20 September, 20:00 Performances: 23, 27, 28 & 30 September 5,

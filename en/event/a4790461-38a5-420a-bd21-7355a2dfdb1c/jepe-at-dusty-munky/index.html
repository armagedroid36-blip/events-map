Jepe at Dusty Munky

Event has ended — October 17, 2026

October 16, 2026, 8:00 PM

Dusty Munky, Limassol, Cyprus

On Friday, 16 October, Portuguese-born, Berlin-based artist JEPE comes to Cyprus for a night of underground electronic music. Formerly one half of John Waynes, JEPE has released music on labels including Diynamic, Get Physical, Compost, Cecille, Exit Strategy, Sum Over Histories and Moodmusic. He is also the founder of Eastern Standard and a mix engineer at Berlin’s Blackhead Studios. Joining him for the night are ION and LUBIMAYA.

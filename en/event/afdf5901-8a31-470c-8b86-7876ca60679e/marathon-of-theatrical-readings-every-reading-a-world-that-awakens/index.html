ΘΟΚ, Κεντρική Σκηνή, Nicosia, Cyprus

The Marathon of Theatrical Readings titled "Every Reading a World That Awakens" aspires to become a living space of meeting and dialogue between new writers, people of the theatre and the audience, giving a platform to new stories, different perspectives and contemporary concerns. Twelve new voices of contemporary theatrical writing, over four evenings dedicated to new Cypriot theatrical work. On October 30, November 13, 20 and December 4, twelve original theatrical texts will be heard for the first time in readings by professional actors of THOC, in curation

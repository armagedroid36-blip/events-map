HOW TO NAVIGATE CRISES AND BECOME STRONGER A discussion between two friends with different approaches to life and problem-solving strategies At the meetup we will discuss: — how to act when everything goes off plan — how to cope with fears and uncertainty — how to use your environment, connections, and other people as support in life — how to not just survive a crisis, but come out of it with new opportunities and greater inner resilience We will unpack our own stories, mistakes, and real crises of recent years. As a result, we'll build a practical mindset that helps you stay resilient and turn any situation into an advantage for yourself. Format: discussion and experience sharing Hosts: Anton Shevlyakov — neuropsychologist, 2+ years helping people solve problems Konstantin Chirkov — expert in social engineering and adult friendship BONUS: participants will receive a free consultation with one of the speakers + a PDF guide with a summary of the meetup Registration is required, only 12 spots.

Rialto Theatre, Limassol, Cyprus

Five performers dance unapologetically, shedding light on the value of dance while testing the limits of the performance condition itself and the system within which it operates. They move, asking: “How much is my dance worth?”, at a time of global crisis, when dancing, and to an extent “investing” in the arts, seems like a paradoxical choice. Their dance on stage is not another “Rite of Spring”, in which one must be sacrificed for the sake of the others, nor another “Dance of Zalongo”, a tragic act of despair and non-choice.

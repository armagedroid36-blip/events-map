Meeting over a cup of coffee with a psychologist.

September 17, 2026, 11:00 AM

Nha Trang

Free

Coffee meetup with a psychologist. Friends, hello! I invite you to a meetup on the topic: "Will infidelity become the norm: old rules in a new reality" Our world is changing. The rules of the game in relationships and marriage are changing too. But the requirement of a partner's fidelity is still stable and unshakable. At the meetup we will discuss: Will it stay this way, or is an epidemic of infidelity ahead? The real reasons for going "astray" Are there guarantees of fidelity What to do if we suspect And if it did happen, how to get through it... ... and much more At the meetup I will share my knowledge and experience, we will go through your cases together and I will answer tough questions. My name is Roman Makeev. I have 20 years in the field of relationship psychology. Everyone is welcome to come. #date: 17.09.2026 Time: 11:00 Location (North) will be provided upon registration. Cost: free (just order a drink at the cafe) Contact for registration @rroman_a

"SHADOWS WITHOUT TABOO" How to love yourself and stop fighting with yourself? Are you familiar with the feeling that something is wrong with you? Do you constantly criticize yourself, compare yourself to others, feel ashamed of your desires, actions, or even feelings? Is it hard for you to accept yourself, especially those sides of you that seem "wrong": anger, envy, selfishness, weakness? But what if it is precisely in these rejected parts of yourself that a huge resource is hidden? I invite you to the meeting "SHADOWS WITHOUT TABOO," where we will: ✅Explore our shadow sides and the reasons they are rejected. ✅Figure out where shame, guilt, and self-rejection come from. ✅Learn to accept those parts of ourselves that we are used to hiding. ✅Find resources where we previously saw flaws. Without judgment, without dividing into "good" and "bad." With interest in your true self.

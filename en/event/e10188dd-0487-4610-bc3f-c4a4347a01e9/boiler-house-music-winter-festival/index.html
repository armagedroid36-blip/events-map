Boiler House: Music Winter Festival

Event removed from the lineup — December 6, 2026

December 4, 2026, 8:00 PM

Ayia Napa, Cyprus

Boiler House celebrates six years with the launch of its first-ever Winter Music Festival, taking place on 4 and 5 December. The new two-day festival is created to bring a major winter music experience to Cyprus, with six international artists joining the milestone celebration. Both days open at 20:00. Early-bird tickets are available at €50 for a two-day pass. Exclusive packages also include two-day festival access and two nights’ accommodation for two people, including breakfast, at selected four- and five-star hotels.

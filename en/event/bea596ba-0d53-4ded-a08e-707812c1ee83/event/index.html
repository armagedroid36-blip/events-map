Μοναστήρι Αγίας Νάπας, Cyprus

The Cultural Academy "Agios Epifanios" presents the artistic tribute "TIS MANAS MAS ICHO," which will take place at the medieval Monastery of Agia Napa, on October 9, 2026, at 19:00 and 21:00 (double performance). Speech, music, and movement compose a unified narrative, following in the footsteps of the women-mothers who passed through the history of the Monastery of Agia Napa. At the center stands the figure of the Mother-Theotokos, a symbol of maternal care and protection.

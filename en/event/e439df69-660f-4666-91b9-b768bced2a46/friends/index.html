Friends! ✌️ This Sunday at Da Nang English Speaking Club — the best place for meeting people and practicing English — we'll be talking about food and how we eat out — from everyday meals to special gastronomic experiences. How do our habits, tastes, and lifestyles influence what and how we eat? Is it just convenience or an important part of culture and communication? We'll discuss home-cooked food and restaurants, street food, favorite cuisines, and memorable dishes. What makes a trip to a restaurant truly great? How do different cultures approach food and shared meals? Come share your favorite dishes, stories, and recommendations — you'll definitely find plenty of ideas for where to go in Da Nang!

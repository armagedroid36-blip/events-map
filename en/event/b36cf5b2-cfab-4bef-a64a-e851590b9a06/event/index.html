Katerina Gogou – My name is Odyssey

October 22, 2026, 9:00 PM

Mikri Arktos, Nicosia, Cyprus

Two evenings dedicated to the rebellious voice of Katerina Gogou come to Mikri Arktos. On Thursday, October 8 and 22, actress Antonia Charalambous and composer Giorgos Kalogirou meet in the music-poetry performance "Katerina Gogou – My Name Is Odyssey," portraying the personality of the actress and poet through her work.

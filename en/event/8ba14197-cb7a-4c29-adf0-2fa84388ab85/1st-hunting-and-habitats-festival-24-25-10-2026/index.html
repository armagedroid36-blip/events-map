Kannavia – Agia Eirini | 24 & 25 October 2026 People • Nature • Tradition • A Shared Future The 1st Hunting and Habitats Festival, taking place on 24 and 25 October 2026 in the beautiful mountain villages of Kannavia and Agia Eirini, is a unique initiative dedicated to nature, hunting traditions, wildlife habitat conservation, and the promotion of Cyprus’s rich cultural heritage. The Festival is organised by the Communities of Kannavia and Agia Eirini, in collaboration with the Hunting Association of Kannavia and Agia Eirini “Ta Laonia” and AEK Kannavion, under the auspices of the Minister of the Interior. At the heart of the Festival lies the vision of promoting harmony between people and the natural environment, raising environmental awareness, and preserving the traditions that connect local communities with their land, history, and cultural identity. Through a diverse programme of educational, cultural, recreational, and interactive activities, the Festival aims to bring together hunters, nature enthusiasts, families, children, and everyone who appreciates the beauty and heritage of the Cypriot countryside. Special emphasis is placed on wildlife protection and habitat conservation, with the participation of the Department of Forests and the Cyprus Federation of Hunting and Wildlife Conservation. Visitors will have the opportunity to learn about biodiversity, the importance of protecting natural ecosystems, and responsible wildlife management. In addition, organised nature walks will invite participants to explore the breathtaking mountain landscapes, discover the natural beauty of the surrounding area, and experience the close connection between the local communities and their environment. Tradition and cultural heritage are equally important elements of the Festival. Through interactive workshops demonstrating traditional halloumi cheese-making, zivania production, and bread baking, as well as exhibitions of local products, traditional music, folk dances, and cultural performances, visitors will experience authentic aspects of Cypriot rural life. A special place in the programme is reserved for children and families, with creative games, educational presentations, a small petting zoo featuring friendly farm animals, and activities designed to inspire younger generations to appreciate nature, tradition, and community spirit. The Festival programme also includes hunting equipment exhibitions, shooting competition award ceremonies, documentary screenings, archery demonstrations, and a wide range of activities combining education with entertainment. Beyond its festive character, the event seeks to make a meaningful contribution to strengthening local communities and promoting the mountain villages of Cyprus. Through the cooperation of local authorities, associations, volunteers, and residents, the Festival highlights the power of collective effort and the importance of preserving the unique identity of rural communities.

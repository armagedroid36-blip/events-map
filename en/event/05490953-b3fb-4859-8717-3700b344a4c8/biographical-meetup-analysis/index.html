💕🌷Biographical Meetup & Analysis🌷💕 Hi! I'm Alevtina, the creator of the women's space "Nezhno-Berezhno." 🤍 I invite you to an intimate women's breakfast at the creative-therapeutic space "Nezhno-Berezhno" — a space in the midst of the hustle and bustle, where you absolutely don't need to be perfect😉 Theme of the meetup: "The Cleaning Lady. The Path of a Single Mother Who Escaped Poverty and Toxic Relationships." Stephanie Land's Journey and the Right to Your Own Dream" Inspired by Stephanie Land's story, we will explore the path of a woman who managed to break free from toxic relationships and absolute poverty. Working as a cleaner for minimum wage to feed her young daughter, she managed not to give up and to hold on to her main dream — becoming a writer. What awaits you in our circle: We'll talk about how to recreate your life from the fragments of past experience. We'll discuss how to find inner support even in the hardest moments. 🗓 When: Friday, October 16, 🕗Starting at 11:30 📍 Coffee shop in the Oceanus area 💸Participation fee — donation ✍️ Sign up via DM: @alevtina_podolenchuk #date: 16.10.2026 Post by: @alevtina_podolenchuk

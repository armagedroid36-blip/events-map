PIAF! The Show – “Hymne à la Môme” @ Pattihio Theatre

January 31, 2027, 6:00 PM

Pattihio Municipal Theatre, Limassol, Cyprus

For the first time in Cyprus, PIAF! The Show – “Hymne à la Môme” brings the music and legacy of Édith Piaf to the stage as part of its international tour. The production has already captivated more than two million spectators worldwide and now returns in a new version combining live music, archival material and modern stage technology. Nathalie Lermitte takes on the role of the legendary French singer, recreating Piaf’s distinctive voice, passion and emotional intensity.

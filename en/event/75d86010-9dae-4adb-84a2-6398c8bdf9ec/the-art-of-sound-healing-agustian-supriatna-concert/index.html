On October 11, The Art of Sound Healing concert with Agustian Supriatna and musicians will take place at The Yoga Barn. Start at 19:30 WITA, end at 21:00. The program combines vocals, Sanskrit mantras, Sufi singing, Javanese and Sundanese lullabies. Instruments include bamboo flutes bánsuri and suling, karinding, tabla, gambus and ngoni strings, as well as singing bowls. Early bird tickets cost 300,000 IDR until October 9; standard — 350,000 IDR. Registration and payment are available online or at The Yoga Barn reception. Children under 10 are not admitted. Participants aged 10–15 must be accompanied by a parent, guardian, or sibling over 18. Arrive 15 minutes early: latecomers may be denied entry without refund. The ticket is personalized and non-transferable. Refunds are possible if requested no later than 24 hours before the start; service fees are non-refundable.

Kubo is a boy storyteller: he plucks the strings, and paper figures come to life, acting out ancient samurai legends. But spirits of the past return for revenge. Only the magical armor of his father, a legendary samurai, can save Kubo. And loyal friends will help him in his search. A stop-motion animated film by Laika studio ("Coraline"). Assembled by hand, frame by frame. Two Oscar nominations and a BAFTA award. A good choice for family viewing. .

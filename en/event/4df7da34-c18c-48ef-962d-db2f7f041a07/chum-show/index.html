Chum Show

September 11, 2026

Vega City Nha Trang, Bai Tien Beach, Vinh Hoa Ward

Chum Show — Bai Tien – Land of Mystery. A show dedicated to the cultural intersection of the Cham, Central Highlands peoples, and Kinh cultures. It features circus and aerial acrobatic acts, live performances with the SEASOUL orchestra. It takes place at Nhà hát Đó (Do Theatre).

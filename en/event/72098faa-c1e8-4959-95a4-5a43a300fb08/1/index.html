1st Sustainable Pedieos Festival… flow of life

October 29, 2026, 12:00 AM

Nicosia, Cyprus

The Municipality of Lakatameia is participating in the "1st Sustainable Pedieos Festival… flow of life", organized by the Office of the Commissioner for the Environment and Animal Welfare! The aim of the Festival is to raise awareness and inform the public about the history, environmental and cultural value of the Pedieos River, and to cultivate a deeper connection between people and the natural environment, contributing to the improvement of the quality of life of residents.

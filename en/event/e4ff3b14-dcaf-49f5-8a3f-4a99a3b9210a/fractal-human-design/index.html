Fractal Human Design. Analysis of consciousness mechanics. Nha Trang | Saturday, September 5 | 16.00-19.00 A meeting for those who want to understand the structure of the Human Design system without mysticism and pretentiousness. We will break down how the algorithms of this mechanic work across different layers of consciousness. What will happen at the meeting: - Breakdown of 6 basic fractalities: conditions and nuances of their manifestation. - Mechanics of layers: how the same program operates at different levels. - Analysis of your individual mechanics: how understanding algorithms leads to an adequate perception of yourself and others. - The theme of acceptance: why knowing your own mechanics removes unnecessary expectations and resistance in life. Who it is for: For those who need logic and a technical understanding of the system. Skeptics and beginners are welcome. Cost: 200k VND.

Schedule available. --- Scroll down for English version 👇 --- The Atalanti Trail is one of the most impressive mountain trails in Cyprus, suitable for those who want to test their endurance on a long-distance walk. The trail took its name from the mythological heroine Atalanti, a famous huntress and runner, protected by the goddess Artemis. The event is organised by the Greek Mountaineering Club of Lefkosia (EOS Lefkosias).

Come to my place

November 8, 2026, 6:30 PM

Artos House, Nicosia, Cyprus

The team Teatro Angelico – Invisible Stage presents for the second year the contemporary play "Come Take My Place" on Sunday, November 8 at 18:30, at Artos House. An original theatrical performance about everything a person may encounter on their path: love, fear, anxiety, dreams, relationships, bullying, loss, choices, empathy, and acceptance.

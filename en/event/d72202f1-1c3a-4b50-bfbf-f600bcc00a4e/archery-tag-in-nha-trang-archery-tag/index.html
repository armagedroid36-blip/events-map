🏹 Archery Tag in Nha Trang: Archery Tag Like paintball, but without bruises and paint: bows with soft arrows and cover on the arena. Your heart pounds just as hard, the adrenaline is real, but there's no pain. You hide behind cover, hear an arrow whistle past, pop out for a split second to shoot, then duck back again. By the end of the round, your hands are shaking from excitement, and your team cheers at every successful hit. No experience needed: we train you in 15 minutes on the spot, gear and instructions included. It's also a great workout: you run, squat, dodge, and after an hour and a half you're as exhausted as after a training session.

On September 29, a group Kundalini Activation session with Alina Avdyukova will take place at ADDA Yoga Canggu. The gathering is scheduled for two hours — from 13:30 to 15:30 local Bali time, WITA. The format includes guided breathing exercises, gentle movements, and time for rest and observing your sensations. This is a spiritual practice that differs from a kundalini yoga class; there is no intense physical workout in the program. The participation fee is 600,000 IDR per person. A registration form for the September 29 session is available to book your spot (https://momence.com/s/132217787?skipPreview=true). Payment is made later on site: the ability to reserve a spot without immediate payment does not mean participation is free. The session takes place at the ADDA Yoga studio in Canggu, on a tropical rooftop space. There are fans, no air conditioning — keep the heat in mind when choosing the format. Sessions with Alina take place on Tuesdays; this listing refers to the September 29 gathering.

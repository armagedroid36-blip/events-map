Goethe-Institut Zypern, Nicosia, Cyprus

The Goethe-Institut Cyprus is organizing the fourth edition of GoetheKino titled "Questions About What Remains." Through this series of screenings and discussions, questions related to the passage of time are explored from different perspectives. Each film serves as a starting point for exchanging views and reflection in a relaxed setting. The discussions feature Olga Kourelou, film academic, as well as experts on the topics of each film, creating a connection to the Cypriot context.

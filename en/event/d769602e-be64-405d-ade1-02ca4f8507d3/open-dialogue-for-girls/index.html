Open dialogue for women "How to understand a man?" ⭐️October 3, Saturday 16:00 A meetup for women who want to better understand men – not abstract ones, but the specific man who is or appears in your life. And they haven't lost hope for it yet 😁 💬 What we'll talk about: ⭐️ how to understand a man's actions and true motives, not just his words; ⭐️ what may be behind his distance, silence, or irritation; ⭐️ why men don't speak directly about what they feel; ⭐️ how to distinguish real signals from your own guesses and interpretations; ⭐️ why the same man can behave completely differently in different relationships. This conversation is NOT about what a woman "should" be, and not a set of universal rules on "how to properly communicate with men." Hosts: A married couple, founders of the community "Svoi v Danange" Yuri Bayandin: • Has been teaching mature communication for 4 years, • Consults businesspeople on choosing a partner, building healthy relationships and family. Ekaterina Melnikova: • Psychologist, art therapist. • Supports people through crises, burnout, divorces, and helps build secure attachment in relationships ⭐️It doesn't matter whether you're in a relationship or not, the meetup will be useful for everyone. ⭐️Men are also welcome, but only to listen, without participating in the discussion. 📅 Saturday 03.10 🕖 Start: 16:00 📍 My An, Là Sangha 💲 Price: 200k 🎁 FREE – for Dating club members ‼️ Mandatory registration — @confidis #date: 03.10

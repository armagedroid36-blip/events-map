Game workshop "Traps on the Path to Happiness" Does it constantly seem that for complete happiness you need to push a little harder, work more, or wait for the "right moment"? Behind this background uncertainty and fatigue, most often hide invisible blocks, prohibitions, and fears that we prefer not to notice. On Monday, we will bypass the mind's defense mechanisms through light game and acting practices, and figure out what actually prevents you from claiming what's yours. What you'll get as a result: 👻 You'll understand your true deficits ⚡️You'll stop draining energy on resistance and see the exact reasons for your stuck points 🪄 You'll leave with a clear head, powerful resource, and a concrete simple action to raise your happiness level 📅 Date: 28/09 Monday ⏰ Time: 11:00 📍 Where: Cafe in the north of Nha Trang 💰 Cost: Donation + you need to purchase a drink at the cafe ✍️ Write to @NataliTsvyak with the code word Happiness #date: 28.09.2026 Post by: Natali Tsvyak ✨ Psychologist-director

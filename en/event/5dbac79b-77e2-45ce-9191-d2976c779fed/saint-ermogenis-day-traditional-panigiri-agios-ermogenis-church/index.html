Saint Ermogenis’ Day – Traditional Panigiri @ Agios Ermogenis Church

Event has ended — October 5, 2026

October 5, 2026, 12:00 PM

Limassol, Cyprus

Saint Ermogenis’ Day is celebrated in the Limassol District with a traditional panigiri, combining religious customs with the atmosphere of traditional Cypriot village life. Local products and traditional sweets are available from stalls set up for the occasion. On the evening before the feast day, a church service takes place, including a procession of the Saint’s icon around the church. Date: 5 October 2026 Venue: Agios Ermogenis Church, Limassol District

Exhibition opening: Sometimes, This is Reality

Event has ended — September 6, 2026

September 6, 2026, 9:00 AM

Le Bajo Bali invites you to the opening of the contemporary light art exhibition *Sometimes, This is Reality* — a project exploring how light affects our perception of space, emotions, and the world around us. The exhibition brings together light installations, sculptures, and photography, creating an immersive audiovisual experience.

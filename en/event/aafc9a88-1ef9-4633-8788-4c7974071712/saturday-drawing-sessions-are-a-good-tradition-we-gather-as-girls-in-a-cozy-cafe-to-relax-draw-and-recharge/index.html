🎨 Saturday drawing sessions — a lovely tradition. We gather as "girls" in a cozy little café to relax, draw, and recharge. This Saturday we're drawing our body image. How do I see myself? What do I like about myself, and what have I gotten used to not noticing or hiding? Through color, lines, and form, we'll look at our bodies not with a critical eye but with a caring and attentive one — and draw the image in which we truly feel good. Plus: coffee, conversations, unclogging the mind, and pure joy in good company. 🍀 I'm Anna Potapova, psychologist and art therapist. @poaniki 🗓 Sat, October 24, 11:00 🖌 Materials: markers, watercolor, pencils, charcoal, A4/A5 paper — I'll bring them.

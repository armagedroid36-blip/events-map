Rialto Theatre, Limassol, Cyprus

From October 10-16, we return to Limassol’s favourite newly reopened square for our annual celebration of short films. Taking inspiration from the restless energy of adolescence, our 16th edition is full of lust for life: a joy that lives within the world, despite it, and perhaps because of it. Shifting in mood and form, moving between stories and ways of seeing, this year’s programme brings together short films from around the world, special screenings and events beyond the screen.

HAPPY HORMONES: HOW TO MANAGE YOUR STATE Women's meetup in Da Nang🩷 Why do we sometimes wake up with energy, wanting to take action, socialize, and make plans? And sometimes — we don't want anything. No strength. We get tired faster. We get irritated more often. Why does our state change? And can we ourselves influence how much energy, pleasure, and sense of resource we have in our lives? At this meetup we will: 💛 break down how hormones are connected to our state 💛 learn what dopamine traps are and why familiar pleasures sometimes stop bringing joy 💛 identify what supports your resourceful state specifically 💛 put together concrete methods that will help influence your mood No magic pills or advice to "just think positively." Theory + practice. Speaker: Valeria Volkova - neuropsychologist, EMDR therapist, international-level PCC ICF coach. 🗓 07.10 (Wednesday), 12:00 📍 Where: ask the organizers 🎟 Participation: donation 📩 Registration: @lera_volkovaaa SELF-DISCOVERY DA NANG🩷 3rd meetup ⚡️Limited spots, registration required #date:07.10

1st Gastronomy and Culture Festival

Event has ended — October 11, 2026

October 11, 2026, 5:00 PM

Town Hall Square (Agios Athanasios), Limassol, Cyprus

Amathounta Municipality is organising its 1st Gastronomy and Culture Festival, inviting the public to a celebration dedicated to traditional cuisine, music and dance. Visitors will have the opportunity to taste a variety of traditional dishes prepared by the “Cooking with Kleio” team, including kokoretsi, stuffed lamb, kavourmas, homemade pies and desserts. At 18:00, chef Theodoris Bouzouras will host a live cooking demonstration, showcasing the preparation of traditional bougatsa and other dishes.

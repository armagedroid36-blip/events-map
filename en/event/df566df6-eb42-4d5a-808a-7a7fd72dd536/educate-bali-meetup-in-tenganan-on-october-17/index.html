Educate Bali meetup in Tenganan on October 17

Event has ended — October 17, 2026

October 17, 2026, 8:00 AM

Tenganan Dauh Tukad, Manggis, Karangasem, Bali

309000 IDR

On October 17 at 08:00, the Educate Bali team will hold a community meeting at SDN 4 Tenganan school in the Manggis district in East Bali. Participants will enjoy group games and interaction with local children. After the main program, you can optionally stay and explore Candidasa together. The ticket costs 309,000 IDR and includes an Educate Bali T-shirt, a lunch box, and drinking water. Bring a reusable water bottle. During the meeting, the organizers will take photos and videos.

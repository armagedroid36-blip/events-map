Art & Bali 2026 will take place on September 11–13 at Nuanu Creative City. The international exhibition-fair will bring together 21 artists, designers, and studios, as well as over 20 galleries and art spaces. In addition to the exhibition segment, the organizers announce discussions, workshops, performances, film screenings, and a family program. This is a standalone cultural event worth planning a separate trip to Nuanu for. The detailed schedule of individual 2026 sessions is still being finalized, so the card lists confirmed dates without invented times. Program and tickets: https://www.artandbali.com/

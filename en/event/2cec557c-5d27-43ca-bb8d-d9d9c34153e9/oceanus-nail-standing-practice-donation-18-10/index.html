Nail standing practice😈 oceanus, donation, 18.10. When: October 18, 08:00 Where: beach in the Oceanus area, Nha Trang This practice is for those who want to learn to work with their attention, feelings, and emotions. For those who are tired of controlling everything, who want to stop the endless flow of thoughts and learn to listen to their body. On the nails, we will explore our reactions, notice habitual ways of coping with tension, and learn to stay in contact with ourselves. Here there is no need to endure pain or prove anything to yourself. It is important not to overcome yourself, but to better understand yourself. The practice is led by Mikhail, founder of the nail standing school "Istochnik." Participation terms: donation - pay what you wish. Limited spots available - only 4. Bring: comfortable clothing and water. Organizer contact: to sign up, message me personally @mikhail_voblago. #date: 18.10

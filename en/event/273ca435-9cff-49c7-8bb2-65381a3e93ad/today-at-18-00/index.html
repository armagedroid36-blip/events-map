TODAY AT 18:00 🎥 37th MEANINGFUL CINEMA EVENING Film screening with discussion 🎬 "Birdman" – won a whopping 4 Oscars. From legendary director Alejandro Iñárritu. This film came out when we were young and unable to fully understand it. We want to rethink it, figure out why it became a cult classic. The story of an actor trying to prove his significance to the world and to himself. About fame and recognition, the fear of being nobody, the conflict between true art and the desire to be loved.

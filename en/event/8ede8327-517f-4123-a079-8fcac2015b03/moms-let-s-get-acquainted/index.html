Moms, let's get acquainted! 💚 Are there any moms of little ones, or actually children of any age, among us who are trying not to lose their minds in motherhood?)) We've come up with an idea to create a community for parents. Not about preachy pedagogy or endless talk about diapers (though that's certainly important 😀). We want to create a space where you can: 🟢 honestly talk about motherhood — from the perspective of evidence-based psychology and our own lived experience 🟢 figure out what's actually happening to us in parenthood 🟢 learn simple self-help techniques 🟢 invite various cool specialists 🟢 discuss relationships, emotions, exhaustion, kids, and ourselves And also just occasionally treat ourselves to little nights out — including with the kids ✨ What will happen at the meeting: ✔️ We'll get to know each other ✔️ We'll break down what happens to a woman's brain and psyche after having a child, why motherhood affects emotions, anxiety, attention, and relationships so deeply ✔️ We'll talk about our own experiences: what turned out harder than expected, what changed in relationships with yourself, your partner, and your child, and what you're especially missing right now ✔️ We'll try a simple self-help technique you can take home and use in everyday life ✔️ At the end, we'll do a small creative project together with the kids You can come alone or with your child! 📅 September 9 | 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM 📖 NHIÊN Bistro and a bit more https://maps.app.goo.gl/TMeyhSEDadRtwWjT9 Participation fee: 100,000 VND per family All art materials are included Hosts: Natalia — CBT therapist, psychologist for moms, and Asya — art educator 💌 Registration: @breezeblock_psy #Date: 09.09

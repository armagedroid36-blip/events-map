Community Kitchen: Cook for People in Need

September 13, 2026, 8:00 AM

Jl. Labuansait No.32, Pecatu, Kec. Kuta Sel., Kabupaten Badung, Bali 80361, Печату (Улувату), Bali

At the paid cultural program Community Kitchen, participants will prepare and pack 200 portions of nasi bungkus for the needy residents of Bukit. Sessions are held on the second and fourth Sunday of the month from 08:00 to 11:00; the format is designed as a paid activity in accordance with Indonesia's immigration regulations.

S.V.E.T. at ETKO

Event has ended — October 10, 2026

October 10, 2026, 3:00 PM

ETKO, Limassol, Cyprus

S.V.E.T returns to Limassol on 10 October with a new symphonic visual show at ETKO’s Organic Stage, combining electronic music, live orchestra, light and visual art. The main performance features KOSTYN x CHERRY x R.A.N alongside a live symphony orchestra, followed by a symphonic performance from The Advocate. DJ sets from Key M and Che Boy complete the lineup. The official afterparty continues at Kesh from 01:00.

PRESS RELEASE The Return of Iphigenia by Yannis Ritsos The Municipality of Strovolos concludes the series of events "Culture in Every Neighborhood" with the music-theatrical performance "The Return of Iphigenia" by Yannis Ritsos on Tuesday 29/9/2026 at 20:30 at the Caves of Akropolis Park. The performance is an original theatrical and musical action inspired by the work of Yannis Ritsos "The Return of Iphigenia." The poetic and philosophical depth of the text is transformed into a vivid sensory experience, where word and sound coexist creatively. The actress Varvara Christofi

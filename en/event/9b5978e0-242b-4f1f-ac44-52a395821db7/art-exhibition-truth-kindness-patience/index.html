Nha Trang

Friends, we invite you to the art exhibition "Truth — Kindness — Tolerance"! 📅 Tuesday, October 20, 17:00 The exhibition will feature reproductions of works by artists from China and Canada, created in classical and realistic styles — from meditative scenes to philosophical canvases. Themes of the exhibition: spiritual improvement, the power of the human spirit, the revival of traditional culture, as well as the confrontation between good and evil. What awaits you: ✨ a guided tour with a story about each work ✨ tea and refreshments ✨ a handmade lotus flower as a gift The exhibition will be of interest to everyone who appreciates art, beauty, and the culture of the East. Admission is free. Write to: @llliiir #date: 20.10.2026 Post by: Sonya

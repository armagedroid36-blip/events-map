Dhrupad concert: Dhani Gundecha and Dnyaneshwar Deshmukh

Event has ended — October 3, 2026

On October 3, Dhani Gundecha and Dnyaneshwar Deshmukh will perform at Usada Bali in Ubud. The Dhrupad & Sound Meditation concert will begin at 19:00 WITA on the second floor of the center. The program features Dhrupad vocals by Dhani Gundecha and pakhawaj rhythms by Dnyaneshwar Deshmukh. The musicians build a dialogue between voice and percussion, leaving space for silence and attentive listening. No prior familiarity with Indian classical music is required. Early bird tickets are on sale — 185,400 IDR; standard rate — 206,000 IDR. Ticket purchase: Dhrupad & Sound Meditation Concert (https://megatix.com.au/events/dhrupad-sound-meditation-concert).

FIELD OF ACTION mastermind for women I love meeting with women when, not yet acquainted, we can create a space for action. Sometimes we have ambitions that we can't share even with those close to us. We're afraid we won't be understood or will be laughed at. Sometimes we get stuck on seemingly simple questions — and somehow just can't move from the spot. There is room for everything here. Whichever area of life your request may be in. We meet once a week to look at what's happening, get support and an outside perspective — and most importantly, take the next step. No ready-made advice or universal recipes. Each woman has her own request, her own pace and her own path. ⌚️First meeting on Tuesday, 15.09 at 9:30 📍Meeting place: a cafe in the North 🫰donation Sign up with me @annushka_privet 4–6 meetings · once a week · small group #date: 15.09 Post by: Anna Fotograf Nha Trang

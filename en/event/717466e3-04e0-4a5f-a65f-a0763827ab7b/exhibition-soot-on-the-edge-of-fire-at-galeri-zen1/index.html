Exhibition "Soot on the Edge of Fire" at Galeri Zen1

Event has ended — October 2, 2026

October 2, 2026, 10:00 AM

Ruko Tuban Plaza, Jl. Bypass Ngurah Rai, Tuban, Kec. Kuta, Kabupaten Badung, Bali 80361, Indonesia

Fire never truly goes out — it only changes form. 🔥 From the flames worshipped by ancient civilizations to the energy stored in batteries and flowing through wires, fire has become an integral part of our daily lives. The exhibition presents works by 21 artists exploring the transformation of fire: from ancestral fire to battery thermal runaway, from political fire to wildfires. The focus is on the trace fire leaves behind: soot. 📅 September 12, 2026 — by invitation only📅 From September 13, 2026 — open to all📍 Galeri Zen1 JakartaJl.

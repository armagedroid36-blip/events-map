Pesta Boneka in Ubud: performances on October 17

Event has ended — October 17, 2026

October 17, 2026

Jl. Cok Rai Pudak No.48, Peliatan, Kecamatan Ubud, Kabupaten Gianyar, Bali 80571, Indonesia

80000 IDR

On 17 October, the second day of the Pesta Boneka #10 puppet theatre festival will take place at Titik Dua Ubud. Taiwan's Yinalang Group will present What Is It That I Am Made Of?, and Bali's Gerhana Carita will perform I BALUAN at the COCA Art Space. Bali's Wayang Sunar will perform MENYANDAR PENDAR in the amphitheatre. Both venues are located at Titik Dua in Peliatan. The one-day pass for 17 October is sold out. A separate ticket for each of the three performances costs 80,000 IDR; a three-day pass for the festival on 16–18 October is 463,500 IDR. Children under six enter free. Tickets for the performances and the three-day pass: https://megatix.co.id/events/holding-hands-pesta-boneka-on-wheels-bali

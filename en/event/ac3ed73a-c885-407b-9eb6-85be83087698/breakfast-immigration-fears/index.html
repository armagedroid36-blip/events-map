☕️ BREAKFAST "FEARS IN IMMIGRATION" Moving to another country is not only new opportunities, but also anxieties that we don't always talk about out loud. ● Fear of being left without money, of not finding your place, of losing your familiar footing. ● Fear of loneliness, of lacking a close circle, and of separation from loved ones. ● The feeling that everyone close to you has stayed far away, and here you have to start life practically from scratch. 🥐 Over breakfast we'll talk about how fears affect our life in emigration, what lies behind anxiety, and how to find inner support in a new reality.

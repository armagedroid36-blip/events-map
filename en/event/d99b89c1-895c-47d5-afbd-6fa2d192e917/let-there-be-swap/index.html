🎀 LET THERE BE A SWAP 🪄✨ As is tradition, we meet on Sunday at 16:00 - our active, fast, fun exchange format 🤍 SWAP is a warm gathering where we exchange things that no longer bring us joy but could become someone else's favorite. Clothes, shoes, accessories, books and more - all with history and mood. It's about feminine vibes, lightness, new acquaintances and the pleasure of the process 💛 About that very feeling when you find "your" item and leave a little happier) We bring only what we wouldn't be ashamed to gift to a friend. We take only what truly brings joy.

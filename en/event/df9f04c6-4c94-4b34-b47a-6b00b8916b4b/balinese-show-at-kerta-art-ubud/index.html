Balinese show at Kerta Art Ubud

Event has ended — September 25, 2026

September 25, 2026, 6:00 PM

Jl. Monkey Forest, Ubud, Kecamatan Ubud, Kabupaten Gianyar, Bali 80571, Indonesia

As part of NIFF 2026, Kerta Art will present an original production, "Legong To Future," created by choreographer Ni Komang Ayu Anantha Putri. The performance brings together traditional Balinese legong dance, contemporary choreography, visual art, and a new musical composition by American composer Paskal Suraj Sayan. The production explores how cultural heritage can evolve and change while preserving its original soul.

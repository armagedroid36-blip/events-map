ZAMNA Bali 2026 electronic music festival at Savaya Bali

Event has ended — October 18, 2026

October 18, 2026, 1:00 PM

Jl. Belimbing Sari, Banjar Tambiyak, Pecatu, Uluwatu, Kabupaten Badung, Bali 80364, Indonesia

ZAMNA returns to Savaya Bali for a two-day October weekend. The format is designed for adult guests 21+ and combines electronic music, the daytime atmosphere of a cliff club, and a large-scale festival production. The first day starts on October 17 at 15:00, the second on October 18 at 13:00. Savaya notes that various DJs will perform throughout the day, with the program building toward the headline artists. Tickets Tickets are non-refundable, non-upgradeable, and non-transferable; entry is strictly 21+.

Cyprus Swim Series is an international series of competitive open water swimming. The event will take place in Pissouri (3rd edition) on the 13th to 15th of November 2026, at Pissouri Beach, just in front of Columbia Beach Resort. Cyprus Swim Series is comprised of a 8km super endurance individual race, a 8km relay race, a 5km endurance race, a 3km challenge race, a 2km fins race, a 1,25km juniors race and a 0,5km kids race. The event attracts people from all around Europe and locals. Junior races are aged from 13 to 17 years old; Kids races are aged from 8 to 12 years old; the other distances are categorized in groups like: 18-29, 30-39, 40-49 and 50+. All distances have different award ceremonies for men and women. 8km individual and relay races have no age categories. 8km relay race can be done in 2 persons of 4km each or 4 person of 2 km each. Pre race gathering is also part of the event, where lines referees and FINA referees explain the race course, talk about safety, fair play and answer questions that participants might have.

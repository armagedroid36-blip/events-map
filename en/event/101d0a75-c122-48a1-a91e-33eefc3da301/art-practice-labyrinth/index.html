Art practice "Labyrinth" Every goal has its own path. And it is rarely a straight one. We may know where we want to arrive, but suddenly we face fear, doubts, inner resistance, the desire to control everything, or, on the contrary, we lose touch with what we truly want. The labyrinth helps to explore this path. Where am I now? Where do I want to arrive? What is stopping me? Where do I turn myself back? At what point do I want to turn away? What do I need to keep moving? In the process, you can feel, choose a direction, experience emotions, and keep going. Sometimes, to arrive at a new place, you first need to walk through your own inner labyrinth.

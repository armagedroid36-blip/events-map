Game workshop "The Power of Attraction" Have you ever experienced giving everything in a relationship or friendship, only to receive coldness and distance in return? Or do you catch yourself smoothing things over again, adjusting, and afraid to say "no" just so you don't lose the person? How do you reach someone's heart instead of standing with an outstretched hand? At the meetup we'll discuss and rehearse various situations to find the balance of significance. You'll leave with lived experience: how to stop betraying yourself for the sake of closeness and become a person whose attention is valued. 📅 Date: Monday 19/10 ⏰ Time: 11:00 📍 Where: Cafe in the north of Nha Trang 💰 Cost: Donation + you need to purchase a drink at the cafe ✍️ To sign up, write to @NataliTsvyak with the code word BALANCE #date: 19.10.2026 Post by: Natali Tsvyak ✨ Psychologist-director

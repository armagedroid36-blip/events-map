On October 15–16, 2026, EDCON 2026 will take place at the Grand Palace Hotel Sanur — an international conference on education, teaching, and learning. Both in-person participation in Bali and an online format are available. The program brings together presentations and discussions on pedagogical research: learning design, student engagement, artificial intelligence in education, and lifelong learning. Among the announced speakers are Professor Voltaire M. Mistades, Dr. Saher Ali, and Dr. Lo Yueh Yea. Participation is paid. For international attendees without a presentation, an in-person ticket costs 410 USD, and online participation costs 230 USD. Registration and participation options (https://educateconference.com/registration-fee-and-packages/).

On September 28, Cocorico — a French party with French Afrobeats, hip-hop, and Shatta — will take place at The Mesa Bali in Canggu. It starts at 20:00 and ends at 05:00 the next morning Bali time. Entry for guests aged 21 and over. From 20:00 to 22:00, there will be live music on the Roofdeck; from 22:00, DJ sets will begin in the nightclub and lounge. The lineup includes Anjaz, Rwin, Dyomase, O.N.E, Pablo, MC Baggio, and MC Meekz. For the first 150 guests with pre-registration, entry is free strictly until midnight. A guest ticket is valid for one entry only; re-entry with it is not allowed. A standard ticket costs 250,000 IDR and includes one drink; after midnight, entry is paid. Paid tickets are non-refundable, and no date transfers are available. Registration and tickets (https://megatix.co.id/events/cocorico24): select the September 28 session, 20:00–05:00.

Performance by EYMELIA - From the golden age of Greek cinema

October 7, 2026, 7:30 PM

Αίθουσα Μελίνα Μερκούρη, Cyprus

Greek cinema showcases the everyday life and emotions of Greek society across the different eras it has gone through. Its "Golden Age" was the time when theaters were packed and film production reached an admirable level. As for the music, it was written with a simplicity but also with very creative inspiration, to such an extent that the film songs have remained beloved and "classic" to this day. The vocal ensemble "EVMELIA" and its conductor promise to let you relive wonderful moments from your youth or even your childhood.

COTERIE SELECTS: Argia at Mason

Event removed from the lineup — December 20, 2026

December 19, 2026, 9:00 PM

Mason Bar, Limassol, Cyprus

COTERIE SELECTS presents ARGIA at Mason on Saturday, 19 December for a night of melodic and house-driven electronic music. One of Spain’s rising electronic talents, ARGIA is known for blending melodic intensity with house influences and a distinctive Madrid sound. Her releases on Kompakt, Bedrock, Higher Ground, Crosstown Rebels and Innervisions have established her presence on the international electronic music scene, with performances at venues including Fabric, Watergate and Pacha Ibiza. She will be joined by Madimiel, who will set the tone for the night.

A contemporary stage show in Da Nang combining dance, circus, music, and impressive stage effects. Inspired by local legends and the natural beauty of the coastal city, especially the mysterious stories surrounding the Son Tra Peninsula. Through creative choreography and emotional storytelling, it recreates a romantic story between a fairy and a fisherman. It combines elements of Vietnamese traditional culture with modern stage technologies: lighting effects and vibrant costumes.

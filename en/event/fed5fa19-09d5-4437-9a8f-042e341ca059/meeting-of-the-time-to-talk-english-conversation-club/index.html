🇬🇧 Time to Talk English Conversation Club Meetup 🇺🇸 ⏰ Time to speak English! The next meetup will focus on the basics of everyday speech — Present Simple and its time markers. We'll be pulling this grammar into live speech — no boring exercises or "open the brackets." We all know perfectly well that he/she/it needs -s. But somehow, in live speech, it's the first one to leave the chat 😁 First hour — lots of talking: first in pairs, then we get the whole group involved and play conversation games 🙀 Second hour — we switch to conversation board games: Codenames / Taboo 🎲 Before the meetup, I'll send out some short materials on the topic — you can review them in advance if you like, so you arrive already warmed up. Organizer: Maria — English teacher with 20 years of experience, degrees from MSLU and MPGU. Level: advanced A1 – B1 Where: Nha Trang center When: 27.09.2026, Sunday Time: 15:00–17:00 Cost: 150k Sign-up and questions: @Maria_Aloha #date: 27.09.2026

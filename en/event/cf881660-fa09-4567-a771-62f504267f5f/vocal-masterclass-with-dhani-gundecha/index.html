Vocal masterclass with Dhani Gundecha

Event has ended — October 15, 2026

October 15, 2026, 9:00 AM

Jl. Penestanan Kelod Jl. Penestanan No.8, Sayan, Kecamatan Ubud, Kabupaten Gianyar, Bali 80571, Indonesia

Dragon Tea Temple invites you to immerse yourself in one of the oldest traditions of Indian classical music. On October 20–22, a three-day Master Vocal Movements Workshop will be held under the guidance of Dhani Gundecha — one of the leading young performers in the dhrupad tradition. Participants will explore the possibilities of breath and voice, resonance, ragas, rhythm, improvisation, and the art of deep listening. And on Saturday, October 24, the musical journey will continue with the concert Sacred Sounds of Dhrupad. Dhani Gundecha and Dnyaneshwar Deshmukh will bring together vocals, pakhawaj playing, rhythm, and silence in an intimate live performance. ✨ Join this special musical journey! 📅 Workshop: October 20–22, from 11:00 to 17:00📅 Concert: October 24, at 19:00📍 Dragon Tea Temple instagram (https://www.instagram.com/usadabali/)

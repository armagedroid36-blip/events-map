🎨 Saturday drawing sessions — a lovely tradition. We gather as "the girls" in a cozy café to relax, draw, and recharge. This Saturday — "Magic of the Sun" ✨ We'll be working with cyanotype — a photographic process where the image develops under sunlight and water. No paints or brushes: just light, paper, and whatever you place on it. It's a good idea to bring beautiful carved leaves, stencils, lace, and if you have any — film negatives. All of these will become the basis for your sun print. ❗️This time registration is required — the number of cyanotype sheets is limited. And as always: coffee, conversations, clearing your head, and good vibes in great company. 🍀 I'm Anna Potapova, psychologist, art therapist. @poaniki 🗓 Sat, October 10, 11:00 🕊 Donation 🗺 Attention: the location is not where it usually is!

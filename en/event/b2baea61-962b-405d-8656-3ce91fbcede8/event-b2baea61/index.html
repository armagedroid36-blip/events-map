With a hug of songs

Event has ended — October 25, 2026

October 25, 2026, 8:00 PM

Rialto Theatre, Limassol, Cyprus

This year’s concert by the ESTIA Cultural Club pays tribute to the great Greek composer Antonis Vardis. The Women’s Choir of Dreams and the Children’s Choir of Dreams will be joined by soloist and conductor Elena Solea, accompanied by a ten-member orchestra conducted by Xenos Papasofroniou. The concert will feature songs by the composer originally performed by some of the greatest Greek performers, such as Giannis Parios, Haris Alexiou, Dimitra Galani, Antonis Remos, Giannis Poulopoulos and others. The concert is being held to support and promote the Cyprus Myeloma Research Foundation K.I.E.

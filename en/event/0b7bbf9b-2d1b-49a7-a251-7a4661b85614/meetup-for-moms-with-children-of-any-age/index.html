Meetup for moms with children of any age Motherhood can take up a lot of space. Sometimes so much that it becomes hard to see the other roles that matter to you: spouse, friend, professional, sexy tigress😉 And just Me - a person who sometimes doesn't want to be anyone at all And unfortunately for our partners, we don't switch between roles at the snap of a finger... The child falls asleep and it's expected that within a minute mom transforms into a lover. Finished work and immediately ready to happily play with the child. Got two hours without the kids - should spend them "quality" time, but she dreams of just lying down and staring at the ceiling At the "Not Just a Mom" meetup we'll talk about identity and our roles: 🟢when one of them sometimes takes up almost all the space and what happens to the others 🟢why you can't switch between roles at the snap of a finger and how to explain this to your partner and loved ones - without feeling like you're rejecting them 🟢what roles make up our "Self," which parts get a lot of space and which have almost disappeared 🟢we'll try to find our own ways of transitioning between roles - not because it's time to be a good wife again, a productive professional, or an interesting person, but because it's important for us to have a choice of who to be right now ➖September 25, 10:00-12:00 ➖NHIÊN Bistro, A bit more ➖Organizer's fee: 150,000 VND You can come alone or with a child - they can draw or play nearby💚 Facilitator: Natalia Gorbunova, psychologist for moms, CBT therapist 💌 To sign up: @breezeblock_psy #date: 25.09

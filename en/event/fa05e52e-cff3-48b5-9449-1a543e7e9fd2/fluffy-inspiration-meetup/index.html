😽A Fluffy Inspiration Meetup😽 How often does your inner voice whisper to you: "This still isn't good enough"? 🤫 Because of this, we hide our talents, are afraid to launch projects, and feel embarrassed to name a price for our work 🥀 I invite you to the fluffiest and warmest meetup of the "Tenderly-Carefully" club! 🤍 Theme: "Inner Critic VS Inner Creator" 🎨✨ And this time we are gathering in a very special place — a cozy cat cafe (Nha Trang, city center)! 🌊🐈 Cat purring is the best natural anti-stress 🐾 🕊️ We will communicate lightly, gently, and with humor, petting our fluffy therapists ☕🧶 How it will go:👇 🤭 We'll laugh at our fears: Humor and self-irony are the best way to turn off the critic in your head and allow yourself to be imperfect. 🌟 We'll show our talents: Each of us will be able to boldly and in a safe environment declare her masterpiece. Read poetry, sing a song, or tell us about your online course! 🎤📖 🌱 We'll talk about sales without stress: We'll break down impostor syndrome and understand how to stop being afraid to sell. We will learn to see money as simple gratitude from people for your work, not a heavy struggle 💸🤝 🍵 Cat therapy and relaxation: We'll drink delicious tea or coffee, pet cats, and chat with those who truly understand you 😻🛋️ The main rule of our meetups: mistakes don't exist, there are only reasons to show tenderness to yourself 🫂🤍 📍Location: A coffee shop in the center of Nha Trang. ⏰Time: 13:00, September 24 (Thursday) 💸Cost of the meetup: (includes: entry to the 2nd floor to the cats, a drink, socks, food for feeding the cats) 300k VND ✍️ Sign up in DMs: @alevtina_podolenchuk #date: 24.09.2026 Post by: @alevtina_podolenchuk

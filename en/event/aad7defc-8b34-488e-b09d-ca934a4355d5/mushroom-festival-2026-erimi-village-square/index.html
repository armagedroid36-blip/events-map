Mushroom Festival 2026 @ Erimi Village Square

Event removed from the lineup — November 5, 2026

November 5, 2026, 9:00 AM

Limassol, Cyprus

Mushroom enthusiasts from across Cyprus are invited to Erimi for a two-day festival dedicated to mushrooms, local flavours and family entertainment. Visitors can discover the world of mycology through talks and expert advice on mushroom growing, picking and identification. The festival will also offer a variety of mushroom dishes, including fried mushrooms, soups, mushroom gyros and pasta. The programme includes games and activities for children, as well as traditional music and dance performances on the main stage. Dates: 5–6 November 2026 Time: From 10:00 Venue: Village Square, Erimi, Limas

Women's meetup in a coffee brunch format🩷 WHY DO WE WANT MORE BUT SETTLE FOR LESS? In money. In relationships. In work. In how we are treated. In what kind of life we allow ourselves. We can want to earn more and stay for years in a job that doesn't suit us. Want relationships with love, intimacy, and respect and stay where we get far less. But why? Why do we clearly understand what we want, but in the moment of choice we choose the familiar, the safe, and... less? At this meetup we'll talk about why we settle for less even when deep down we want something completely different, what psychological mechanisms are behind this, and what stops us from choosing what we truly want. Organizer: Valeria Volkova - neuropsychologist, EMDR therapist, international-level coach PCC ICF. ☕️ This is a live coffee brunch for women, not a lecture: we'll talk, share stories, reflect, support each other, and look at our familiar patterns in a new way. 🗓 30.09 (Wednesday), 12:00 📍 Where: ask the organizers 🎟 Participation: donation 📩 Sign up: @lera_volkovaaa SELF-DISCOVERY IN DANANG 🩷 2nd meetup ⚡️Limited spots, registration required #date: 30.09

Deus Slidetoberfest 2026 Festival

Event has ended — October 25, 2026

October 23, 2026

Jl. Pantai Batu Mejan No.8, Canggu, Kec. Kuta Utara, Kabupaten Badung, Bali 80361

Slidetoberfest is an annual festival of surfing, motorcycles, and music that Deus Ex Machina (https://baliforum.ru/places/deus-ex-machina) hosts in Canggu and on the beaches near Tanah Lot. In 2026 it takes place from October 23 to 25: competitions run during the day, and musicians and DJs play in the evenings. On Friday, October 23, surfers compete at Pererenan Beach (https://baliforum.ru/places/surf-spot-pererenan). Heats start at seven in the morning, with four people in each, and 30 minutes are allotted for the final. From 16:00 the program moves to Deus, where a barbecue is held from 18:30. On Saturday, October 24, on the black sand of Pasut Beach (https://baliforum.ru/places/plyazh-pasut) (Pantai Pasut), about half an hour's drive from Canggu, a Dress Up Drag Race is held — straight-line speed races in which riders compete in costumes. Participants are divided into classes by engine type, while customs — one-off bikes built as a single copy — compete separately. In the evening at Deus, Andre Yoga — the artist who designed this year's festival — opens his exhibition. On Sunday, October 25, at nine in the morning at Pangkung Tibah Beach (Pantai Pangkung Tibah) near Kedungu (https://baliforum.ru/places/plyazh-kedungu), the Swank Rally starts — a sand rally for experienced racers and beginners. At 16:00 the festival closes at Deus: winners are announced, trophies are awarded, and then the Indonesian surf-rock band The Panturas performs. Online registration for participants is already closed. Deus publishes the festival schedule and news on Instagram (https://www.instagram.com/deusexmachina.id/).

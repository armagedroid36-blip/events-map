Free Access Bars® Masterclass September 6 at PRIME If you feel a lot of tension, anxiety, or stress inside, your head is constantly occupied with thoughts, and it's hard to truly relax even during rest — come and get acquainted with Access Bars. This is a body-based practice that uses light touches on points on the head. During a session, the body relaxes, and it becomes calmer and quieter within. At the masterclass, we will talk about the technique and demonstrate on participants how a session goes. You will see the process itself and understand how this practice differs from massage and other relaxation methods.

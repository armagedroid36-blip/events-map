Event has ended — October 9, 2026

Anemona Theatre, Cyprus

The social comedy "Babies Are Brought by the Stork" by Reppa-Papathanasiou is being staged in Nicosia and other cities in Cyprus, from October to November 2026. Tickets available. --- Scroll down for English version 👇 --- Theatro Anemona opens the new theatrical season 2026–2027 with one of the most beloved social comedies of modern Greek dramaturgy. The performance is staged at Theatro Anemona in Latsia, Nicosia, every Friday and Saturday at 20:30 and every Sunday at 19:00, while performances outside Nicosia also take place in Paphos, Larnaca, and Limassol. 🎟

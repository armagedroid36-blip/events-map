STRIVE to Thrive Indonesia will take place on September 10 at The Trans Resort Bali. This one-day conference is designed for owners and managers of villas, short-term rental properties, and companies operating in this industry. The program includes practical sessions on real strategies and challenges in property management, experience sharing among operators, and professional networking. After the main program, the organizers plan an evening social segment.

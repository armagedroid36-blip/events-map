On October 8, from 19:30 to 21:00, a kirtan with Sevadar Babaji and local musicians will take place at The Yoga Barn in Ubud. Guests participate in mantra chanting in a call-and-response format. Entry costs 250,000 IDR; for KITAS holders and Indonesian citizens — 200,000 IDR. Sign-up and payment can be made at The Yoga Barn reception 30 minutes before the start; there is no online booking. At Garden Kafe, a buffet is available separately for 150,000 IDR from 18:00 to 21:30.

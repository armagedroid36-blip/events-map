Memory Workshop in Platanistasa

Event has ended — October 29, 2026

October 29, 2026, 10:00 AM

Platanistasa Village Square, Nicosia, Cyprus

An experiential theater workshop that invites participants to discover, share, and bring to life the personal and collective memories of their community. Through theater games, creative writing exercises, and improvisations, the residents' narratives will be transformed into original theatrical stories, which will be presented openly to the public. With particular emphasis on the participation of individuals over 60 years old, the initiative seeks to preserve valuable testimonies and experiences, strengthening intergenerational communication and the cultural identity of each community.

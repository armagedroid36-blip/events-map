CONSTELLATIONS // SEPTEMBER 27 Sometimes a person understands their situation very well with their head. They understand why relationships unfold the way they do. They understand where the fear comes from. They understand it's time to change jobs, break out of an old pattern, or finally make a decision. But understanding is not enough!! Because part of what governs our lives lies deeper than habitual logic — in relationships with parents, family ties, old adaptations, feelings, and inner decisions that once helped us survive but today already limit us. On September 27 at 12:00 we meet again for an open systemic constellations group in Nha Trang. You can come to the meeting with various requests: — relationships and partnership — mom, dad, and the family of origin — money and self-realization — a difficult choice — recurring patterns — inner conflict — the feeling that "I understand everything, but nothing changes" — or simply with a question that has been troubling you for a long time You don't have to do your own constellation right away. You can come as a guest, be a representative, watch how the field works, get acquainted with the constellation method and with the facilitator. (if you want to work through your own situation, it's better to book a client spot in advance — the number of full sessions at one group is limited) The meeting is facilitated by Oleg Shtark @ShtarkOleg — psychotherapist, systemic constellations facilitator, direct student of Hellinger, over 18 years of private practice. 🧐 Nha Trang, Yen Garden Bistro 🖤 September 27, Sunday 🗝️ 12:00 🍓 Participation for representatives — donation 🐰 To sign up, message @arhangelinaa with the word CONSTELLATIONS. Come with your question. We will look not at how things "should be," but at what is actually happening. #date: 27.09

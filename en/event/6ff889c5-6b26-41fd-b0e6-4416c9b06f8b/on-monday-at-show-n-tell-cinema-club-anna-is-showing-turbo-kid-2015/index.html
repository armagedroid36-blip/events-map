🎬 On Monday at Show’n’Tell Cinema Club, Anna is screening “Turbo Kid” (2015)! Directors François Simard, Anouk Whissell, and Yoann-Karl Whissell tell the story of a young comic book fan surviving in a post-apocalyptic wasteland. Meeting an unusual girl named Apple turns his lonely life into a dangerous adventure — and forces him into a struggle against the brutal ruler of this world. A vibrant, wild, and unexpectedly touching film in which the adventurous spirit of the 80s, friendship, and first love coexist with absurd humor and deliberately gory action. 🚲⚡️ 🗣 The film will be shown in its original language with English subtitles, after which we will discuss the film together and share our impressions. 📽 Participation: donation (recommended amount 50k) ⏰ Time: October 19, 19:00 📍 Venue: The Workshop 📫 Address: 35 Che Lan Vien, Da Nang, Vietnam Contact information @infraredfade #date: 19.10

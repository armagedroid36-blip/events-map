Beirut Shorts x ISFFC

Event has ended — October 11, 2026

October 11, 2026, 5:30 PM

Rialto Theatre, Limassol, Cyprus

Separated by just 245 kilometres, Beirut and Limassol share a proximity that is geographical, cultural and deeply felt. Building on a longstanding relationship between our two festivals, this programme explores the complex political and social fabric of Lebanon, touching on questions and realities that resonate particularly strongly in Cyprus. Three short films and a live film performance open out from intimate experience onto the city itself. Curated by Nicolas Khabbaz. The screenings will be followed by a discussion.

❤️‍🩹We invite you to a therapeutic analysis session: "RETURN YOURSELF TO YOURSELF" Hello dear person! If you are currently stuck in your life, perhaps you are in a crisis, you don't see meaning, you have no energy or strength, things aren't working out in relationships or in self-realization — most likely you have lost connection with yourself, with your DEPTH and YOUR VALUES. ❓WHO IS THIS ANALYSIS FOR: - a feeling of losing yourself, you feel you are not realizing your potential and want to understand your path; - income has decreased or your activity no longer ignites you; - difficulties in relationships or they don't exist at all, but you really want them; - emotional swings: anxiety, depression, burnout - illnesses and allergies and you want to remove them once and for all with the help of PSYCHOSOMATICS - you are in pain right now, feeling empty and unclear ⸻ 💎WHAT WILL HAPPEN AT THE SESSION: This will not be a lecture, but truly live therapeutic work. I will work through real requests from participants using my method — "Meta-Heart Therapy" and "Return Yourself to Yourself" We will examine your request through 4 levels: 🧠 1. LEVEL OF THINKING We look at what beliefs and attitudes govern your life. Where you live by someone else's rules, go against yourself, or have lost understanding of what you truly want. 🌿 2. EMOTIONS AND BODY We search for accumulated fear, anxiety, anger, resentment, tension and other reactions that continue to influence your life. Through the body and feelings, we will restore contact with yourself and release what is no longer needed. ❤️ 3. FIELD OF THE HEART We look at where self-love is lost, where you don't accept your vulnerability, reject a part of yourself, or are afraid to show up. We return warmth, acceptance and connection with your depth. ✨ 4. SPIRITUAL-ENERGETIC LEVEL We look at where connection with the Source, God and your depth is lost. We will learn to expand beyond the familiar state, to feel more freedom, lightness, inner space and a living connection with the Source. ⸻ Often we try to change life by changing only external circumstances: relationships, work, environment. But at the same time we continue to carry fears and limitations inside

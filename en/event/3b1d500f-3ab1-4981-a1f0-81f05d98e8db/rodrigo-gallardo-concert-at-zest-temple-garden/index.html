On 31 October, Chilean musician Rodrigo Gallardo will perform at Zest Temple Garden in Ubud. The concert starts at 19:00 WITA. The live program blends Andean music and electronics: charango, siku flute, guitar and vocals sound together with electronic rhythms and bass. This is a standalone concert of Rodrigo Gallardo's tour program in Bali. An early ticket for the show at Zest costs 450,000 IDR and is on sale until 17 October. Pre-sale at 650,000 IDR opens on 30 October; a standard ticket at 850,000 IDR — on 31 October. There is a combined ticket for two concerts: 31 October at Zest and 4 November at Labyrinth Dome in Nuanu. The early combined rate is 750,000 IDR until 17 October; the next one is 1,000,000 IDR from 17 October.

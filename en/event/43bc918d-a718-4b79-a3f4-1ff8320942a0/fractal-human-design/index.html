Human Design Fractal. Analysis of the mechanics of consciousness. Nha Trang | Wednesday, September 30 | 11.00-14.00 A meetup for those who want to explore fractal Human Design without mysticism or pathos. We'll break down how the algorithms of this mechanics work across different layers of consciousness. What to expect at the meetup: - Breakdown of the 6 base fractals: conditions and nuances of their manifestation. - Mechanics of layers: how the same program operates on different levels. - Analysis of your individual mechanics: how understanding the algorithms can lead to an adequate perception of yourself and others. - The theme of acceptance: why knowing your mechanics removes unnecessary expectations and resistance in life. Who it's for: For those who need logic and a technical understanding of the system. Skeptics and beginners are welcome. Cost: 200k VND.

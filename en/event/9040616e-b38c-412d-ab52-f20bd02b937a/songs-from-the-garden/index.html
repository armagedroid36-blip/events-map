Axiothea Mansion, Nicosia, Cyprus

In the multimedia music performance Songs from the Garden, four Cypriot creators with different artistic backgrounds meet on stage to compose a shared world of sound, image, and narration, weaving original songs, polyphonic singing, and contemporary puppetry into a unified stage composition. At the heart of the performance is the figure of a female writer, embodied through a puppet, who is trying to complete her book.

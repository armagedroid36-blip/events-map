Game workshop "How Not to Sell Yourself for Three Cents?" We often sacrifice our own interests for work, relationships, friendship... and don't even notice it — until suddenly a wave of hopelessness hits and it feels all rotten and bleak inside. Enough betraying yourself! On Monday morning, we'll take stock of losing deals and play with different scenarios — how to get what you want without compromising yourself. You'll leave the meeting with a formula for the ideal deal for you and a recipe for boosting your self-worth. You'll learn to spot red flags so you don't step on the same rake twice.

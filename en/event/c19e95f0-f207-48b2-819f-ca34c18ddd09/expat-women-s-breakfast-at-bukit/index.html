Expat women's breakfasts on Bukit 🫶🏼 We are expat women who have chosen to live and build our lives in Bali. Over a delicious breakfast, we come together to share experiences, support, and inspiration. Besides heartfelt conversations, we host business games, talks by guest speakers, and formats that help you open up and level up yourself. 💌 We invite you into a circle where you can be yourself, make new connections, and find support among those living a similar path). When: Every Thursday Time: 10:00 🕙 Where: Bukit The meetups are free, but spots are limited! To sign up, write to @juli_heal on Telegram ✨

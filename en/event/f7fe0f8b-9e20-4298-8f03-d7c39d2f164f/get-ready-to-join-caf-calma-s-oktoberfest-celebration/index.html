Get ready to join Café Calma’s Oktoberfest celebration

Event has ended — October 5, 2026

Unleash the Bavarian spirit! For Saturday, October 4 th and Sunday, October 5 th , Café Calma invites you to join in on the Oktoberfest festivities as they bid farewell to this year’s legendary beer celebration in style. Café Calma will be adorned with vibrant decorations inspired by the iconic Erdinger Oktoberfest, with every detail designed to immerse guests in the festive spirit.

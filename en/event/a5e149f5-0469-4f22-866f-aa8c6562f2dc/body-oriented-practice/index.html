BODY-ORIENTED PRACTICE I invite you, friends, to an evening of body-oriented practice, where your own body will be the main guide. Immerse yourself in the depth of your sensations and return to your true self. Who is this for? For those who are tired of thoughts and want to shift attention from the head to the body. For couples and singles who want to deepen trust and discover new facets of intimacy. For everyone who wants to release muscle tension and restore inner resources. What awaits you? Practices for sensing yourself and your partner: through gentle touch, deep interaction, and conscious breathing. Eye gazing: learning sincere presence and deep feeling without words. Free dance: letting go of control and giving the body freedom of movement. Daoist massage practice by the elements. No esotericism or mysticism. Only bodily experience.

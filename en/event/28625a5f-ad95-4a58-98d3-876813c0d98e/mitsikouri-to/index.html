MITSIKOURI – THE CHILDREN'S FESTIVAL TRAVELS TO LIMASSOL!

Event has ended — October 18, 2026

October 10, 2026, 5:00 PM

Limassol, Cyprus

Three international productions from Italy, Luxembourg, and France are presented this year in Limassol by the International Festival of Performing Arts for Children and Young People Mitsikouri. From October 10 to 18, children and families will have the opportunity to experience three different proposals of contemporary performing arts: "Archipelago" by Teatro Telaio, "Coperta" by Rotondes, and "Souvenirs" by Claire & Antho. "ARCHIPELAGO" - Teatro Telaio, Italy Saturday, October 10 | 17:00 & 19:00 Papadakis Warehouses, Limassol For children 7+ "Archipelago" is an interactive installation

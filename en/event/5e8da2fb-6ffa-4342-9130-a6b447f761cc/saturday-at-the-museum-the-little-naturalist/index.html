Saturday at the Museum: The Little Naturalist

Event has ended — October 24, 2026

October 24, 2026, 11:00 AM

CVAR, The Centre of Visual Arts and Research, Nicosia, Cyprus

The educational program aims to introduce children to the flora and fauna of Cyprus through the works of travelers who visited the island from the 18th to the 20th century. Using paintings from the Museum, young naturalists discover the nature of Cyprus, observe the trees, animals, and birds depicted in the works, and explore how the artists captured the island's natural wealth.

WHINING IN NYACHANG Tired of working or looking for housing? Fed up with the rain, bikes, visas, money and uncertainty? Friends from Russia think you're in paradise just because there are palms and sea here? Let's whine together 🫂 What is Whining? It's a meetup for those who just want to say: "I have no strength left, I feel awful" And not hear in response: "I'd love to have your problems" Here you can complain about work, relationships, emigration, money, people, weather, yourself, or just about everything at once. You can talk. You can listen. You can stay silent The rules are simple: - no unsolicited advice - we don't try to fix anyone - no criticism - support only upon request Whining is not therapy and not a mutual help group We don't solve problems. We temporarily allow ourselves not to solve them And yes - there's often laughter here ☺️ When you complain out loud and see that you're not the only one - it gets easier.

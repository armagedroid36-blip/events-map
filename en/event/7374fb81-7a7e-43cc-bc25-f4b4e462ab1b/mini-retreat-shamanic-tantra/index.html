SHAMANIC TANTRA RECLAIM YOUR POWER September 20 · on the eve of the autumn equinox It seems like a lot is happening, but there is little joy. The head is busy with plans, old conversations continue inside, the body is always slightly tense. Even your own victories fly by: it worked out and immediately the next task. You want to feel pleasure again, to desire, to create, but attention is occupied with everything except yourself. I invite you to Shamanic Tantra - a deep practice to release accumulated tension, awaken sensuality and feel what you truly want to spend your life on. On the eve of the autumn equinox, we will gather our harvest: acknowledge what has already worked out, what we have learned, what we have been through. We will give ourselves time to enjoy it and choose what we take with us further. What is in the program ✦ Energy activation. We will start with a dynamic practice: we will go through the entire chakra system with the intention to activate and balance their work, awaken the body and prepare for immersion. ✦ Shamanic breathing. A journey into the depths of your own consciousness and subconscious. Breathing with throat sound, addressing unspoken feelings, inner limitations and habitual attitudes. An opportunity to give expression to what has been held back for a long time and feel more freedom in self-expression. ✦ Shamanic tantra and a journey to your lineage for gifts. A meditative immersion through images and sensations for support, strength and qualities that you want to reveal in yourself. We will turn to our sensuality, the ability to receive and enjoy. We will claim our victories and determine where we will direct energy and attention next. This meeting is for you if you want to: • let go of at least part of what is constantly spinning inside; • express feelings and desires more freely; • bring back pleasure from your own body and life; • stop devaluing yourself and your results; • feel what you want right now. For men and women. No partner interaction. No practice experience needed. September 20 | 12.00-15.00 📍 U

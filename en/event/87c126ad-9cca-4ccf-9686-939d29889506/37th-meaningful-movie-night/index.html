🎥 The 37th MEANINGFUL MOVIE NIGHT with a film discussion 🎬 "Birdman" – took a whopping 4 Oscars. From the legendary director Alejandro Iñárritu. This film came out when we were young and unable to fully understand it. We want to rethink it, to figure out why it became a cult classic. The story of an actor trying to prove his significance to the world and to himself. About fame and recognition, the fear of being nobody, the conflict between true art and the desire to be loved. And about how easy it is to lose yourself while trying to become someone in the eyes of others. 🗣🎬 After the screening, we'll discuss the film together – we'll talk about symbols, images, and hidden meanings, look at what psychological processes stand behind the characters' actions, and try to understand why this particular story resonates with each person differently. ❗️P.s. if you just want to watch the film in Russian dubbing on the big screen, with good picture, sound, and comfortable soft chairs, you can skip the discussion. 📅 Saturday 24.10 🕖 Start: 18:00 📍 Venue: Tổ càphê 💰 Price: 200k ✉️ Registration - @breezeblock_psy #date: 24.10

Alix Aymé Exhibition at the Pasifika Museum

October 18, 2026, 9:00 AM

Complex Bali Tourism Development Corporation (BTDC) Area Block P, Benoa, South Kuta, Badung Regency, Bali 80361, Indonesia

Meet Alix Aymé — one of the artists whose works are featured in the exhibition "Women Artists of Asia-Pacific: Nostalgi = Transendensi" at the Museum Pasifika. A French artist whose artistic development was largely shaped by the years she spent in Asia. Her works became part of a broader artistic dialogue between Europe and Asia. Alix Aymé found inspiration in the landscapes, cultures, and everyday life she encountered during her travels. The Museum Pasifika presents one of her works — "Les Jeunes Filles," painted in 1930. Discover the art of Alix Aymé at the exhibition "Women Artists of Asia-Pacific: Nostalgi = Transendensi." 📍 Museum Pasifika, Nusa Dua, Bali📅 October 9 — December 9, 2026 instagram (https://www.instagram.com/museumpasifika/)

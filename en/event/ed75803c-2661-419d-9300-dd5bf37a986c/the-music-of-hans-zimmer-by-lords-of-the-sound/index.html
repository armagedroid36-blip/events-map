Experience the iconic music of Hans Zimmer performed live by Lords of the Sound in a spectacular symphonic show. The programme features music from some of Hollywood’s most famous films, including Dune, Interstellar, The Dark Knight, Gladiator, Inception, Pirates of the Caribbean and many more.

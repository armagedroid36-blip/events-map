Friends! ✌️ This Sunday at Da Nang English Speaking Club — the best place to meet people and practice English — we'll be talking about games — not just video games, but all kinds of games in general: board games, card games, sports, mobile games, games from childhood, and much more. Why do people love playing so much? Are games just entertainment, or can they teach us something? We'll talk about competition, teamwork, nostalgia, strategy, and the role games play in our lives today. Come share your favorite games, funny stories, childhood memories, and thoughts on whether games bring people together — or sometimes drive them crazy.

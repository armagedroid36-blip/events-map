Event removed from the lineup — February 25, 2027

February 25, 2027, 12:00 AM

Nicosia, Cyprus

The Municipality of Nicosia in collaboration with NiMAC [Nicosia Municipal Arts Centre, Associated with the Pierides Foundation], present the Nicosia Pop Up 2020, which is to be held between 25 February and 14 March 2021 at Theatro Polis OPAP. Taking into account the current epidemiological picture and the relevant protocols, the proposals selected this year concern the Performing and the Visual Arts.

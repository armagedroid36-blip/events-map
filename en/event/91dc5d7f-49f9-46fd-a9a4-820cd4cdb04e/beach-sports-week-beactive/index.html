Beach Sports Week – #BeActive

Event has ended — September 27, 2026

September 27, 2026, 3:00 PM

Limassol Municipality Beach Sports Centre, Cyprus

Limassol Municipality is taking part in the European Week of Sport #BeActive, inviting everyone to discover and enjoy beach sports free of charge at the Municipality’s Beach Sports Centre. Throughout the week, the courts will be available for free bookings, alongside friendly mini-tournaments, DJ music and recreational activities. The programme will culminate on Sunday with a special celebration of sport and entertainment for all ages. The initiative aims to encourage residents to try beach sports, stay active and embrace a healthy lifestyle. When: 27 September 2026 Location: Limassol Muni

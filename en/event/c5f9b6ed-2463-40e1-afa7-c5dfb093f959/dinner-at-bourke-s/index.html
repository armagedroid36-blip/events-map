Dinner at Bourke's

Event has ended — September 15, 2026

September 15, 2026, 5:00 PM

84RH+RM2, Canggu, Kuta Utara, Badung Regency, Bali, Indonesia

Bali has been waiting for this collaboration all year! 🇮🇩 On September 15 and 16, YUKI Bali chefs — Kadek Bajil and Komang Mertawan — will host two exclusive guest dinners at Bourke's. Since 2021, YUKI has been actively growing in Bali, offering a modern take on traditional Japanese izakaya cuisine — classic techniques here are combined with fresh local produce and Balinese flavors. Special attention is paid to fresh and sustainably sourced ingredients. The YUKI team works with local fishermen to serve seafood straight from the boat. The special menu for the evening features YUKI's signature dishes:🍣 Tuna Tartare🌮 Nori Ahi Taco🦪 Hokkaido Scallop🥪 Steak Sando🐟 Pan Seared Sea Bassand other dishes. Island-inspired cocktails will be crafted by Aled Burt, and Bourke's main drinks menu will also be available all evening. 🍸🌴 📅 September 15–16🕔 From 17:00📍 Bourke's🎟️ Walk-in only, no reservations The number of servings is limited, so it's best to come early! instagram (https://www.instagram.com/bourkes.hk/)

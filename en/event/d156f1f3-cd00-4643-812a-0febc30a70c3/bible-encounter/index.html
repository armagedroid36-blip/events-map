BIBLE MEETING 🤍 Have you ever had the experience of wanting to hear God but not knowing how to recognize His voice? And if you did hear it — how do you stay faithful to what He revealed to you? There is no need to know all the answers in advance — what matters is the desire to reflect, listen, and share your thoughts.

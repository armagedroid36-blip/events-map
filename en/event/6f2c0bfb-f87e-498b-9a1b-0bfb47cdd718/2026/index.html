Light in Life 2026

Event has ended — September 26, 2026

September 26, 2026, 3:00 PM

Eleftheria Square, Cyprus

The Pancyprian Anti-Leukemia Association "ZOE", within the framework of World Leukemia Awareness Month, is organizing a march in Nicosia on Saturday, September 26, 2026, with the slogan "LIGHT TO LIFE". The march will start at 6:00 p.m. from Eleftheria Square, will follow a route through Ledras and Onasagorou streets, and will end again at Eleftheria Square. Upon completion of the march, the symbolic lighting of the "Candles of ZOE" will take place, as a message of support and hope for the patients who are fighting their own battle, and also in memory of those who have passed away from us.

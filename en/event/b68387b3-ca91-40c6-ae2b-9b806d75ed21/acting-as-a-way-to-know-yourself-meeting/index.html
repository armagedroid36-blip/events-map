🎭 ACTING AS A WAY TO KNOW YOURSELF - MEETUP Acting is not just about the stage, roles, and the ability to portray emotions. It is also a way to better understand yourself, your feelings, your reactions, and the people around you. On October 8, I invite you to a live interactive meetup where we will talk about theater and acting through questions, discussion, and personal experience. What will we talk about? 🎭 What does acting actually give you and what is it for? 💭 What myths exist about acting and which of them are true? 🪞 Why can acting become a tool for self-discovery? ❤️ How to work with your emotions and learn to express them? 💬 Can acting practices help you become more free and confident? ❓ And, of course, we will discuss your questions about acting and theater. This meetup is suitable for those who are interested in theater, want to try acting, or simply want to better understand themselves. 📅 October 8 ⏰ 16:00–18:00 📍 prime.space.nhatrang 💰 Participation — no matter how much, what matters is your participation ❤️ ☕️ Before or during the meetup, you can order a drink at Lulu cafe on the first floor — a great reason to come a bit earlier, grab a coffee or something tasty, and get in the mood for our conversation. Come not only to watch theater, but also to look at yourself through it. 🎭 💌 Sign up: @W1kot I will be waiting for you at the conversation! #date 8.10

WHINING IN NYACHANG Tired of working or looking for housing? Fed up with the heat, rain, motorbikes, visas, money, and the eternal "what's next?" Friends from Russia think this is paradise just because there are palm trees and sea? Let's whine together 🫂 What is Whining? It's a meetup for those who just want to say: "I have no strength left, I feel bad" And not hear in response: "I'd like to have your problems" Here you can complain about work, relationships, emigration, money, people, weather, yourself, or just about everything at once. You can talk. You can listen. You can stay silent. The rules are simple: - no unsolicited advice - we don't try to fix each other - we don't say "you should..." or "others have it worse" - we listen and support, if there's a request Whining is not therapy and not a mutual aid group We don't solve problems. We temporarily allow ourselves not to solve them 📍 Cedruss Coffee (North) 🕐 13.09, at 18.00 Come. Sometimes whining is important too 🫂 🎫 Participation is free 📲 Sign up: @A_logoped #date: 13.09

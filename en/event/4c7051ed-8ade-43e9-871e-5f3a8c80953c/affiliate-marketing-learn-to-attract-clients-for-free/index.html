Affiliate Marketing: Learn to Attract Clients for Free A meetup where I'll show you how to get clients through partners, without an ad budget or cold calls. Here's an example of how it works. You're a psychologist. You make arrangements with a coach, a fitness trainer, and a massage therapist: they recommend you to their clients, you pay them a percentage. The result: warm clients, payment only after the fact. Sounds simple, right? But it barely works for anyone. Partners agree, but don't bring in clients. At the meetup we'll break down why that happens and how to do it differently: - what to give a partner so it's easy for them to recommend you - how to track who referred whom, and not abandon it all after a month - how many partners you need and what a client ultimately costs you And most importantly, we'll build an affiliate scheme for your business right at the meetup. Free, because I'm kind. #date: 22.09 Tuesday 13.00 Where: Mix Greek (center) Who to message: @Thukydides

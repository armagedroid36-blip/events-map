💃 Argentine Tango / The Art of Interaction in a Couple🕺 In this class we will learn to: ✨ hear and feel the partner; ✨ trust and take responsibility; ✨ lead and follow without words; ✨ create comfortable interaction where both feel good; ✨ move naturally, beautifully, and confidently.

On October 15, a meetup with Lina Bystrova — head of Sunrise Visa and Sunrise Business — will take place at the ASH restaurant in the Nuanu area. It will focus on doing business and long-term living in Bali. The program includes company registration, authorized capital, taxes and reporting for PT PMA, investor and long-stay visas, as well as immigration inspections. After the presentation, attendees will be able to ask the speaker questions; participants will receive a checklist for company verification. Guest check-in is at 15:30, the event starts at 16:00. Tickets cost 100,000 IDR and include entry to Nuanu. Food and drinks at the restaurant are paid separately; bringing your own food and drinks is not allowed.

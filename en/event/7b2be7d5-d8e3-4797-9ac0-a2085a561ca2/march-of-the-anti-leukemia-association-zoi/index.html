September is Leukemia Awareness Month — a month of information, prevention, hope, and solidarity. Proper information can dispel fears and prejudices, encourage early seeking of medical help, and help us better understand the difficulties faced by patients and their families. The Pancyprian Anti-Leukemia Association "ZOE" is organizing a series of events, calling on society to get informed, participate, and stand practically by the side of people fighting their own battle.

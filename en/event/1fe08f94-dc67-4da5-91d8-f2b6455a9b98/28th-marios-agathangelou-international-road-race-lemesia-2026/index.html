The “Marios Agathangelou” International Road Race returns to Limassol for its 28th edition, continuing a tradition of almost three decades and promoting sportsmanship, fair play and a love of sport. Dedicated to the memory of athlete and educator Marios Agathangelou, the race has become an important event in Cyprus’ running calendar, attracting hundreds of participants from Cyprus and abroad. The event offers races for different ages and fitness levels: 10 km – Men and Women 5 km – Men, Women and Corporate Race 1 km – Kids Race (up to 15 years old) Date: 11 October 2026 Location: Limassol

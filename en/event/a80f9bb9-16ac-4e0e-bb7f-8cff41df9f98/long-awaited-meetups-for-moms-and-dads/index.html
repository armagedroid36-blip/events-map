Long-awaited meetups for moms (and dads) 🧳 LIFE ON SUITCASES: PARENTING IN EMIGRATION Moving to live by the sea sounds great! But along with our suitcases, we bring our family, children, parental responsibilities, and a million questions that we have to find answers to all over again in a new country)) How to choose a kindergarten or school when it's unclear where we'll be living in a year? Which language should we teach our child? How to make acquaintances when even chatting with neighbors is an advanced-level task? On October 30, I invite moms (and dads) to a meetup dedicated to parenting in emigration We'll talk about: 🟢how to make decisions about our children's future when our own looks uncertain 🟢why you can feel lonely even surrounded by people 🟢how the language barrier affects our independence and communication 🟢why everyday tasks take so much energy and how to gradually settle into a new reality We'll share experiences, work through our feelings, and look for what helps us feel more confident far from our familiar home And, of course, meet each other! After all, one of the best things you can find in a new city is your own people💚 Facilitator: Natalya Gorbunova, clinical psychologist, CBT consultant, psychologist for moms, mother of two, gave birth to a daughter in emigration 📏October 30, Friday 📏10:00–12:00 📏Little M kid Lounge 💰 Participation: 150,000 dong plus play area entry if you come with a child Yes, yes, you can come with children!

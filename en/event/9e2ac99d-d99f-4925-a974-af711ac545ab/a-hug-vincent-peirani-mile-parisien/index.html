A Hug – Vincent Peirani, Émile Parisien

Event has ended — September 29, 2026

September 29, 2026, 5:30 PM

Axiothea Mansion, Nicosia, Cyprus

Two leading French musicians unite accordion and saxophone in a fascinating dialogue between jazz, tango and classical music. The title Abrazo (Embrace) is an eloquent image of the deep musical relationship between the leading French musicians Vincent Peirani and Émile Parisien. With more than a thousand joint concerts over the past decade, their on-stage communication is characterized by rare immediacy, creative freedom and an effortless musical dialogue that only two artists with such a long shared journey can develop.

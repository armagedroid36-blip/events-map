COMMANDARIA & TRADITION FESTIVAL – 17-18.10.2026

Event has ended — October 18, 2026

October 17, 2026, 2:30 PM

Central Square of the Community, 68 Commandarias Avenue, Kalo Chorio, Limassol, Cyprus

Free

The event will be dedicated to the traditional wine of Cyprus, Commandaria, for which our Community is famous throughout Cyprus and its derivatives, namely palouze, siousioukko, as well as various other traditional products (pisies, making halloumi, resi, etc.). Attendees, during the event, will have the opportunity to help in the preparation of palouze and to taste for free the traditional products that will be available at the event, as well as to try Commandaria and zivania. Come to the beautiful Koumandarochori for a special weekend, full of Cypriot flavors, tradition and authentic traditional products. We look forward to seeing you there to enjoy them together!

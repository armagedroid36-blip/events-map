🔥 Big guitar jam by the campfire • "Vyshe Gor" & "Scooter Party" On Saturday we're bringing together two communities — "Vyshe Gor" and Scooter Party — and gathering at Cozy Beach for a big guitar jam by the campfire. There will be lots of music, familiar songs, guitars, drums and other instruments. The idea is simple: musicians play, everyone else joins in, and at some point the whole crowd is singing together 😁 No stage and no formal program. We come to the sea, light a fire, get to know each other, hang out and build this evening ourselves. 🎶 The songbook is ready — over a hundred familiar songs for guitar. Plenty to play and plenty to shout along to 😁 🎸 If you play — bring your instrument 🥁 Guitars, drums, tambourines, jaw harps and anything you can jam on are welcome 🍉 Feel free to bring food, fruit and snacks 🫖 Tea or a thermos with hot water 🧺 A mat, blanket or towel to sit on the sand 🙌 And anything you'd like to share with the crew 🗓 October 17, Saturday 🕕 18:00 📍 Cozy Beach: 💚 Participation is free. 📩 If you're coming — message: @siberian_cat_69 #date: 17.10 Post by: 🐱 Kotik Dima

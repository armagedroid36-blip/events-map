Material and Community Exhibition, walks, workshops

December 18, 2026, 10:00 AM

Fyti Village Square, Paphos, Cyprus

The project "Matter and Community" brings contemporary art into creative dialogue with the cultural heritage of the villages of Lasa and Fyti, highlighting stone, earth, and traditional techniques as living elements of local identity. Through an art exhibition, an artist residency program, experiential workshops, presentations, and narrative tours, residents and visitors are invited to discover the stories, memories, and knowledge connected to the natural and built landscape of the area.

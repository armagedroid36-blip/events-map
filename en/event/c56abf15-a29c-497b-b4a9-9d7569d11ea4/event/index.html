"THE BELLY" A theatrical monologue by Marina Vronti The theatrical monologue "THE BELLY" by Marina Vronti is presented at KTIRION 53, directed by Stavros Potamaris with Marilia Charidimou on stage. A few words about the play: In "The Belly," you will be moved by the way laughter opens space for a truth that is difficult to speak. Marina stands before us as she is, with her humor, her anger, and her tenderness, so that we may hear behind her words all that she was taught to hide.

Event has ended — October 3, 2026

Lefkola Square Protaras, Paralimni, Cyprus

The Cyprus Agricultural Payments Organization (CAPO), with the support of the Municipality of Paralimni – Deryneia, is organizing the 10th Festival of Rural Culture, on Friday, October 2 and Saturday, October 3, 2026, from 17:00 to 23:00, at the Municipal Square "Leukolla" in Protaras. The Festival is held under the auspices of H.E. the President of the Republic of Cyprus, Mr. Nikos Christodoulides, and includes a rich program dedicated to the Cypriot countryside, tradition, gastronomy, and culture.

Shrek Rave, the globally touring Halloween party phenomenon, comes to Limassol for a one-night Halloween Special at Monfik. Step into the swamp for a night of Shrek-themed DJ sets featuring soundtrack hits, party anthems and high-energy dance music. The programme also includes live Shrek performers, mass singalongs, themed visuals, giveaways, games and Shrek-inspired drinks. Come dressed in green or as your favourite Shrek character — the best Shrek-inspired costume of the night will win a prize.

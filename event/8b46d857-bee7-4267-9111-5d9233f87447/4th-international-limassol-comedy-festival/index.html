4th International Limassol Comedy Festival

Событие прошло — 4 октября 2026

30 сентября 2026, 16:30

Limassol Old Town, Лимасол, Кипр

Five days of stand-up comedy across Limassol Old Town. The 4th International Limassol Comedy Festival brings 34 shows, more than 45 comedians and 7 formats (open mic, line-up shows, solo shows, showcases, interactive, RAW and festival specials) to six venues: Theatre ENA, Sto Dromo, ARCO, the Old Dog Club, Da Vinci and the Alley Stage. Shows run in English, Greek and Cypriot Greek, with Russian-language and Serbo-Croatian comedy also on the bill. Opening night on Wednesday 30 September is free: competitive open mics in English and Cypriot Greek, the New Festival Voices showcases and the Roast

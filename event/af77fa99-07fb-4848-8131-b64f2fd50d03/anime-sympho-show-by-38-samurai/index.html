Anime Sympho-Show by 38 SAMURAI

Событие снято с афиши — 22 января 2027

22 января 2027, 18:30

Лимасол, Кипр

Step into the world of Japanese animation with Anime Sympho-Show, performed by the 38 SAMURAI orchestra and coming to Cyprus for the first time. The concert features more than 20 soundtracks from iconic anime, including Attack on Titan, Naruto, Dragon Ball, One Piece, Death Note, Demon Slayer, Tokyo Ghoul, Howl’s Moving Castle and many more. An orchestra, rock band, choir and guest soloists come together in a powerful symphonic show combining well-known anime music with spectacular visuals. Date: 22 January 2027 Time: 19:30 Venue: Monte Caputo, Lemesos

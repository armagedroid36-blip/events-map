A programme specially designed for older adults, based on the idea that physical activity has no age limit and can be easily adapted to each person’s needs and abilities. Through a simple, engaging and enjoyable exercise programme, guided by instructors from the Sport for All (AGO) programme, older adults will have the opportunity to stay active, exercise and enjoy time together. The activity is organised as part of the European Week of Sport 2026. When: 25 September 2026

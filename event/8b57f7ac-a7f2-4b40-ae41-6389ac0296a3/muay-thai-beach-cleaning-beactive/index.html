Akti Olympion Beach, Лимасол, Кипр

Together for a cleaner and healthier Limassol! The Limassol Municipality Sports Office, in cooperation with Fight & Fitness Co., invites everyone to a special activity combining sport with community action. The event begins with a voluntary clean-up of Akti Olympion Beach, helping to keep Limassol’s seafront clean and beautiful for residents and visitors. It will continue with an open Muay Thai training session at the Molos Open-Air Amphitheatre, where children and adults will have the opportunity to discover the sport, exercise and experience the energy and spirit of martial arts.

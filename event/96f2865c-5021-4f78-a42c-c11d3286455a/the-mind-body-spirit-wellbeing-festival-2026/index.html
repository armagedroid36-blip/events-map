St Raphael Resort, Лимасол, Кипр

The Mind, Body & Spirit Wellbeing Festival returns to Limassol for a special edition celebrating 25 years of MBS Cyprus and its community dedicated to holistic living and wellbeing. The two-day festival brings together more than 70 exhibitors and over 80 lectures and workshops focused on physical, emotional and mental wellbeing. Visitors can explore yoga and movement classes, biohealth and biohacking, holistic practices, mini therapies, natural products and handmade wellness creations, with activities also available for children.

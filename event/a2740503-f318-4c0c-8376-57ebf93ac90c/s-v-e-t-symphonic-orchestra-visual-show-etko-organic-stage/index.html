S.V.E.T: Symphonic Orchestra Visual Show @ ETKO Organic Stage

Событие снято с афиши — 10 октября 2026

10 октября 2026, 15:00

The Warehouse by IT, Лимасол, Кипр

S.V.E.T returns to Limassol with a new immersive audiovisual show combining electronic music, a live symphony orchestra, light design and multimedia art at ETKO Organic Stage. At the heart of the night, KOSTYN x CHERRY x R.A.N bring together electronic sound and the power of a live orchestra in a performance created especially for this edition. The programme also features a symphonic performance by The Advocate and DJ sets from Key M and Che Boy. The night continues with an official afterparty at KESH, Limassol Agora, from 01:00. Date: 10 October 2026 Time: From 18:00 Venue: ETKO Organic Stag

Cyprus premiere of 'All of It' by British playwright Alistair McDowall, translated, dramaturged and directed by Maria Ioli Karolidou, with Antonia Charalambous on stage. A stream-of-consciousness monologue: a torrent of thoughts and memories tracing one woman's journey from birth to the final parting, through childhood, adolescence, adulthood, marriage, motherhood, divorce, everyday routine, loss and death, told with humour, honesty, directness and tenderness.

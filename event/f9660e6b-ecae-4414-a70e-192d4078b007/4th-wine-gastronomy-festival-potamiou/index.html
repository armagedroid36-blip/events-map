4th Wine & Gastronomy Festival @ Potamiou

Событие прошло — 4 октября 2026

4 октября 2026, 11:30

Лимасол, Кипр

Potamiou celebrates its local traditions, flavours and community spirit with the 4th Wine & Gastronomy Festival. Visitors can discover and taste traditional Cypriot products including palouzes, soutzoukos, trachanas, tremithopita, local grapes, wine and Zivania. Hands-on workshops will also offer the opportunity to learn how some of these traditional delicacies are prepared. The programme combines gastronomy with activities exploring the village and its heritage, including a cycling wine route, local stories and traditional houses, as well as live traditional music and dancing.

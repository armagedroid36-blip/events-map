Лимасол, Кипр

The Cyprus Symphony Orchestra presents Starlight 1: From Saint-Saëns to Carmen!, featuring internationally acclaimed pianist and composer George-Emmanuel Lazaridis in his first collaboration with the orchestra, under the baton of conductor Zoe Zeniodi. The evening opens with the Cyprus premiere of Nestor Taylor’s The Path of the Archangel, an evocative elegy for strings. The programme continues with Saint-Saëns’ brilliant Piano Concerto No. 2 in G minor and concludes with the passion and drama of Bizet’s much-loved Carmen Suites Nos. 1 and 2.

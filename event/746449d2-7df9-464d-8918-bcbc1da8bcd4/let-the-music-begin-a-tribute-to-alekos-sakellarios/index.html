“Let The music Begin” – A Tribute to Alekos Sakellarios

Событие прошло — 11 октября 2026

11 октября 2026, 19:00

Korfi Communal Park, Лимасол, Кипр

Marking 35 years since the passing of Alekos Sakellarios, this concert celebrates the beloved songs of classic Greek cinema, with a special tribute to the great writer, lyricist and director whose name became inextricably linked with some of the most popular films of his era. The programme features beloved songs by Manos Hadjidakis, Giorgos Zampetas, Stavros Xarchakos, Mikis Theodorakis and Mimis Plessas, accompanied by photographic projection from the films associated with the songs.

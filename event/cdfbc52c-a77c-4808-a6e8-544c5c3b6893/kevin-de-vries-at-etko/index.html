Kevin de Vries heads to Hangar ETKO in Limassol on Saturday, 21 November. A core Afterlife artist since 2018, Kevin de Vries has developed a distinctive sound within melodic techno, combining hypnotic loops, driving beats, rhythmic arrangements and emotional intensity. His music has taken him to venues and festivals including Sphere Las Vegas, Space Miami, UNVRS Ibiza, Coachella, EDC Las Vegas, Tomorrowland, Awakenings and Time Warp, with tracks such as “Dance With Me” and “Metro” becoming key examples of his production style. Joining him at Hangar are Key M and Damen, completing the lineup

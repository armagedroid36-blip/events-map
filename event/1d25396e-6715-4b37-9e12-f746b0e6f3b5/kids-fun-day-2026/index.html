Kids Fun Day 2026

Событие прошло — 4 октября 2026

4 октября 2026, 08:00

Lanitio Theatre, Лимасол, Кипр

A full day of games, creativity and activities for children is coming to Lanitio Park, supporting an important cause: the Cyprus ADHD Association. The Kids Fun Day programme includes face painting, glitter tattoos, slime and messy play, experiments, painting, clay creations, crafts, survivor games, a weaving workshop, dance therapy and many more creative activities. Children will also get to meet popular mascots, while a Food & Coffee Station will be available for both children and adults. - Check out all the events in Limassol here.
